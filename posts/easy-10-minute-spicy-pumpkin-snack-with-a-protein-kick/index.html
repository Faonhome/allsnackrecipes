Easy 10-Minute Spicy Pumpkin Snack with a Protein Kick

Chef
Published on September 22, 2026
10 min Prep Time
6 Servings
Easy Difficulty
4.8 (53 ratings)✓ Thanks for rating!

This spicy pumpkin snack blends Indian flavors with high protein and low calories. Using protein-packed chickpeas and warming spices, it's perfect for an afternoon boost without guilt. I love eating this after workouts or when I need a filling, flavorful treat!

Introduction

I’ve always struggled with feeling hungry on low-calorie diets, especially when cutting and trying to keep protein high. Small portions just don’t cut it for me—I want something that fills me up without blowing my calorie budget. That’s why I created this spicy pumpkin snack inspired by Indian flavors. It’s packed with chickpeas and Greek yogurt for a solid protein punch, but the pumpkin adds bulk and fiber so you get a giant bowl that feels like a treat, not a diet chore. What makes this version special is how I use warming spices like garam masala and cumin to give the pumpkin a savory, spicy twist that keeps me coming back. Plus, I add a tiny bit of xanthan gum to thicken the mix without adding calories or fat, so it holds together nicely instead of being watery. I’ve tried other pumpkin snacks before, but they were either too thin or loaded with sugar and fat. This one is just right—creamy, spicy, and filling. I usually whip this up in about 10 minutes and keep it in the fridge for a few days. It’s my go-to afternoon slump buster or post-workout snack. My friends always ask how I eat so much pumpkin and chickpeas and still lose weight! It’s all about volume and protein, my secret weapons for satiety. If you hate tiny portions and want a snack that’s actually satisfying, give this a try—you’ll love how much food you get for under 200 calories.

What You'll Need

Approximately 1 cup per serving, 6 snack servings, about 6 cups total volume

  • 1 cup cooked pumpkin puree, fresh or canned
  • 1 cup cooked chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 1/2 cup zero-fat Greek yogurt
  • 2 tbsp finely chopped cilantro
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1/2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper (adjust to taste)
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • Salt to taste
  • 1/4 tsp xanthan gum (optional, for thickening)
  • 1 tsp water (to mix with xanthan gum if using)

How To Do It

Step 1
In a large mixing bowl, combine the cooked pumpkin puree and chickpeas. Mash the chickpeas slightly with a fork or potato masher to create some texture but keep some whole for bite. This step is key because it adds bulk and texture, making the snack feel more filling and less like a puree. Avoid over-mashing or it will become too smooth and lose the satisfying mouthfeel.
⏱ Time: 3 minutes
✓ Doneness: Mixture is thick but still chunky with visible chickpea pieces
Step 2
Add the zero-fat Greek yogurt, lemon juice, garam masala, cumin, cayenne pepper, salt, and chopped cilantro. Stir gently to combine all ingredients evenly. The yogurt adds creaminess and protein without fat, while the spices infuse the pumpkin with authentic Indian flavor. Mix just until combined to prevent the yogurt from breaking down and becoming watery.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Smooth, creamy mixture with evenly distributed spices and herbs
Step 3
If using xanthan gum, sprinkle it over 1 tsp of water and whisk until dissolved to create a slurry. Slowly fold this into the pumpkin mixture. This thickens the snack without adding calories or fat, helping it hold its shape and avoid watery separation. Be careful to add it slowly and whisk well to prevent clumps.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Mixture thickens slightly and holds shape when scooped
Step 4
Taste the mixture and adjust salt or cayenne pepper as desired. The balance of spice and salt is important to bring out the pumpkin’s natural sweetness and complement the protein-rich chickpeas. Don’t skip this step because seasoning can make or break the final flavor.
⏱ Time: 1 minute
✓ Doneness: Flavor is balanced with a gentle heat and savory notes
Step 5
Transfer the mixture to serving bowls or meal prep containers. This snack can be enjoyed immediately or chilled to let flavors meld. Chilling also firms the texture slightly, making it easier to scoop and eat. If you chill, cover tightly to prevent drying out.
⏱ Time: 1 minute
✓ Doneness: Snack is portioned and ready to eat or store
Step 6
For extra volume, you can add chopped cucumber or celery on top when serving. These low-calorie veggies add crunch and hydration without adding fat or calories, boosting the volume eating effect. Plus, they complement the spicy pumpkin flavor nicely.
⏱ Time: Optional, 1 minute
✓ Doneness: Crunchy vegetable pieces visible on top
Step 7
If you want a smoother texture, blend the mixture briefly in a food processor before serving. This is great if you prefer a creamy dip or spread. However, keep in mind this reduces the volume slightly, so if you want max fullness, stick to the chunky version.
⏱ Time: Optional, 1-2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Mixture is creamy and smooth without lumps
Step 8
Store any leftovers in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days. This snack holds up well chilled and the flavors deepen over time. Avoid freezing as the texture can become watery upon thawing due to the yogurt and pumpkin content.
⏱ Time: N/A
✓ Doneness: Stored snack remains thick and flavorful
Step 9
When ready to eat, stir the snack gently if it has separated slightly. Enjoy cold or at room temperature. You can add a squeeze of fresh lemon juice to brighten the flavor just before serving for an extra zing.
⏱ Time: N/A
✓ Doneness: Snack tastes fresh and vibrant with balanced texture

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Skipping xanthan gum and ending with watery texture.

Why it happens People often assume pumpkin and yogurt alone will thicken the snack, but low-fat dairy mixtures tend to separate and become runny without a binder.

Fix Add a small amount of xanthan gum mixed with water to the recipe. This binds moisture and creates a creamy, thick texture without extra calories or fat.

Using full-fat yogurt instead of zero-fat.

Why it happens Some think full-fat yogurt will taste better or be healthier, not realizing it adds unnecessary calories and fat to a low-calorie snack.

Fix Stick to plain zero-fat Greek yogurt to keep calories low and protein high. This maintains the snack’s light texture and macro balance.

Over-mashing chickpeas into a smooth paste.

Why it happens Trying to create a uniform texture can backfire by removing satisfying chunks that add volume and chew.

Fix Mash chickpeas just enough to break them up but keep pieces intact for texture and volume.

Pro Tips

Add xanthan gum slowly and whisk well to avoid clumps.
Xanthan gum thickens the mixture by binding water molecules, creating a creamy texture without fat. If added too quickly or not whisked properly, it forms lumps that ruin the texture. Proper dispersion ensures smoothness and prevents separation, which is crucial in low-fat, high-water snacks.
Use cold Greek yogurt straight from the fridge.
Cold yogurt helps maintain the snack’s thick texture and prevents early separation. Warm yogurt can cause protein to break down faster, leading to watery mixtures. Keeping ingredients cold until mixing preserves creaminess and improves mouthfeel in low-calorie recipes.
Mash chickpeas lightly, not into a paste.
Leaving some chickpeas whole adds texture and bite, making the snack more satisfying. Over-mashing turns the base into a puree that feels less filling and less like a snack. This balance of smooth and chunky keeps the mouth interested and enhances satiety.

Variations

Spicy Pumpkin with Egg Whites

Swap chickpeas for whipped egg whites to boost protein and create a fluffier texture. Egg whites add volume and lightness, making the snack feel like a savory mousse. This reduces carbs and keeps calories low, perfect for those avoiding legumes.

Note: Whip egg whites to soft peaks before folding into pumpkin puree and spices. Be gentle to maintain airiness and prevent deflating.

Pumpkin and Edamame Protein Bowl

Use cooked shelled edamame instead of chickpeas for a different plant protein source with a slightly nutty flavor. Edamame adds fiber and volume, complementing the pumpkin’s creaminess while keeping it low-calorie.

Note: Lightly mash edamame to keep texture but remove tough skins. Adjust seasoning to balance the different flavor profile.

Sweet Spiced Pumpkin Snack

Add a pinch of cinnamon and a few drops of stevia to make a sweet version of this snack. It’s still high protein and low calorie but with a dessert vibe, great for evening cravings without guilt.

Note: Reduce cayenne and garam masala to avoid overpowering sweetness. Mix stevia carefully to avoid aftertaste.

Storage & Tips

Room Temperature

This snack should not be left at room temperature for more than 2 hours due to the yogurt content. Keep covered to prevent drying out and contamination. Water separation may occur if left out too long, making the texture less appealing.

Refrigerator

Store in an airtight container for up to 3 days. The snack firms up slightly when chilled, holding its volume well. Flavors deepen and it tastes best cold or at room temperature after a short rest.

Freezer

Freezing is not recommended because the high water content in pumpkin and yogurt causes ice crystals that break the texture upon thawing, resulting in a watery, less creamy snack.

Reheating

This snack is best eaten cold or at room temperature. Reheating is not advised as it can cause separation and loss of texture. If needed, let it come to room temp naturally before eating.

Make Ahead

Prepare the pumpkin and chickpea mixture up to 2 days ahead and store in the fridge. Add fresh herbs and any crunchy veggies just before serving to maintain texture. Xanthan gum helps retain thickness during storage.

FAQ

Can I substitute protein powder for chickpeas in this recipe?
Yes, you can replace chickpeas with unflavored whey isolate protein powder to increase protein further and reduce carbs. However, protein powder alone won’t provide the same texture or volume, so you may need to add xanthan gum or egg whites to maintain thickness and mouthfeel.
How do I prevent the snack from becoming watery?
Using xanthan gum or a similar hydrocolloid is key to binding water and preventing separation. Also, use zero-fat Greek yogurt, which is thicker and less watery than other yogurts. Avoid over-mixing or letting the snack sit too long at room temperature to maintain texture.
Is this snack suitable for meal prepping?
Absolutely! It stores well in the fridge for up to 3 days. Just keep it in airtight containers and add any fresh veggies or herbs before eating to keep it fresh and crunchy.
Can I eat this snack while cutting or on a low-calorie diet?
Yes, this snack is designed to be high protein and low calorie, making it perfect for cutting phases. The volume eating aspect helps you feel full without excess calories, supporting fat loss while preserving muscle.
What if I don’t have xanthan gum?
You can try a small amount of chia seeds soaked in water as a substitute, but this may change the texture and flavor slightly. Alternatively, whipping egg whites into the mixture can add volume and help thicken it without xanthan gum.
How spicy is this snack? Can I adjust it?
The cayenne pepper adds a moderate heat, but you can easily adjust it to your liking. For milder flavor, reduce or omit cayenne, or add more warming spices like cumin and garam masala for flavor without heat.
Will this snack keep me full for long?
Yes! The combination of protein, fiber from pumpkin and chickpeas, and volume from water-rich ingredients triggers fullness hormones and stretches your stomach, keeping hunger at bay for hours.
Can I make this snack vegan?
To keep it vegan, swap Greek yogurt for unsweetened soy or coconut yogurt, and use chickpeas or edamame for protein. Note that texture and protein content may vary slightly, so adjust xanthan gum as needed.
