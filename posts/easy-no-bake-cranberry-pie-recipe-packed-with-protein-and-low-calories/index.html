Easy No Bake Cranberry Pie Recipe Packed with Protein and Low Calories

Chef
Published on November 23, 2026
10 min Prep Time
6 Servings
Easy Difficulty
4.5 (45 ratings)✓ Thanks for rating!

This no bake cranberry pie recipe is a perfect guilt-free treat loaded with protein and low in calories. Using zero-fat Greek yogurt and egg whites, it’s super fluffy and filling. I love having it as a post-workout snack or whenever I need a sweet, satisfying bite without the guilt.

Introduction

I've always struggled with feeling hungry on low-calorie diets, especially during my afternoon slump when I want something sweet but don't want to blow my macros. That’s why I created this no bake cranberry pie recipe — it’s a giant bowl of fluffy, creamy goodness that’s packed with protein but low in calories. Unlike store-bought protein bars or snacks that leave me wanting more, this pie fills me up without the guilt. The trick is using zero-fat Greek yogurt combined with whipped egg whites to create a light and airy texture that feels indulgent but isn’t. I learned the hard way that skipping the xanthan gum made my first batch too watery and sad, but now it holds together beautifully without any added fat. Plus, the fresh cranberries add a tart pop that keeps each bite interesting. I usually whip this up on Sundays for meal prep and keep it chilled for a quick snack after workouts or when I’m craving something sweet but filling. Honestly, my friends keep asking how I eat so much and still stay on track — this pie is my secret weapon. It’s easy, fast, and makes snacking enjoyable again.

What You'll Need

Approximately 1 generous slice per serving, 6 servings total, about 1 cup volume each

  • 1 cup zero-fat Greek yogurt, chilled
  • 4 large egg whites, pasteurized
  • 1/4 cup unflavored whey protein isolate powder
  • 1/2 cup fresh or frozen cranberries, chopped
  • 2 tbsp sugar-free cranberry syrup or monk fruit sweetener
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/2 tsp xanthan gum (optional, for thickness)
  • 1 tbsp water (to dissolve xanthan gum if using)

How To Do It

Step 1
Start by chilling your mixing bowl and beaters in the fridge for about 10 minutes; this helps the egg whites whip up better. Pour the pasteurized egg whites into the chilled bowl and begin whisking at medium speed. Whipping egg whites incorporates air, which creates volume and fluffiness without adding calories or fat. The goal is to reach stiff peaks where the whites hold their shape firmly. Avoid rushing this step because under-whipped whites won’t provide the airy texture needed, and over-whipping can cause dryness. Watch for a glossy, firm texture that stands tall when you lift the whisk.
⏱ Time: 10
✓ Doneness: Whites form stiff, glossy peaks that hold shape without collapsing.
Step 2
In a separate bowl, combine the zero-fat Greek yogurt, protein powder, vanilla extract, and sugar-free cranberry syrup or monk fruit sweetener. If using xanthan gum, dissolve it in a tablespoon of water first to prevent clumps, then whisk it into the yogurt mixture. The protein powder hydrates best in cold, acidic dairy like yogurt, which helps prevent chalkiness. Xanthan gum acts as a natural thickener, improving texture and preventing watery separation common in low-fat snacks. Mix until smooth and creamy, ensuring no lumps remain.
⏱ Time: 5
✓ Doneness: Mixture is smooth, thick, and slightly glossy with no lumps.
Step 3
Gently fold the whipped egg whites into the yogurt-protein mixture using a spatula. Use a light hand and fold in batches to retain as much air as possible. This step is crucial because folding preserves the fluffiness that makes the pie feel voluminous and satisfying. Avoid stirring vigorously, which can deflate the mixture and lead to a denser, less appealing texture. The final batter should look airy and light with no streaks of egg white or yogurt.
⏱ Time: 3
✓ Doneness: Mixture is uniformly light and airy, no visible streaks.
Step 4
Fold in the chopped cranberries evenly throughout the mixture. The cranberries add bursts of tart flavor and a bit of texture contrast, enhancing the overall eating experience. Distributing them well ensures every bite has some fruit without weighing down the airy base. Be gentle to avoid crushing the berries and releasing too much juice, which could thin the mixture.
⏱ Time: 2
✓ Doneness: Cranberries are evenly dispersed without breaking down.
Step 5
Transfer the mixture into a serving dish or individual cups. Smooth the top with a spatula for an even surface. Because the mixture is light and airy, it will look like a large portion despite being low in calories. This visual volume helps satisfy hunger and cravings psychologically as well as physically.
⏱ Time: 2
✓ Doneness: Mixture evenly spread with a smooth surface.
Step 6
Refrigerate the pie for at least 2 hours to allow it to set and firm up. Chilling helps the xanthan gum activate fully and the protein powder to hydrate completely, improving texture and mouthfeel. If rushed, the pie might be too loose or watery. The cold temperature also enhances the refreshing tartness of cranberries and the creamy feel of the yogurt base.
⏱ Time: 120
✓ Doneness: Pie is firm to the touch but still light and fluffy.
Step 7
Before serving, stir gently if any watery separation has occurred at the edges — this is normal with zero-fat dairy but can be reincorporated. Portion into slices or scoops. The pie holds its shape well and provides a filling, protein-rich snack that’s perfect for any time of day.
⏱ Time: 1
✓ Doneness: No watery pools; pie holds shape when scooped.
Step 8
Optional: garnish with a few whole cranberries or a sprinkle of cinnamon for extra flavor and color. This adds visual appeal and a touch of spice without adding calories or fat.
⏱ Time: 1
✓ Doneness: Garnish looks fresh and evenly applied.
Step 9
Serve chilled and enjoy immediately for best texture and flavor. This pie is great as a post-workout snack, afternoon treat, or anytime you want a volume-filled protein boost. Store leftovers in an airtight container in the fridge up to 3 days.
⏱ Time: 0
✓ Doneness: Snack is cold, creamy, and retains fluffiness.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Skipping the xanthan gum leading to watery texture.

Why it happens People often underestimate the role of stabilizers in fat-free dairy snacks, assuming yogurt alone will hold the mixture together.

Fix Always include a small amount of xanthan gum dissolved in water to thicken and stabilize the pie, preventing watery separation and improving mouthfeel.

Over-whipping egg whites causing dryness.

Why it happens Trying to get stiffer peaks but whipping too long can break down the foam structure.

Fix Stop whipping as soon as stiff, glossy peaks form; test by lifting the whisk and checking the whites hold shape without collapsing.

Using full-fat yogurt increasing calories unexpectedly.

Why it happens Mistaking 2% or full-fat yogurt for zero-fat leads to higher calorie content and changes in texture.

Fix Check labels carefully and always use zero-fat Greek yogurt to keep the snack low calorie and maintain the intended creamy texture.

Pro Tips

Chill your mixing bowl and beaters before whipping egg whites.
Cold equipment helps egg whites whip faster and reach stiffer peaks, creating more volume and a fluffier texture. Warm bowls can slow the process and lead to less stable foam, which affects the final snack’s lightness.
Dissolve xanthan gum in water before adding to yogurt mixture.
Xanthan gum can clump if added directly to thick mixtures. Pre-dissolving ensures even distribution, preventing lumps and improving texture stability in this low-fat, high-protein snack.
Fold egg whites gently into yogurt mixture to retain airiness.
Vigorous stirring deflates whipped egg whites, reducing volume and making the snack denser. Folding preserves the trapped air, resulting in a light, fluffy pie that feels like a larger portion.

Variations

Blueberry No Bake Protein Pie

Swap cranberries for fresh or frozen blueberries for a sweeter, milder flavor. Blueberries add antioxidants and fiber while keeping the snack low calorie. Expect a slightly softer texture as blueberries release more juice. Macros remain similar with minimal calorie change.

Note: Because blueberries release more liquid, consider adding a bit more xanthan gum to maintain firmness.

Chocolate Cranberry Protein Pie

Add 1 tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder to the yogurt mixture for a chocolate twist. This adds antioxidants and a rich taste without many calories. Use sugar-free sweetener to balance bitterness. The texture stays light and fluffy.

Note: Mix cocoa powder thoroughly with protein powder before combining to avoid clumps.

Vegan No Bake Cranberry Pie

Replace egg whites with aquafaba whipped to stiff peaks and use plant-based protein powder. The texture is slightly less firm but still airy. Use coconut or almond-based yogurt alternative to keep calories low.

Note: Aquafaba whips differently than egg whites; whip longer and fold gently to maintain volume.

Storage & Tips

Room Temperature

This no bake cranberry pie should not be left at room temperature for more than 1 hour due to the egg whites and dairy content. Keep it covered to prevent contamination and drying out.

Refrigerator

Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. The pie firms up when chilled and holds its volume well. It tastes best cold, and the texture remains creamy and light.

Freezer

Freeze in a sealed container for up to 1 month. Thaw overnight in the fridge. Note that freezing may cause slight texture changes, making the pie less fluffy and a bit icy, so it’s best enjoyed fresh or refrigerated.

Reheating

This snack is best eaten cold and should not be reheated as heat can cause separation and loss of fluffiness. If needed, let it come to room temperature for 10 minutes before serving.

Make Ahead

Prepare the yogurt-protein mixture and whip egg whites separately up to 1 day ahead. Store both components chilled and combine just before serving to maintain maximum volume and freshness.

FAQ

Can I use regular egg whites instead of pasteurized ones?
You can use fresh egg whites if you’re confident in their safety and freshness, but pasteurized egg whites are recommended to reduce the risk of foodborne illness since this recipe isn’t cooked. Always handle raw eggs carefully and store the snack properly.
What if I don’t have xanthan gum? Can I skip it?
Xanthan gum helps stabilize and thicken the pie, preventing watery separation. Without it, the texture may be runnier and less creamy. If you don’t have it, try adding a teaspoon of chia seeds soaked in water as a natural thickener, but expect a slightly different texture.
How can I avoid the chalky taste from protein powder?
Use a high-quality whey isolate protein powder and mix it thoroughly with the acidic yogurt base. The acidity helps break down the protein powder, reducing chalkiness. Also, ensure the powder is fresh and stored properly to maintain flavor.
Is this recipe suitable for cutting or weight loss?
Absolutely! It’s low in calories but high in protein and volume, which helps keep you full and supports muscle maintenance during weight loss. The large portion size also helps satisfy cravings without excess calories.
Can I make this pie ahead for meal prep?
Yes! You can prepare it up to 1 day in advance. Keep the whipped egg whites and yogurt mixture separate until ready to serve for best volume. Once combined, store in the fridge and consume within 3 days.
What’s the best time to eat this snack?
It’s perfect post-workout to refuel with protein or as an afternoon snack to curb sweet cravings while staying on track. The protein and volume help keep hunger at bay between meals.
Can I substitute the cranberries with other fruits?
Yes, fresh or frozen berries like blueberries or raspberries work well. Just be mindful that some fruits release more juice, which may affect texture. Adjust xanthan gum slightly if needed.
How do I store leftovers to maintain texture?
Keep leftovers in an airtight container in the fridge. Stir gently before serving if any liquid separates. Avoid freezing if possible, as it can change the texture and reduce fluffiness.
