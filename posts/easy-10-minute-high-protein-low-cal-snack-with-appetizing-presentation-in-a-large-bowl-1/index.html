Easy 10-Minute High-Protein Low-Cal Snack with Appetizing Presentation in a Large Bowl

Chef
Published on June 7, 2026
10 min Prep Time
6 Servings
Easy Difficulty

This snack is a creamy, protein-packed bowl that feels like a treat but keeps calories low. Using zero-fat Greek yogurt and a touch of egg whites for fluff, it fills you up without guilt. Perfect for an afternoon pick-me-up or post-workout refuel!

Introduction

I’ve always struggled with feeling hungry when I’m cutting calories, especially on days when I’m only eating around 1200. Tiny portions just don’t cut it for me, and I hate feeling like I’m starving. That’s why I came up with this giant protein snack bowl that feels like a full meal but clocks in under 200 calories. It’s perfect for those afternoon slumps or right after a workout when you want something filling but light. The trick? Using zero-fat Greek yogurt bulked up with some liquid egg whites for fluffiness and a little xanthan gum to keep it creamy without adding any fat. I used to make versions that ended up watery or chalky, but finding the right brand of yogurt and the right ratio of protein powder made all the difference. Plus, throwing in fresh berries adds natural sweetness and volume so you feel like you’re eating a feast, not a snack. You’ll love how it fills you up and tastes way better than any protein bar I’ve tried.

What You'll Need

Approximately 1 large bowl per serving, 6 snack servings total, about 2 cups volume per serving

  • 2 cups zero-fat Greek yogurt, chilled
  • 1/2 cup liquid egg whites, pasteurized
  • 1 scoop unflavored whey protein isolate
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 tsp powdered erythritol or monk fruit sweetener
  • 1/4 tsp xanthan gum
  • 1/2 cup mixed fresh berries (blueberries, raspberries)
  • Optional: 1 tbsp finely chopped cucumber for crunch

How To Do It

Step 1
Start by chilling your zero-fat Greek yogurt and liquid egg whites in the fridge for at least 30 minutes before mixing. Cold ingredients help the xanthan gum hydrate properly and prevent the mixture from becoming watery. In a large bowl, whisk together the yogurt and egg whites gently to combine without deflating the egg whites too much. This step is crucial because it creates a light, fluffy base that will hold volume and texture without needing fat. Avoid over-whisking here, or the mixture can become too dense and lose its creamy mouthfeel.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: The mixture should look smooth and slightly airy, with no separation or watery liquid pooling at the bottom.
Step 2
Add the unflavored whey protein isolate, vanilla extract, and sweetener to the yogurt mixture. Slowly sprinkle xanthan gum over the surface while whisking continuously to avoid clumps. This thickening agent mimics creaminess without fat and helps the protein powder blend smoothly. If you add xanthan gum too quickly or in large amounts, it can gel up and create an unpleasant texture. Whisk until fully incorporated and the mixture thickens slightly, which takes about 2-3 minutes. The protein powder hydrates best in cold yogurt, so be patient and don’t rush this step.
⏱ Time: 3 minutes
✓ Doneness: The mixture will be noticeably thicker, creamy, and hold its shape when scooped, without any graininess or clumps.
Step 3
Gently fold in the fresh berries and optional chopped cucumber for added volume and a refreshing crunch. Be careful not to overmix here to keep the berries intact and prevent the mixture from turning watery. The berries add natural sweetness and fiber, which help with satiety and make the snack feel indulgent without extra calories. The cucumber contributes a subtle crunch and freshness that balances the creamy texture.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Berries are evenly distributed without being crushed; the mixture remains thick and creamy.
Step 4
Divide the mixture evenly into six large bowls or meal prep containers. Because of the volume created by the egg whites and xanthan gum, each serving looks generous and satisfying, perfect for volume eating. Portioning now helps you control calories and makes grabbing a snack easy throughout the week. The bowls should be filled nearly to the top, giving the impression of a full meal rather than a tiny snack.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Each bowl is filled about 2 cups with a fluffy, creamy texture visible.
Step 5
Cover the bowls with airtight lids or plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least 1 hour before eating. Chilling firms the texture and melds the flavors, while also preventing watery separation common in low-fat dairy mixtures. If you eat it right away, the texture might be too loose and less satisfying. The cold temperature enhances the creamy mouthfeel and keeps the protein powder from tasting chalky.
⏱ Time: 60 minutes
✓ Doneness: The snack is firm to the touch but still creamy and fluffy when scooped.
Step 6
Before serving, give the bowl a gentle stir to reincorporate any slight separation that may have occurred during chilling. This refreshes the texture and evenly distributes the berries and cucumber. If the mixture feels too thick, add a splash of unsweetened almond milk and fold gently to loosen it without losing fluffiness.
⏱ Time: 1 minute
✓ Doneness: The snack is creamy, smooth, and evenly textured with no watery layers.
Step 7
Taste and adjust sweetness if needed by adding a tiny pinch of sweetener or a few drops of vanilla extract. Because this snack is low-calorie, it’s easy to tweak the flavor to your liking without adding significant calories. Avoid adding too much sweetener to prevent aftertaste, especially with erythritol or monk fruit.
⏱ Time: 1 minute
✓ Doneness: Balanced sweetness with no bitter or chemical aftertaste.
Step 8
Enjoy your snack cold straight from the bowl or container. The creamy, fluffy texture combined with the fresh berries makes it feel indulgent yet healthy. This snack is perfect for keeping hunger at bay between meals or after exercise without breaking your calorie bank.
⏱ Time: Immediate
✓ Doneness: Satisfying fullness and creamy mouthfeel with fresh berry bursts.
Step 9
For meal prep, store leftovers in the fridge for up to 3 days. Re-stir before eating to maintain texture. Avoid freezing as the high water content in yogurt and egg whites can cause icy texture and separation upon thawing.
⏱ Time: Storage advice
✓ Doneness: Snack stays fresh and creamy after refrigeration, with no off smells or texture changes.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Skipping xanthan gum and ending up with a watery, separated snack.

Why it happens People often think yogurt and protein powder alone will thicken the mixture, but without a binder, water separates easily in low-fat snacks.

Fix Add a small amount (about 1/4 tsp) of xanthan gum slowly while whisking to stabilize the mixture and create a creamy, cohesive texture.

Using full-fat yogurt and increasing calories unknowingly.

Why it happens It’s easy to grab regular Greek yogurt without checking fat content, which bumps calories and fat, defeating the low-calorie goal.

Fix Always choose zero-fat Greek yogurt and read labels carefully to keep calories low and protein high.

Over-whisking egg whites causing dense, rubbery texture.

Why it happens Whipping egg whites too much in a low-fat mixture can collapse the air bubbles and make the snack tough.

Fix Gently fold liquid egg whites into yogurt without vigorous whisking to maintain fluffiness and volume.

Adding too much sweetener leading to chemical aftertaste.

Why it happens It’s tempting to add a lot of sugar-free sweetener to mask yogurt tang, but this causes an unpleasant taste.

Fix Add sweetener gradually, tasting as you go, to find the perfect balance without overdoing it.

Not chilling the mixture before eating, resulting in loose texture.

Why it happens Eating immediately after mixing means the xanthan gum and protein powder haven’t set, giving a watery or grainy mouthfeel.

Fix Refrigerate the snack for at least 1 hour to firm texture and improve flavor.

Pro Tips

Always chill your ingredients before mixing to help xanthan gum hydrate properly.
Cold ingredients prevent watery separation by allowing the xanthan gum to absorb moisture gradually and thicken the mixture evenly. If ingredients are warm, the gum can clump or fail to thicken, resulting in a thin, unappealing texture.
Add xanthan gum slowly while whisking to avoid clumps.
Xanthan gum hydrates quickly and can form lumps if added too fast or in large amounts. Slow addition while whisking ensures an even distribution and smooth texture, mimicking fat’s creaminess without calories.
Use pasteurized liquid egg whites for safety and best texture.
Raw egg whites can carry salmonella risk, so pasteurized versions are key for no-cook recipes. Liquid whites incorporate easily and whip gently into yogurt to add volume without affecting flavor.
Fold in berries gently to keep them whole and prevent watery mixture.
Crushed berries release juice that can thin the snack and reduce volume. Gently folding preserves their integrity, adds natural sweetness, and maintains the snack’s thick, creamy texture.
Don’t skip chilling the snack before eating.
Chilling firms the texture and melds flavors. Eating immediately can result in a loose, less satisfying mouthfeel. Cold temperature also reduces protein powder chalkiness.
Use unflavored whey isolate to avoid added sugars and flavors.
Flavored powders often contain sweeteners and fillers that increase calories and can clash with yogurt’s tang. Unflavored isolate keeps the snack clean and lets you control sweetness.
Measure sweeteners carefully to avoid aftertaste.
Low-calorie sweeteners can have strong aftertastes if overused. Start with small amounts and adjust to taste to keep the flavor natural and pleasant.

Variations

Berry Citrus Protein Bowl

Add a tablespoon of fresh lemon or orange zest and swap berries for citrus segments. This adds brightness and a refreshing twist while keeping calories low. Expect a slightly tangier flavor and a bit more volume from the citrus water content. Protein and calories remain similar.

Note: Citrus juice can thin the mixture slightly, so increase xanthan gum by 1/8 tsp to maintain creaminess.

Chocolate Mocha Protein Bowl

Mix in 1 tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder and a shot of cooled espresso or strong coffee. Sweeten with a pinch of stevia. This variation gives a rich, indulgent flavor with minimal calories added. The texture remains creamy and fluffy with the same volume.

Note: Cocoa powder can dry out the mixture; add a splash of unsweetened almond milk to maintain smoothness.

Tropical Protein Bowl

Fold in diced pineapple and mango with a sprinkle of shredded coconut (1 tsp). This gives a sweet, tropical vibe with extra fiber and volume. Calories increase slightly but remain under 200 per serving. The texture is juicy and refreshing.

Note: Fresh pineapple enzymes can break down proteins if left too long; consume within 24 hours for best texture.

Vanilla Cinnamon Protein Bowl

Add 1/2 tsp cinnamon and 1/2 tsp vanilla extract for a warm, cozy flavor. Swap berries for chopped apple or pear for crunch. This version is great for fall or winter snacking with similar macros and volume.

Note: Cinnamon can slightly thicken the mixture; adjust xanthan gum down by 1/8 tsp if needed.

Peach and Ginger Protein Bowl

Incorporate diced fresh peaches and a tiny pinch of ground ginger for a sweet-spicy combo. This adds flavor complexity and natural sweetness without added sugar. Volume and calories stay low, perfect for summer.

Note: Ginger may intensify flavor quickly; start with a small amount to avoid overpowering.

Storage & Tips

Room Temperature

This snack should not be left at room temperature for more than 1 hour due to the dairy and egg whites. Cover it tightly if you need to keep it out briefly, but refrigeration is best to maintain texture and safety. Warm temperatures can cause watery separation and volume loss.

Refrigerator

Store in airtight containers for up to 3 days. The snack firms up nicely when chilled and the flavors meld. Texture remains creamy and fluffy after stirring. It tastes best cold but can be brought to room temperature for 10 minutes before eating if preferred.

Freezer

Freezing is not recommended as the high water content in yogurt and egg whites causes icy texture and separation when thawed. The volume and creaminess are lost, resulting in an unpleasant mouthfeel.

Reheating

This snack is best enjoyed cold. Reheating can cause separation and loss of fluffiness due to the egg whites and xanthan gum. If you prefer it warmer, gently stir in a splash of unsweetened almond milk and serve at room temperature.

Make Ahead

Prepare the base mixture 1-2 days ahead and store in the fridge. Add fresh berries or crunchy vegetables just before serving to maintain texture and volume. Portion into individual containers for grab-and-go convenience during the week.

FAQ

Can I use flavored protein powder in this recipe?
You can, but be aware that flavored powders often contain added sugars and fillers which can increase calories and affect texture. Unflavored whey isolate is best to keep the snack low-calorie and maintain a clean flavor. If you use flavored powder, reduce or omit the sweetener to balance sweetness.
Why does my snack sometimes turn watery after mixing?
Watery texture usually happens if xanthan gum is skipped or not mixed properly. Xanthan gum binds water and stabilizes the mixture. Also, warm ingredients can cause separation. Chill all ingredients beforehand and add xanthan gum slowly while whisking to avoid this issue.
Can I substitute Greek yogurt with regular yogurt?
Regular yogurt is thinner and lower in protein, which will affect texture and protein content. Greek yogurt’s thickness and high protein are key for volume and creaminess. If using regular yogurt, strain it to remove excess whey and consider adding more protein powder.
Is it safe to eat raw egg whites in this recipe?
Use pasteurized liquid egg whites to ensure safety since they have been heat-treated to kill bacteria. Avoid raw egg whites from fresh eggs, which carry a risk of salmonella. Pasteurized egg whites also whip better and blend smoothly.
How long does this snack keep in the fridge?
Stored in airtight containers, this snack stays fresh for up to 3 days. The texture remains creamy and fluffy after stirring. Beyond that, the yogurt may start to sour and the egg whites lose their volume, so it’s best consumed within this timeframe.
Can I add nuts or nut butter to increase calories?
You can, but nuts and nut butters add significant calories and fats, which goes against the low-calorie goal of this snack. If you want more calories, add small amounts (1 tsp) of nut butter, but be mindful of portion size to keep macros balanced.
What’s the best time to eat this snack?
This snack works great as a post-workout refuel, afternoon energy boost, or anytime you want a filling, low-calorie protein hit. Its volume and protein content help curb hunger and support muscle recovery.
How do I avoid the chalky taste from protein powder?
Use unflavored whey isolate and mix it into cold acidic dairy like Greek yogurt. Adding vanilla extract and sweetener also helps mask chalkiness. Chilling the mixture for at least an hour lets the protein hydrate fully, improving texture and flavor.
