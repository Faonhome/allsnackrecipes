Easy 10-Minute Creamy Cucumber High-Protein Low-Cal Snack

Chef
Published on August 24, 2026
10 min Prep Time
6 Servings
Easy Difficulty
4.6 (55 ratings)✓ Thanks for rating!

This creamy cucumber snack is a refreshing, high-protein treat that keeps calories low and volume high. Using zero-fat Greek yogurt and fresh cucumbers, it’s perfect for a light post-workout boost or afternoon pick-me-up without guilt.

Introduction

I’ve always struggled with feeling hungry on low-calorie days, especially when trying to cut without losing muscle. Small snacks just didn’t satisfy me, and I hated how protein bars felt so dense and sometimes chalky. That’s why I created this creamy cucumber snack — it’s huge in volume but light on calories, so I can eat a big bowl and still stay under 200 calories. The secret? Using zero-fat Greek yogurt whipped with egg whites to get that fluffy, creamy texture without any added fat. Plus, fresh cucumber adds crunch and hydration, making it feel like a real meal instead of a tiny bite. I used to try similar recipes that turned out watery or bland, but adding a touch of xanthan gum and fresh lemon juice really brings it all together. Now, I eat this every afternoon when I need a pick-me-up or after workouts, and my friends always ask how I manage such big portions while losing weight. It’s the perfect balance of creamy, refreshing, and satisfying—without the guilt.

What You'll Need

Approximately 1.5 cups per serving, 6 generous snack servings

  • 2 cups zero-fat Greek yogurt, chilled
  • 1 large cucumber, diced
  • 3 large egg whites, lightly whipped
  • 1 tablespoon fresh dill, finely chopped
  • 1 teaspoon lemon juice, freshly squeezed
  • 1/2 teaspoon xanthan gum (optional, for creaminess)
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • Stevia or monk fruit sweetener, to taste (optional)

How To Do It

Step 1
Start by finely dicing the cucumber into small cubes. This adds bulk and crunch without many calories, which is key for volume eating. Make sure to pat the cucumber dry with a paper towel to remove excess water—too much moisture can make the snack watery and dilute the creamy texture. Using fresh, firm cucumbers will give the best bite and avoid sogginess. This step sets the foundation for a satisfying snack that feels like you’re eating a lot without packing in calories.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Cucumber pieces are small, dry, and firm to the touch without excess moisture
Step 2
In a chilled bowl, combine the zero-fat Greek yogurt with lemon juice and fresh dill. Stir gently to blend the flavors. The acidity from the lemon brightens the yogurt and helps prevent the protein powder or yogurt from tasting chalky, which is common in low-fat mixtures. Adding herbs like dill gives freshness and complexity without calories. This mixture forms the creamy base that carries the protein and flavor in the snack.
⏱ Time: 3 minutes
✓ Doneness: Yogurt mixture is smooth, slightly tangy, and well combined with herbs
Step 3
Lightly whip the egg whites until soft peaks form. Whipping egg whites adds volume and fluffiness without fat or calories. This technique is essential because low-fat dairy alone can feel dense or chalky, but folding in whipped egg whites creates a lighter, mousse-like texture. Be careful not to overbeat, which can make the whites dry and difficult to fold in smoothly.
⏱ Time: 4 minutes
✓ Doneness: Egg whites hold soft peaks that gently bend over when the whisk is lifted
Step 4
If using xanthan gum, sprinkle it evenly over the yogurt mixture and whisk quickly to avoid clumping. Xanthan gum acts as a zero-calorie thickener to mimic creaminess lost by skipping fat. It helps the snack hold together and prevents watery separation. This step is optional but recommended for best texture, especially if you want to store the snack for later.
⏱ Time: 1 minute
✓ Doneness: Yogurt mixture thickens slightly and feels smooth without lumps
Step 5
Gently fold the whipped egg whites into the yogurt mixture using a spatula. Use a light hand to keep as much air as possible. This folding technique preserves volume and creates a creamy, airy texture that feels indulgent but is low in calories. Avoid stirring vigorously, which deflates the egg whites and results in a dense final product.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Mixture looks fluffy and uniform without streaks of egg white
Step 6
Fold in the diced cucumber carefully to distribute the crunch evenly throughout the creamy base. This adds texture contrast and keeps the snack feeling fresh and satisfying. The cucumber also adds hydration and fiber, which helps with fullness and digestion. Make sure the cucumber is evenly mixed but not crushed to maintain volume.
⏱ Time: 1 minute
✓ Doneness: Cucumber pieces are evenly dispersed without breaking down
Step 7
Taste the mixture and season with salt, pepper, and a pinch of stevia or monk fruit sweetener if desired. Balancing savory and sweet flavors enhances satisfaction without adding calories. Adjust seasoning carefully because over-salting can make the snack feel heavy, while a touch of sweetness brightens the overall flavor profile.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Flavor is balanced, fresh, and slightly tangy with a hint of sweetness
Step 8
Chill the snack in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes before serving. Chilling helps the xanthan gum thicken the mixture further and allows flavors to meld. It also firms the texture, making it easier to scoop and eat. Skipping this step can result in a runnier snack that doesn’t hold its shape well.
⏱ Time: 30 minutes
✓ Doneness: Snack is cold, slightly firm, and creamy with no watery separation
Step 9
Serve in large bowls or portion into meal prep containers for easy grab-and-go snacks. This recipe yields a generous serving size that feels like a full snack or light meal. The volume and protein content keep you full for hours without excess calories. Enjoy chilled, optionally garnished with extra dill or a lemon wedge for added freshness.
⏱ Time: 1 minute
✓ Doneness: Snack holds its shape well and looks appetizing with visible cucumber pieces

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Skipping the xanthan gum and ending up with a watery snack.

Why it happens People often assume yogurt alone will stay thick, but zero-fat yogurt lacks fat to stabilize texture, causing watery separation.

Fix Add a small amount of xanthan gum (about 1 teaspoon) to thicken and stabilize the mixture. Whisk it in thoroughly to avoid lumps and improve creaminess.

Pro Tips

Use chilled ingredients and bowls when whipping egg whites and mixing yogurt.
Cold temperatures help egg whites whip more easily and hold volume better. Chilled yogurt mixes more smoothly and prevents early separation. Warm ingredients can cause the mixture to become watery and lose fluffiness quickly.
Pat cucumbers dry before folding into the mixture.
Removing excess moisture from cucumbers prevents watery separation in the final snack. Water dilutes the creamy base and reduces the satisfying texture, making the snack less enjoyable and less filling.
Add xanthan gum gradually and whisk quickly to avoid clumps.
Xanthan gum thickens the mixture to mimic fat’s creaminess but tends to clump if not dispersed properly. Whisking immediately after sprinkling ensures a smooth texture without lumps, improving mouthfeel and stability.

Variations

Spicy Creamy Cucumber Snack

Add a pinch of cayenne pepper and chopped jalapeño for a spicy kick. This variation keeps the protein and calories similar but adds bold flavor. The heat pairs well with the cool cucumber and creamy base, making it a refreshing yet zesty snack.

Note: Be careful folding in jalapeño to avoid breaking up the texture. Adjust spice level to taste.

Storage & Tips

Room Temperature

Due to the zero-fat dairy and egg whites, this snack should not be left at room temperature for more than 1 hour. Cover it tightly to prevent drying out. Warm temperatures can cause separation and loss of volume.

Refrigerator

Store in an airtight container for up to 3 days. The snack firms up when chilled and flavors meld nicely. It maintains volume well but may lose a bit of fluffiness over time. Best served cold or at room temperature.

Freezer

Not recommended to freeze this snack as the high water content in cucumber and yogurt will form ice crystals, ruining texture. Freeze if necessary for up to 1 month, thaw in fridge, and stir gently.

Reheating

This snack is best eaten cold or at room temperature. Reheating is not advised as it will cause egg whites and xanthan gum to break down, leading to separation and loss of texture.

Make Ahead

Prepare the yogurt base and whip egg whites up to 2 days ahead, storing separately in airtight containers. Dice cucumber fresh on the day of serving to maintain crunch. Fold everything together just before eating for best volume and texture.

FAQ

Can I substitute the egg whites with something else?
Egg whites are key here for adding volume without fat or calories. If you’re vegan or allergic, aquafaba (chickpea water) whipped to soft peaks can work as a substitute, but texture and flavor may differ slightly. It won’t be quite as creamy but still adds lightness.
How do I avoid a chalky taste from the protein powder?
Using acidic ingredients like lemon juice helps mask chalkiness. Also, choose a high-quality whey isolate with minimal fillers. Mixing protein powder thoroughly in cold yogurt and combining with herbs and spices balances flavor.
Is xanthan gum necessary?
Xanthan gum isn’t mandatory but highly recommended. It thickens the mixture and prevents watery separation common in zero-fat dairy snacks. Without it, the texture can be runny and less satisfying.
Can I add sweeteners?
Yes! Use natural zero-calorie sweeteners like stevia or monk fruit if you want a touch of sweetness. Add sparingly to avoid aftertaste. This snack balances savory and sweet well, so sweetener is optional.
How long does this snack keep me full?
Thanks to its high protein and fiber from cucumber, this snack keeps most people satisfied for 2-3 hours. The volume eating effect helps prevent hunger pangs despite low calories.
Can I meal prep this for the week?
Yes, but keep the cucumber separate until ready to eat for best crunch. The yogurt and egg white base can be made up to 2 days ahead and stored chilled. Fold in cucumber fresh for maximum texture.
What if I don’t have xanthan gum?
You can try adding a small amount of chia seeds soaked in water as a thickener, but this will change texture and add some calories. Otherwise, accept a looser texture and stir before eating.
Is this snack suitable for cutting phases?
Absolutely! It’s low in calories, high in protein, and large in volume, making it perfect for cutting without feeling deprived or hungry.
