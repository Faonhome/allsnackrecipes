Easy 10-Minute Quick Cucumber High-Protein Low-Cal Snack

Published on August 18, 2026
10 min Prep Time
6 Servings

This quick cucumber snack mixes crisp fresh cucumber with creamy zero-fat Greek yogurt and egg whites for a protein-packed, low-calorie treat. It’s perfect for an afternoon pick-me-up or post-workout refuel when you want something filling but light!

What You'll Need

Approximately 1.5 cups volume per serving, 6 snack servings

  • 2 large cucumbers, peeled and diced
  • 1 cup zero-fat Greek yogurt, chilled
  • 4 large egg whites, lightly whipped
  • 1 tsp lemon juice, fresh
  • 1/2 tsp xanthan gum (optional, for creaminess)
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 tsp chopped fresh dill or parsley
  • Stevia or monk fruit sweetener to taste (optional)

How To Do It

Step 1
Start by peeling and dicing the cucumbers into small, bite-sized pieces. This increases the surface area, helping the cucumbers mix better with the yogurt and egg whites. Make sure to remove any excess water by gently patting the diced cucumber with a paper towel—too much moisture can make the snack watery and dilute the flavors. This step is crucial because cucumbers hold a lot of water, and if you skip drying them, your snack might turn out soggy and less creamy.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Cucumber pieces feel dry to the touch and are evenly diced.
Step 2
In a chilled bowl, combine the zero-fat Greek yogurt with lemon juice, salt, pepper, and the optional stevia or monk fruit sweetener. Stir gently to blend the flavors. The acidic lemon juice not only brightens the taste but also helps prevent the protein powder or yogurt from tasting chalky. Mixing at this stage ensures the seasoning is evenly distributed before adding the egg whites.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: The yogurt mixture looks smooth and slightly glossy.
Step 3
Whip the egg whites until they form soft peaks using a hand mixer or whisk. Whipping incorporates air, which adds volume and fluffiness to the snack without adding calories or fat. Be careful not to overbeat—stiff peaks can make the texture rubbery. Soft peaks provide the perfect lightness to balance the creamy yogurt and crunchy cucumber.
⏱ Time: 3 minutes
✓ Doneness: Egg whites hold soft peaks that gently fold over when the whisk is lifted.
Step 4
If using xanthan gum, sprinkle it evenly over the yogurt mixture and whisk quickly to prevent clumps. Xanthan gum acts as a thickener and stabilizer, preventing watery separation in this low-fat snack. It mimics creaminess without adding calories or fat. This step is optional but highly recommended if you want a silky, consistent texture.
⏱ Time: 1 minute
✓ Doneness: The mixture thickens slightly and feels smooth without lumps.
Step 5
Gently fold the whipped egg whites into the yogurt mixture with a spatula. Use a light hand to keep as much air as possible, which ensures the snack stays fluffy. Folding rather than stirring vigorously preserves the volume and gives you that airy texture that makes the snack feel indulgent despite being low-calorie.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Mixture looks light and airy with no streaks of egg whites or yogurt.
Step 6
Fold in the diced cucumber and fresh herbs carefully. The goal is to evenly distribute the cucumber without deflating the fluffy mixture. The herbs add fresh flavor and a nice color contrast. Mixing too roughly here can cause the snack to lose its volume and become watery.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Cucumber and herbs are evenly dispersed throughout the creamy mixture.
Step 7
Taste and adjust seasoning with a pinch more salt, pepper, or sweetener if needed. The balance of savory with a hint of sweet and acidity makes this snack really pop. Don’t skip tasting—sometimes a little tweak makes all the difference in flavor without adding calories.
⏱ Time: 1 minute
✓ Doneness: Flavor is balanced, fresh, and satisfying.
Step 8
Chill the snack in the refrigerator for at least 10 minutes before serving. Chilling firms up the texture because the zero-fat yogurt and egg whites set slightly when cold. This step also helps the flavors meld together. If you try to eat it right away, it might feel too loose or watery.
⏱ Time: 10 minutes
✓ Doneness: Snack is cool and slightly firmer to the touch.
Step 9
Serve in generous bowls or portion into meal prep containers for grab-and-go snacking. The volume here is impressive—about 1.5 cups per serving—so you’ll feel full without overdoing calories. Enjoy immediately or keep chilled until ready to eat.
⏱ Time: 0 minutes
✓ Doneness: Snack holds its shape, looks appetizing, and is ready to enjoy.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Skipping drying the cucumber leading to watery snack.

Why it happens People often assume fresh vegetables don’t add much water, but cucumbers are over 90% water. Without drying, this moisture dilutes the creamy base.

Fix After dicing, gently pat cucumbers dry with paper towels to remove excess water. This prevents watery separation and keeps the snack creamy and thick.

Overwhipping egg whites to stiff peaks.

Why it happens Trying to get maximum volume, some overbeat the egg whites, which tightens the protein and squeezes out moisture.

Fix Whip only to soft peaks where whites hold shape but still fold easily. This keeps the texture light and moist.

Using full-fat yogurt instead of zero-fat.

Why it happens Full-fat yogurts add calories and fat, defeating the purpose of a low-calorie snack.

Fix Choose plain zero-fat Greek yogurt to keep calories low and protein high without added fat.

Adding too much xanthan gum causing gummy texture.

Why it happens More isn’t better with thickeners; excess xanthan gum creates unpleasant sliminess.

Fix Use only about 1/2 to 1 teaspoon per batch and whisk quickly to avoid clumps.

Not chilling the snack before eating.

Why it happens Eating immediately results in a loose, watery texture as the mixture hasn’t set.

Fix Refrigerate for at least 10 minutes to firm up and blend flavors.

Pro Tips

Pat the diced cucumber dry before mixing.
Cucumbers hold a lot of water, which can make your snack watery and thin. Removing excess moisture ensures the creamy yogurt and whipped egg whites maintain their fluffy texture and prevents separation.
Whip egg whites to soft peaks, not stiff.
Soft peaks add volume and lightness without becoming rubbery or dry. Overwhipping causes the protein structure to tighten and lose moisture, resulting in an unpleasant texture.
Use xanthan gum sparingly to thicken.
Too much xanthan gum creates a gummy, slimy texture. Just a small amount stabilizes the mixture, mimics fat creaminess, and prevents watery separation in this low-fat, high-protein snack.
Chill the snack before serving.
Chilling firms the texture as the yogurt and egg whites set slightly, making the snack more enjoyable and less watery. Eating it immediately may feel loose and less satisfying.
Add fresh herbs for flavor without calories.
Herbs like dill or parsley brighten the dish and add aroma and color, enhancing the eating experience without adding calories or fat.
Use fresh lemon juice, not bottled.
Fresh lemon juice has a brighter, cleaner taste and no preservatives, which improves flavor and helps reduce any chalkiness from protein ingredients.
Fold ingredients gently to keep volume.
Folding preserves the air whipped into egg whites, ensuring the snack stays fluffy and light. Stirring too vigorously deflates the mixture and results in a denser, less pleasant texture.

Variations

Spicy Quick Cucumber Snack

Add a pinch of cayenne pepper and a dash of smoked paprika to the yogurt mixture for a spicy kick. This variation keeps macros similar but adds a warming flavor that pairs well with the cool cucumber. Expect a slightly bolder taste but the same creamy, fluffy texture.

Note: Spices won’t affect texture but add complexity; ensure to balance salt accordingly.

Berry-Infused Quick Cucumber Bowl

Fold in 1/4 cup fresh blueberries or raspberries for a sweet contrast. This adds natural antioxidants and a hint of sweetness while keeping calories low. Protein remains high, but carbs increase slightly due to fruit sugars.

Note: Add berries last and fold gently to avoid crushing; chill before serving.

Vegan Quick Cucumber Snack

Replace egg whites with aquafaba (chickpea water) whipped to soft peaks and use a plant-based zero-fat yogurt. Protein is slightly lower but still adequate, and the texture remains airy and creamy with a subtle legume flavor.

Note: Aquafaba whips differently; whip longer and fold carefully to maintain volume.

Herb-Loaded Quick Cucumber Snack

Double the fresh herbs like dill, parsley, and chives for a fragrant, savory snack. This doesn’t change macros but enhances flavor complexity and freshness, making it ideal for savory snack lovers.

Note: Chop herbs finely and fold in gently to keep texture light.

Protein Powder Boosted Quick Cucumber Snack

Add 1 scoop unflavored whey isolate protein powder to the yogurt before folding in egg whites. This ups protein to 25g per serving with minimal calorie increase. Texture thickens slightly, so xanthan gum is recommended to maintain creaminess.

Note: Mix protein powder well with yogurt to prevent clumps and chalkiness.

Storage & Tips

Room Temperature

This snack should not be left at room temperature for more than 1 hour due to the egg whites and dairy content. Keep covered to prevent contamination. Water separation may occur if left out too long, affecting texture.

Refrigerator

Store in an airtight container for up to 3 days. The snack firms up when chilled and flavors meld nicely. Volume remains good, though some slight water separation can occur—just stir gently before eating. Best enjoyed cold.

Freezer

Freezing is not recommended as the high water content in cucumber and yogurt causes icy texture and separation upon thawing. If frozen, thaw in fridge and stir well but texture will be compromised.

Reheating

This snack is best eaten cold and does not reheat well. Heating can cause the egg whites and xanthan gum to separate and lose texture. If needed, serve at room temperature but avoid microwaving.

Make Ahead

Prepare the yogurt and egg white mixture up to 2 days ahead and store chilled. Dice cucumber fresh the day of serving to maintain crunch and prevent sogginess. Add cucumber just before eating or packing for best texture.

FAQ

Can I substitute protein powder for egg whites in this recipe?
You can add protein powder to boost protein content, but it won’t replace the volume and fluffiness that whipped egg whites provide. Egg whites add air and light texture, which protein powder alone can’t achieve. For best results, use both or keep egg whites as the main volume source.
Why does my snack turn watery after mixing?
This usually happens if the cucumber isn’t dried well or if you skip xanthan gum. Cucumbers release water, which dilutes the creamy base. Patting cucumber dry and adding a small amount of xanthan gum helps stabilize the mixture and maintain creaminess.
Is it safe to eat raw egg whites in this snack?
Using pasteurized egg whites significantly reduces any risk from raw eggs. If you’re concerned, opt for pasteurized cartons or cook the egg whites gently before whipping, though this may change texture. Alternatively, aquafaba can be used for a vegan option.
Can I use full-fat Greek yogurt instead of zero-fat?
You can, but it will increase calories and fat content, making the snack less suitable for low-calorie goals. Full-fat yogurt also changes texture, making it richer but less airy. For a light, fluffy snack, zero-fat yogurt is best.
How long does this snack keep in the fridge?
Stored in an airtight container, it keeps well for up to 3 days. The texture may firm up and some water separation can occur, so stir gently before eating. Fresh cucumber crunch is best within 1-2 days.
Can I add other vegetables instead of cucumber?
Yes, crunchy low-calorie veggies like celery or bell peppers work well. However, cucumber’s high water content and mild flavor make it ideal for volume and freshness. Other veggies may alter texture and flavor.
What sweeteners work best in this recipe?
Stevia and monk fruit are excellent zero-calorie options that don’t add aftertaste when used sparingly. Avoid sugar or honey as they add calories and can change texture.
Is this snack suitable for meal prep?
Absolutely! Prepare the creamy base ahead and add fresh cucumber just before eating or packing. This keeps the cucumber crunchy and the snack fresh for up to 3 days refrigerated.
