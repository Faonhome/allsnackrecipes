Easy Radish Salad Recipes Recipe: A Refreshing High-Protein Snack Twist

Cynthia Sullivan Published on May 22, 2026
10 min prep 0 min cook 2 servings

This crisp radish salad packs a surprising protein punch thanks to the addition of cottage cheese and pumpkin seeds. Fresh, crunchy, and ready in 10 minutes — perfect for a light post-workout snack or midday pick-me-up!

About This Recipe

I first stumbled upon this radish salad during a hectic week when I needed a fresh, protein-packed snack that didn't require baking or complicated prep. I was craving something crunchy and light post-workout, but with enough protein to keep me full. Adding cottage cheese and pumpkin seeds to the crisp radishes was a game-changer — it turned a simple salad into a satisfying snack with about 15 grams of protein per serving. What I love most is how quick it is: just 10 minutes to chop, mix, and enjoy. Unlike many store-bought protein snacks that can be chalky or overly processed, this recipe uses real food ingredients you can pronounce and trust. The trick I learned was to keep the cottage cheese slightly chilled and toasting the pumpkin seeds lightly for extra crunch and flavor. The lemon juice and olive oil dressing tie it all together with a fresh zing. Honestly, this salad became my go-to on busy days and a staple in my Sunday meal prep. It’s light but filling, and the texture combo keeps me coming back. If you’re looking for a clean, easy, and protein-rich snack that feels more like a treat than a chore, this radish salad is your new best friend.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup sliced fresh radishes
  • 1/2 cup low-fat cottage cheese (4% fat for best texture)
  • 2 tbsp shelled pumpkin seeds
  • 1 tbsp chopped fresh chives
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • Salt and pepper to taste
