Easy 10-Minute Cheesy Corn Recipe for a High-Protein Low-Cal Snack

Chef
Published on August 22, 2026
10 min Prep Time
6 Servings
Easy Difficulty
4.5 (43 ratings)✓ Thanks for rating!

This cheesy corn snack mixes creamy low-fat cheese with sweet corn for a satisfying, protein-packed treat under 200 calories. The secret? Using fat-free cheese and a touch of Greek yogurt to keep it fluffy and filling. Perfect for an afternoon pick-me-up or post-workout refuel!

What You'll Need

Approximately 1 cup per serving, 6 snack servings, generous portion size for volume eating

  • 1 1/2 cups cooked sweet corn kernels, fresh or thawed frozen
  • 3/4 cup fat-free shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 cup zero-fat Greek yogurt, plain
  • 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • Salt to taste (about 1/8 teaspoon)
  • Freshly ground black pepper, a pinch
  • 1 teaspoon chopped fresh chives (optional, for garnish)

How To Do It

Step 1
Start by gently warming the cooked corn kernels in a non-stick pan over low heat for about 3 minutes. This step helps release the natural sweetness and softens the corn just enough to blend well with the cheese without making the mixture watery. Avoid high heat here, as it can dry out the corn or cause it to stick. Stir occasionally to prevent burning and ensure even warming. Gently warming also prevents the cheese from clumping when added later, helping it melt smoothly into the corn.
⏱ Time: 3 minutes
✓ Doneness: Corn is warm to the touch and slightly softened but not mushy
Step 2
Lower the heat to very low and add the fat-free shredded mozzarella cheese to the warmed corn. Stir continuously for about 2 minutes until the cheese starts to melt and coat the corn evenly. Keep the heat low to avoid the cheese becoming rubbery or separating. This slow melting process ensures a creamy texture without adding fat. If you rush this step or use high heat, the cheese may clump or become grainy, ruining the smoothness of the snack.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Cheese is melted and fully combined with corn, forming a creamy coating
Step 3
Remove the pan from heat and immediately stir in the zero-fat Greek yogurt. Mixing the yogurt off the heat prevents it from curdling or separating, which can happen if added to hot ingredients. The yogurt adds creaminess and a slight tang that balances the sweetness of the corn and richness of the cheese without fat. Stir gently but thoroughly to combine into a smooth, fluffy mixture.
⏱ Time: 1 minute
✓ Doneness: Mixture is creamy, smooth, and slightly fluffy with no lumps
Step 4
Add the garlic powder, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper. These spices enhance the cheesy corn flavor and add a subtle smoky depth without calories. Stir well to evenly distribute the seasoning. Taste and adjust salt if needed. Seasoning at this stage is important because the flavors meld better once mixed with the cool yogurt.
⏱ Time: 1 minute
✓ Doneness: Spices are evenly mixed, and the aroma is inviting and balanced
Step 5
Transfer the mixture to a large bowl and let it chill in the refrigerator for at least 10 minutes. Chilling helps the flavors meld and firms up the texture since there’s no fat to provide structure. The cold temperature also enhances the creamy mouthfeel and keeps the snack refreshing. Avoid skipping this step, or the snack may feel loose or watery.
⏱ Time: 10 minutes
✓ Doneness: Mixture is firm but creamy, with flavors blended
Step 6
Before serving, give the cheesy corn a gentle stir to fluff it up again. If you want extra volume without calories, you can fold in a teaspoon of xanthan gum dissolved in a tablespoon of cold water to thicken and stabilize the mixture. This step is optional but great for preventing watery separation if you plan to store leftovers.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Snack is light, fluffy, and thickened without lumps
Step 7
Divide the cheesy corn evenly into six portions for individual servings. Using a measuring cup helps keep portions consistent for accurate calorie and protein tracking. Each serving is about 1 cup, which feels like a generous snack helping with volume eating and satiety.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Six even portions ready for storage or immediate eating
Step 8
Garnish each serving with chopped fresh chives if desired. This adds a fresh flavor and a nice pop of color without adding calories. It’s a simple way to make the snack feel more special and visually appealing.
⏱ Time: 1 minute
✓ Doneness: Fresh chives evenly sprinkled on top, snack looks inviting
Step 9
Enjoy immediately or store the portions in airtight containers in the fridge. This snack keeps well chilled and can be eaten cold or at room temperature. Re-stir before eating to restore fluffiness if needed. Avoid reheating as it may change the texture and cause separation.
⏱ Time: 0 minutes
✓ Doneness: Snack is fresh, creamy, and ready to eat or store

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Skipping the slow warming step, resulting in clumpy cheese.

Why it happens People often rush this step, thinking high heat will melt cheese faster. However, this causes uneven melting and rubbery texture.

Fix Warm corn slowly on low heat before adding cheese, stirring constantly. This ensures smooth melting and a creamy texture.

Adding Greek yogurt to hot mixture, causing curdling.

Why it happens Adding yogurt while pan is still hot can cause the proteins to separate and curdle.

Fix Remove pan from heat before stirring in yogurt. This preserves creaminess and prevents separation.

Using full-fat cheese or yogurt, increasing calories unexpectedly.

Why it happens Some assume all cheeses or yogurts are similar in calories, leading to higher calorie counts.

Fix Use fat-free mozzarella and zero-fat Greek yogurt to keep calories low and protein high.

Not measuring portions, leading to overeating.

Why it happens Without measuring, it’s easy to eat more than intended, especially with tasty snacks.

Fix Use a measuring cup to divide into consistent 1-cup servings for accurate tracking.

Skipping chilling step, resulting in watery texture.

Why it happens People want to eat immediately and miss the firming effect of chilling.

Fix Chill the snack for at least 10 minutes to let flavors meld and texture set.

Pro Tips

Warm the corn slowly over low heat before adding cheese.
Slow warming releases natural sugars and softens the corn without drying it out or making it mushy. This step ensures the cheese melts smoothly over the corn, creating a creamy texture rather than clumps. High heat can cause uneven melting and a rubbery cheese texture, which ruins the snack's mouthfeel.
Add Greek yogurt off the heat to prevent curdling.
Greek yogurt contains proteins that can separate or curdle if exposed to high heat. Adding it after removing the pan from heat preserves its creamy texture and tangy flavor. This step is crucial for maintaining the smooth, fluffy consistency that makes the snack feel indulgent without fat.
Use fat-free shredded mozzarella for the best melt and protein boost.
Fat-free mozzarella provides a good protein punch while keeping calories low. It melts differently than full-fat cheese, so warming it carefully with the corn prevents clumping. Choosing the right cheese brand without fillers ensures clean flavor and texture, key to a satisfying low-calorie snack.
Chill the mixture to firm up texture and meld flavors.
Chilling helps the snack firm up since there's no fat to provide structure. It also allows the flavors to blend, making the snack taste richer and more balanced. Skipping chilling can result in a watery, loose texture that feels less satisfying.
Fold in xanthan gum dissolved in water if you want extra thickness.
Xanthan gum stabilizes the mixture without adding calories or fat. It prevents watery separation during storage and improves mouthfeel by mimicking creaminess. This is especially helpful for meal prep or if you prefer a firmer snack texture.
Measure portions with a cup to maintain calorie accuracy.
Consistent portion sizes help with tracking macros and calories, which is key for anyone on a deficit or meal prepping. Using a measuring cup ensures you get the volume eating benefits without accidentally overeating.
Avoid reheating this snack to preserve texture.
Reheating can cause the egg-white-like proteins in yogurt and cheese to separate, resulting in a grainy or watery texture. The snack is best enjoyed cold or at room temperature, maintaining its creamy and fluffy qualities.

Variations

Spicy Jalapeño Cheesy Corn

Add finely chopped jalapeños and a pinch of cayenne pepper for a spicy kick. This variation adds a bit of heat while keeping calories low. The spice complements the creamy cheese and sweet corn perfectly. Protein and calorie counts remain similar.

Note: Add jalapeños with the spices in step 4 to let flavors infuse evenly.

Herbed Cheesy Corn with Basil and Oregano

Swap chives for fresh basil and oregano for a Mediterranean twist. This adds fresh herbal notes without calories and pairs well with the creamy texture. It’s a great way to switch up flavors while maintaining high protein and low calories.

Note: Add herbs as garnish or fold in gently before chilling for best flavor.

Cheesy Corn with Egg White Whip

Fold in softly whipped pasteurized egg whites to increase volume and protein without adding fat or calories. This makes the snack even fluffier and lighter. Expect a slightly different texture, more airy and mousse-like.

Note: Whip egg whites to soft peaks and fold in gently after mixing cheese and yogurt.

Smoked Paprika and Lemon Zest Cheesy Corn

Add fresh lemon zest along with smoked paprika for a bright citrus note that cuts through the creaminess. It adds freshness and complexity without calories. Protein and volume remain high, making this a refreshing snack option.

Note: Add lemon zest with spices in step 4 for balanced flavor.

Cheesy Corn with Roasted Red Pepper Puree

Stir in a few tablespoons of roasted red pepper puree to add sweetness and color. This variation adds antioxidants and a subtle smoky flavor. It keeps the snack low calorie and high protein while adding a vegetable boost.

Note: Mix puree in with Greek yogurt off heat to maintain creaminess.

Storage & Tips

Room Temperature

This snack should not be kept at room temperature for more than 1 hour due to the dairy content. Cover with plastic wrap or store in an airtight container to prevent drying out. If left out longer, the texture may become watery and less appealing.

Refrigerator

Store in an airtight container for up to 3 days. Chilling firms the texture and enhances flavor. The snack holds its volume well after chilling but may need a gentle stir before serving to restore fluffiness. It tastes best cold or at room temperature.

Freezer

Freezing is not recommended as the high water content and dairy can separate and become icy. If frozen, thaw slowly in the refrigerator and expect some texture loss.

Reheating

Avoid reheating as it can cause separation and graininess. If you prefer it warm, gently bring to room temperature and stir in a splash of cold water or almond milk to restore creaminess.

Make Ahead

Prepare the cheesy corn mixture up to 2 days ahead and store in the fridge. Add fresh herbs or chives just before serving to keep them vibrant. Portion into containers for grab-and-go snacks. Adding xanthan gum before chilling helps maintain texture during storage.

FAQ

Can I use full-fat cheese or yogurt instead of fat-free versions?
You can, but it will increase the calorie and fat content significantly. Using fat-free cheese and zero-fat Greek yogurt keeps this snack low-calorie and high-protein, which is the whole point. Full-fat versions also change the texture and may make the snack feel heavier rather than fluffy.
How can I prevent the cheesy corn from becoming watery?
Slow warming of corn, adding yogurt off heat, and chilling the mixture all help prevent watery texture. Optionally, fold in a small amount of xanthan gum dissolved in water to stabilize. Avoid rushing heating steps or skipping chilling, as these cause separation and watery consistency.
Can I add protein powder to this recipe?
Yes, but choose an unflavored or mild whey isolate to avoid chalkiness. Add it with the Greek yogurt off heat and whisk well to incorporate. Be mindful that protein powder can thicken the mixture, so you may need to adjust yogurt or add a little water to keep creaminess.
Is this snack suitable for meal prep?
Absolutely! It stores well in the fridge for up to 3 days. Portion into containers for easy grab-and-go snacks. Just stir gently before eating to fluff it back up. Avoid freezing, as texture suffers.
When is the best time to eat this snack?
This snack works great as an afternoon pick-me-up, post-workout refuel, or whenever you need a filling, high-protein snack under 200 calories. Its volume and protein content help curb hunger and keep energy steady.
Can I make this snack vegan or dairy-free?
This recipe relies on dairy for creaminess and protein, so a vegan version would need alternative protein sources like silken tofu or plant-based yogurts and cheeses. Texture and flavor will differ significantly, so it requires experimentation.
How do I avoid a chalky taste from the cheese or yogurt?
Using fresh, high-quality fat-free mozzarella and Greek yogurt with good acidity helps mask chalkiness. Also, warming cheese gently and adding yogurt off heat creates a smooth texture. Adding spices like smoked paprika and garlic powder enhances flavor and reduces any aftertaste.
Can I add vegetables for more volume?
Yes! Finely diced bell peppers or cucumbers can add crunch and volume with minimal calories. Add them after chilling to keep them crisp. This boosts fiber and bulk without affecting protein much.
