Easy 10-Minute Spiced Pumpkin Protein Snack That’s Low Cal and Filling

Chef
Published on November 6, 2026
10 min Prep Time
6 Servings
Easy Difficulty
4.6 (49 ratings)✓ Thanks for rating!

This spiced pumpkin snack mixes creamy zero-fat Greek yogurt with pumpkin puree and warming spices for a high-protein, low-calorie treat that feels like dessert without the guilt. Perfect for an afternoon pick-me-up or post-workout refuel!

Introduction

I’ve always struggled with feeling hungry on low-calorie days, especially when I’m cutting or trying to stay lean. Tiny snack portions just don’t cut it for me — I want something that feels like a real treat but won’t sabotage my macros. That’s why I love this spiced pumpkin protein snack. It’s creamy, cozy, and packed with protein, yet it’s under 150 calories per serving. The secret? Using zero-fat Greek yogurt as the base gives it a thick, creamy texture without the fat, and mixing in pumpkin puree adds volume and fiber so you feel full longer. Plus, the warming spices make it taste like fall in a bowl, which is perfect for my afternoon slump or post-gym refuel. I once tried making this without xanthan gum and it turned out watery — such a bummer! Now I add just a pinch, which helps it thicken up beautifully without adding calories. I also found that using a good quality vanilla whey isolate keeps the texture smooth and avoids that chalky protein powder taste. This snack is a total game changer for me when I want a filling, flavorful treat that fits right into my calorie goals. It feels like I’m eating a giant bowl, but it’s all smart ingredients that keep me on track.

What You'll Need

Approximately 1 large bowl per serving, 6 snack servings, about 1 1/4 cups volume per serving

  • 1 1/2 cups zero-fat Greek yogurt, cold
  • 1 cup canned pumpkin puree, unsweetened
  • 1 scoop vanilla whey protein isolate powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground nutmeg
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground ginger
  • 1/8 teaspoon ground cloves
  • 1 teaspoon powdered erythritol or monk fruit sweetener
  • 1/4 teaspoon xanthan gum (optional, for thickness)
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

How To Do It

Step 1
Start by combining the zero-fat Greek yogurt and canned pumpkin puree in a large mixing bowl. Use a whisk or hand mixer to blend them together until smooth and creamy. This step is important because the pumpkin adds moisture and fiber, which bulk up the snack without adding many calories. Whisking well ensures that the texture is even and prevents clumps of pumpkin or yogurt. Avoid rushing this step; if you don’t mix thoroughly, you might end up with a lumpy texture that’s less enjoyable. The mixture should look uniform and thick but scoopable.
⏱ Time: 3 minutes
✓ Doneness: Smooth, creamy mixture with no lumps or streaks of pumpkin visible
Step 2
Add the vanilla whey protein isolate powder, ground cinnamon, nutmeg, ginger, cloves, erythritol, and vanilla extract to the pumpkin-yogurt base. Stir gently but thoroughly to combine. Protein powder hydrates best when mixed slowly into a moist base, so take your time to avoid clumps. The spices give the snack its signature cozy flavor, so be sure to measure carefully for balance. If you want a thicker texture, sprinkle in the xanthan gum last and whisk quickly to disperse it evenly; this will help prevent watery separation later without adding calories. Skipping the xanthan gum may cause the snack to be runnier, especially since there’s no fat to thicken it naturally.
⏱ Time: 4 minutes
✓ Doneness: Evenly mixed, thickened batter with no visible powder or spice clumps
Step 3
Once all ingredients are combined, chill the mixture in the refrigerator for at least 20 minutes. Chilling firms up the texture because cold temperatures help the protein and xanthan gum set. This step is key to achieving that creamy, spoonable consistency without adding fat. If you skip chilling, the snack will be thinner and less satisfying. You can prepare this ahead of time and store it covered in the fridge for up to 3 days, making it perfect for meal prep. When ready to eat, give it a quick stir to fluff it back up.
⏱ Time: 20 minutes
✓ Doneness: Snack is noticeably thicker, creamy, and holds its shape when scooped
Step 4
Taste the chilled snack and adjust sweetness or spice if needed. Sometimes the erythritol can dull flavors, so a little extra cinnamon or a splash of vanilla extract can brighten it up. Avoid adding too much sweetener to keep calories low and prevent aftertaste. Stir gently to incorporate any adjustments. This step personalizes the snack to your taste and ensures it’s enjoyable without extra calories.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Balanced flavor with warm spices and mild sweetness, no bitterness or chalkiness
Step 5
Serve the spiced pumpkin protein snack in generous bowls or jars. Because this recipe uses volume eating principles, the portions look large and filling, tricking your brain into feeling satisfied on fewer calories. Garnish with a sprinkle of cinnamon or a few fresh berries if you like, but keep extras minimal to maintain low calorie count. Enjoy immediately or pack it for a convenient on-the-go snack.
⏱ Time: 1 minute
✓ Doneness: Portion looks substantial and appetizing, with smooth creamy texture
Step 6
If you want to add a bit of texture, try mixing in a handful of air-popped popcorn or chopped celery right before serving. These low-calorie, high-volume ingredients add crunch without adding many calories. The contrast in texture makes the snack more interesting and satisfying. Just be careful not to add too much or the macros will change significantly.
⏱ Time: 1 minute
✓ Doneness: Crunchy bits evenly distributed without making the snack watery
Step 7
For a fluffier texture, you can gently fold in whipped egg whites (pasteurized for safety) before chilling. Whipping egg whites traps air, increasing volume and making the snack feel lighter. This adds protein without fat and helps prevent the dense, heavy mouthfeel that some low-fat snacks have. Be gentle folding to keep the air bubbles intact. This step is optional but highly recommended if you want a mousse-like experience.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Mixture is airy and light, with a fluffy texture after chilling
Step 8
Avoid using full-fat yogurt or adding oils as this will increase calories and reduce the volume eating benefit. Also, don’t rush mixing protein powder into cold yogurt as it can clump or taste chalky. Using whey isolate powder helps minimize chalkiness and keeps calories low. If you experience a gritty texture, try blending the mixture with a hand blender for a smoother finish.
⏱ Time: Varies
✓ Doneness: Smooth, creamy texture with no gritty or chalky mouthfeel
Step 9
Store any leftovers tightly covered in the fridge and consume within 3 days. The texture may firm up further but will remain creamy. Avoid freezing this snack, as the high water content and zero-fat dairy can cause ice crystals and separation, ruining the texture. When ready to eat leftovers, stir well to restore creaminess and enjoy chilled or at room temperature.
⏱ Time: Varies
✓ Doneness: Snack retains creamy texture and flavor after storage

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Skipping xanthan gum and ending up with a watery snack.

Why it happens People often assume yogurt alone will thicken the mixture, but zero-fat yogurt lacks fat to provide structure, so liquids separate easily.

Fix Add a small amount (1/4 teaspoon) of xanthan gum and whisk thoroughly to create a stable, creamy texture that holds up well.

Using full-fat yogurt which increases calories and reduces volume eating benefits.

Why it happens Full-fat yogurts are often mistaken as healthier or creamier options, but they add unnecessary calories.

Fix Stick to zero-fat Greek yogurt to keep calories low and maximize volume for satiety.

Mixing protein powder too quickly causing clumps and chalkiness.

Why it happens Protein powders hydrate slowly and can clump if stirred too fast or added all at once.

Fix Add powder gradually while whisking gently to ensure smooth, lump-free texture.

Pro Tips

Always mix protein powder into yogurt slowly and gently to avoid clumps.
Protein powders can clump when added too quickly to thick, cold dairy bases. Slowly folding it in while whisking ensures smooth hydration and prevents gritty texture. This is especially important in zero-fat recipes where there’s no fat to mask chalkiness.
Use a pinch of xanthan gum to thicken low-fat yogurt snacks without adding calories.
Xanthan gum thickens and stabilizes watery mixtures by binding water molecules. This mimics the creamy mouthfeel fat provides, preventing separation and improving texture in zero-fat dairy snacks.
Chill the snack for at least 20 minutes before serving to firm up texture.
Cold temperatures help protein and xanthan gum set, creating a creamy, spoonable consistency. Eating immediately after mixing often results in a runnier, less satisfying texture.

Variations

Chocolate Pumpkin Protein Snack

Swap the vanilla protein powder for chocolate flavor and add 1 tablespoon unsweetened cocoa powder. This gives a rich, dessert-like taste while keeping calories low and protein high. You might want to add a touch more sweetener to balance the cocoa’s bitterness. Expect a slightly denser texture but still creamy and satisfying.

Note: Cocoa powder can thicken the mixture slightly, so adjust liquid or xanthan gum accordingly to maintain creaminess.

Vegan Pumpkin Protein Snack

Use a plant-based protein isolate (pea or rice) and replace Greek yogurt with unsweetened soy or almond yogurt. The snack remains high protein but may have a slightly different texture and flavor. Add a bit more xanthan gum to help thicken since plant yogurts are often thinner.

Note: Plant proteins hydrate differently and can be grittier; blending well and chilling longer improves texture.

Pumpkin Spice with Egg White Fluff

Whip in pasteurized egg whites folded gently into the mixture before chilling. This adds volume and a mousse-like lightness, increasing portion size without extra calories. The texture becomes airy and less dense, perfect for those who want a dessert feel.

Note: Be gentle folding to keep air bubbles intact; overmixing deflates the fluffiness.

Storage & Tips

Room Temperature

This snack contains zero-fat dairy and pumpkin, so it should not be left at room temperature for more than 1 hour. Keep it covered to prevent drying out or contamination. Warm temperatures can cause separation or spoilage quickly.

Refrigerator

Store in an airtight container for up to 3 days. Chilling firms up texture and flavors meld nicely. The snack holds its volume well after chilling and tastes best cold or slightly chilled.

Freezer

Freezing is not recommended as the high water content causes icy texture and separation. If you must freeze, do so in an airtight container for up to 1 month and thaw slowly in the fridge, stirring well before eating.

Reheating

This snack is best enjoyed cold or at room temperature. Reheating is not advised as it can cause the dairy and xanthan gum to separate, ruining texture.

Make Ahead

Prepare the base mixture up to 2 days ahead and store covered in the fridge. Add any crunchy mix-ins or whipped egg whites the day of serving to maintain texture. Chilling before eating ensures optimal creaminess.

FAQ

Can I substitute the whey protein powder with plant-based protein?
Yes, you can use plant-based protein isolates like pea or rice protein. Keep in mind plant proteins may have a grittier texture and require more mixing or chilling time. Also, add a bit more xanthan gum if the mixture seems thin, as plant yogurts tend to be less creamy than Greek yogurt.
Why does my snack turn watery after mixing?
This usually happens when you skip adding xanthan gum or don’t mix it well. Xanthan gum thickens and stabilizes the mixture, preventing watery separation common in zero-fat dairy snacks. Also, make sure to chill the snack before eating to help it set.
Can I use full-fat yogurt instead of zero-fat?
While you can, full-fat yogurt will increase calories and reduce the volume eating benefit. Zero-fat Greek yogurt allows you to eat a larger, more filling portion for fewer calories, which is key for satiety and sticking to your diet.
How do I avoid the chalky taste of protein powder?
Use a high-quality whey protein isolate and mix it slowly into acidic dairy like Greek yogurt. Adding vanilla extract and sweetener also helps mask any chalkiness. Whisking thoroughly and chilling the mixture improves texture and flavor.
Is this snack suitable for meal prep?
Absolutely! You can prepare the snack base up to 2 days in advance and store it covered in the fridge. Add any crunchy toppings or whipped egg whites just before eating to keep texture fresh.
Can I eat this snack while cutting or on a low-calorie diet?
Yes, this snack is designed for cutting phases. It’s high in protein and low in calories, with a large volume that helps you feel full without overeating. It’s perfect for managing hunger and maintaining muscle mass.
What if I don’t have xanthan gum?
You can try guar gum or soaked chia seeds as alternatives, but they may alter the texture slightly. Another option is to strain your yogurt to thicken it before mixing. Just know the snack might be less creamy without xanthan gum.
How long does this snack keep in the fridge?
Stored in an airtight container, it stays fresh and tasty for up to 3 days. The texture might firm up a bit, but stirring before eating restores creaminess.
