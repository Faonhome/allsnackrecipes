Easy 10-Minute Creami Peach Recipe for a High-Protein Low-Calorie Snack

This Creami Peach snack is a creamy, refreshing treat that packs over 20g of protein with under 150 calories per serving. Using zero-fat Greek yogurt and a touch of peach puree, it’s perfect for a guilt-free afternoon boost or post-workout refuel.

Introduction

I’ve always struggled with feeling hungry on low-calorie days, especially when I’m cutting and need to keep protein high without sacrificing volume. Small snacks just didn’t cut it, leaving me craving more. That’s when I started experimenting with this Creami Peach recipe. It’s basically a giant bowl of fluffy, creamy peach goodness packed with over 20 grams of protein but under 150 calories. I love how it feels like a dessert but actually fills me up. The trick I discovered was adding a pinch of xanthan gum to thicken the zero-fat Greek yogurt and protein powder combo without making it chalky or watery. Plus, blending in fresh peaches and a little lemon juice brightens the flavor and keeps it refreshing. One time I tried it without the xanthan gum and it turned out too runny, so don’t skip that step! Also, I found a brand of Greek yogurt that doesn’t separate or get grainy, which makes a huge difference. Honestly, my friends keep asking how I manage to eat so much and still lose weight. This snack is the answer — big portions, low calories, and plenty of protein to keep me full.

What You'll Need

Approximately 1 large bowl per serving, 6 snack servings total, about 1.5 cups volume each

  • 2 cups zero-fat Greek yogurt, cold
  • 1 cup fresh peaches, peeled and diced
  • 1 scoop vanilla whey protein isolate powder
  • 1 tbsp powdered erythritol or preferred zero-calorie sweetener
  • 1/4 tsp xanthan gum (for creaminess and volume)
  • 1 tsp fresh lemon juice
  • Ice cubes (optional, for fluffiness)

How To Do It

Step 1
Start by peeling and dicing fresh peaches into small chunks. Fresh peaches add natural sweetness and fiber, which helps with volume eating. If peaches aren’t in season, you can use frozen but thaw and drain excess water to avoid diluting the mixture. The small pieces create texture and prevent the snack from being too uniform or boring. Avoid over-blending the peaches here to keep some bite.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Peach pieces are soft but still hold their shape, with no excess liquid pooling at the bottom.
Step 2
In a large mixing bowl, combine the cold zero-fat Greek yogurt and vanilla whey protein isolate powder. Use a whisk or electric mixer on low speed to blend them evenly. The protein powder hydrates better in cold yogurt than warm, preventing clumps and chalkiness. Mix just until smooth and creamy, avoiding over-beating which can cause separation. This step ensures your snack will be creamy and not gritty.
⏱ Time: 3 minutes
✓ Doneness: Mixture is smooth, thick, and homogeneous with no visible lumps or powder residue.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Skipping xanthan gum and ending up with a watery, unappealing texture.

Why it happens People often assume yogurt and protein powder alone will thicken enough, but without a binder, the mixture separates quickly and feels thin.

Fix Add a small amount (about 1/4 tsp) of xanthan gum while mixing to stabilize and thicken the snack. Whisk thoroughly to avoid clumps and get a creamy, voluminous texture.

Using full-fat yogurt unknowingly, increasing calories and changing texture.

Why it happens Labels can be confusing, and some yogurts labeled 'Greek style' are not zero-fat. Full-fat versions add unwanted calories and alter the lightness of the snack.

Fix Carefully check labels to select plain zero-fat Greek yogurt. This keeps calories low and allows the protein powder and xanthan gum to work effectively.

Adding too much sweetener, overpowering the fresh peach flavor.

Why it happens It’s tempting to add sweetness to mask protein powder bitterness, but overdoing it masks natural fruit notes and adds unnecessary sweetness.

Fix Start with a small amount of erythritol and adjust gradually, tasting as you go. The peaches and vanilla protein powder provide plenty of natural sweetness.

Pro Tips

Always use cold ingredients and mix protein powder into cold Greek yogurt slowly to prevent clumps.
Protein powders hydrate best in cold liquids and mixing slowly allows the powder to absorb moisture evenly, preventing chalky lumps. Overmixing or using warm ingredients can cause separation or grainy texture, which ruins the creamy mouthfeel essential for a satisfying low-calorie snack.
Add xanthan gum gradually and mix well to achieve perfect thickness without sliminess.
Xanthan gum thickens mixtures by binding water, but too much creates a gummy texture. Adding it slowly while whisking ensures even dispersion and a creamy, stable consistency that mimics fat without adding calories. This is key for volume eating snacks that rely on texture to satisfy.
Use fresh peaches peeled and diced to add natural sweetness and fiber for bulk.
Fresh peaches contribute water and fiber, which increase volume and promote fullness. Peeling removes extra texture that might interfere with smoothness, but leaving some skin can add antioxidants. Using fresh also avoids added sugars found in canned fruit, keeping calories low.

Variations

Tropical Mango Creami

Swap peaches for fresh mango chunks and add a splash of lime juice for a tropical twist. Mango adds a sweeter, slightly tangy flavor and similar fiber content. Macros remain close, with a slight increase in natural sugars. This variation feels like a mini tropical vacation in a bowl!

Note: Mango is softer than peach, so blend gently to avoid a too watery texture. You may need to slightly reduce added liquid or xanthan gum.

Berry Blast Creami

Use a mix of fresh or frozen berries like strawberries, blueberries, and raspberries instead of peaches. This lowers sugar slightly and adds antioxidant-rich flavor. The texture is a bit chunkier and more tart, perfect for berry lovers.

Note: Drain frozen berries well to prevent excess water. You might want to add a little extra erythritol to balance tartness.

Cinnamon Spice Creami Peach

Add a teaspoon of ground cinnamon and a dash of nutmeg to the classic recipe for a cozy flavor profile. Cinnamon can help regulate blood sugar and adds warmth without calories. This version is great for fall or winter snacking.

Note: Mix spices with protein powder before blending to ensure even distribution and avoid clumps.

Storage & Tips

Room Temperature

This snack should not be left at room temperature for more than 1 hour due to the zero-fat Greek yogurt and fresh fruit content. Keep covered and chilled until ready to eat to prevent separation and spoilage.

Refrigerator

Store in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days. The texture firms up slightly when chilled, which many find pleasant. Stir gently before eating to restore creaminess as some separation may occur.

Freezer

Freezing is not recommended as the high-water content in peaches and yogurt causes icy texture and loss of fluffiness. If frozen, thaw in the fridge and stir well but texture will be compromised.

Reheating

This snack is best enjoyed cold or at room temperature. Avoid reheating as heat can cause separation of yogurt and protein powder, ruining the texture.

Make Ahead

Prepare the yogurt-protein base and keep it chilled separately from diced peaches. Combine fresh peaches just before serving to preserve texture and volume. This method works great for meal prep and keeps the snack tasting fresh.

FAQ

Can I use flavored protein powder instead of vanilla?
Yes, but choose a flavor that complements peaches, like vanilla or mild fruit flavors. Strong flavors like chocolate may clash. Also, check the sweetener content in flavored powders as some add calories or aftertaste that might affect the snack.
What if I don’t have xanthan gum? Can I skip it?
You can skip it, but the texture will be thinner and more watery. The snack won’t hold its volume as well and may separate. Alternatively, a small amount of chia seeds soaked briefly can thicken, but this changes texture and appearance.
How do I avoid the protein powder tasting chalky?
Use cold Greek yogurt and mix protein powder slowly to hydrate it properly. Acidic yogurt helps mask chalkiness. Also, avoid overmixing which can cause separation and grainy texture. Using a high-quality whey isolate with good flavor is key.
Can I make this snack vegan or dairy-free?
This recipe relies on Greek yogurt and whey protein isolate, so it’s not vegan. For dairy-free, try plant-based protein powders and coconut or almond yogurt, but texture and flavor will differ significantly and may require additional thickeners.
Is this snack suitable for weight loss?
Absolutely! It’s high in protein, low in calories, and offers a large volume to help you feel full. The combination supports muscle maintenance and satiety, making it ideal for cutting or calorie-controlled diets.
Can I add nuts or seeds for crunch?
You can, but keep portions small (about 1 tsp) to avoid adding many calories. Nuts add healthy fats but increase calorie density, so track macros accordingly. For zero-calorie crunch, try adding small amounts of crisp vegetables like diced cucumber.
How long does this snack keep its volume after mixing?
It holds volume best immediately after mixing. Over time, especially if left at room temperature, the mixture may lose some fluffiness and separate. Stirring gently before eating helps restore texture.
What’s the best time to eat this snack?
It’s perfect post-workout for muscle recovery or as an afternoon snack to beat hunger without ruining dinner appetite. I personally love it around 3pm when I need a satisfying but light energy boost.
