Easy One Pot Recipes Without Tomatoes for High Protein Low Calorie Meals

Chef
Published on July 4, 2026
10 min Prep Time
6 Servings
Easy Difficulty
4.7 (34 ratings)✓ Thanks for rating!

If you’re tired of tomato-based dishes but want a quick, high-protein, low-calorie meal, these one pot recipes are a lifesaver. Packed with lean proteins and veggies, they fill you up without the guilt. Perfect for meal prep or a busy weeknight!

Introduction

I’ve always struggled with feeling hungry on low-calorie diets, especially when I’m cutting and want to keep my protein high. Tiny portions just don’t cut it for me—I need volume to feel satisfied. That’s why I love these one pot recipes without tomatoes. They’re super filling, packed with lean protein and veggies, and the best part? No weird ingredients or heavy sauces. I can eat a huge bowl and stay under 200 calories per serving. The trick I learned is to bulk up the dish with cooked quinoa and steamed veggies, which add volume without extra calories. Plus, folding in egg whites helps give the dish a fluffy texture without fat. I did have a batch once that turned out watery because I skipped draining the veggies properly, so watch out for that! These meals keep me full for hours and are perfect for meal prep Sundays or quick dinners when I’m wiped out. Honestly, my friends always ask how I eat so much and still lose weight. This is my secret weapon.

What You'll Need

Approximately 1 large bowl per serving, 6 servings total, about 2 cups volume per serving

  • 1 cup cooked quinoa, cooled
  • 1 cup cooked chicken breast, shredded
  • 1 cup steamed broccoli florets
  • 1/2 cup chopped bell peppers (red and yellow)
  • 1/2 cup chopped zucchini
  • 1/2 cup non-fat Greek yogurt
  • 2 large egg whites, lightly beaten
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 tsp olive oil (optional, for sautéing veggies)

How To Do It

Step 1
Start by steaming your broccoli and zucchini until just tender but still bright and firm. This keeps the veggies from getting mushy and losing volume. Overcooked veggies release water, which can make the final dish watery and less satisfying. Drain them well and set aside. Steaming also helps preserve the nutrients and natural sweetness, which adds flavor without calories.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Veggies are tender but still crisp, vibrant in color with no excess water pooling
Step 2
In a large non-stick pan, heat 1 tsp olive oil over medium heat and sauté the bell peppers until they soften slightly, about 3 minutes. This step brings out their natural sweetness and adds a subtle depth of flavor without adding many calories. If you prefer zero fat, you can dry sauté them in a non-stick pan but watch closely to avoid burning.
⏱ Time: 3 minutes
✓ Doneness: Bell peppers are softened but still hold their shape and color
Step 3
Add the cooked quinoa and shredded chicken breast to the pan with the sautéed peppers and steamed veggies. Stir gently to combine everything evenly. The quinoa adds bulk and fiber, making the meal more filling, while the chicken packs in lean protein to support muscle and satiety.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Ingredients are evenly mixed, with no clumps or dry spots
Step 4
In a separate bowl, whisk the egg whites with garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and pepper. Whisking incorporates air, which helps the egg whites add fluffiness to the final dish without extra fat. Seasoning here ensures every bite is flavorful, avoiding the blandness common in low-calorie meals.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Egg whites are frothy and slightly thickened
Step 5
Pour the egg white mixture over the quinoa and veggie blend in the pan. Stir continuously over low heat to gently cook the egg whites without scrambling. This technique binds everything together and adds a creamy texture while keeping calories low. Avoid high heat to prevent rubbery eggs.
⏱ Time: 4 minutes
✓ Doneness: Mixture thickens slightly and coats ingredients evenly without lumps
Step 6
Remove the pan from heat and fold in the non-fat Greek yogurt. The yogurt adds creaminess and a tangy note while boosting protein. Mixing it in off heat prevents curdling and keeps the texture smooth and luscious without adding fat.
⏱ Time: 1 minute
✓ Doneness: Mixture is creamy and smooth with no separation
Step 7
Taste and adjust seasoning with salt and pepper if needed. This final step is key because the low-fat ingredients can sometimes mute flavors, so don’t be shy to season well. Fresh cracked pepper adds a nice bite and aroma.
⏱ Time: 1 minute
✓ Doneness: Flavor is balanced and savory without blandness
Step 8
Divide the mixture into 6 portions for meal prep or immediate serving. Each portion is about 2 cups, which looks huge but stays under 200 calories thanks to the volume from veggies and quinoa. This makes it perfect for those times you want to eat a lot but keep calories in check.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Portions look generous and hold their shape in containers
Step 9
Store leftovers in airtight containers in the fridge and reheat gently in the microwave or on the stovetop with a splash of water or almond milk to keep moist. Avoid overheating to prevent drying out the egg whites and yogurt mixture.
⏱ Time: N/A
✓ Doneness: Reheated portions are warm and creamy without dryness

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Skipping draining veggies leading to watery texture.

Why it happens People often rush the process or underestimate how much water veggies release, thinking it won’t affect the dish.

Fix Always drain steamed or sautéed veggies thoroughly using a fine mesh strainer or paper towel before mixing. This keeps the final dish thick and prevents dilution of flavor.

Using full-fat yogurt instead of non-fat, increasing calories unexpectedly.

Why it happens Full-fat yogurt might be more available or preferred for taste, but it adds unwanted calories and fat in a low-calorie recipe.

Fix Check labels carefully and select 0% fat Greek yogurt to keep the recipe lean and protein-rich without extra calories.

Overcooking egg whites causing rubbery texture.

Why it happens High heat or prolonged cooking turns egg whites tough and unappetizing.

Fix Cook egg whites gently over low heat, stirring constantly until just set to maintain soft, fluffy texture.

Pro Tips

Drain steamed veggies thoroughly before mixing to avoid watery dishes.
Water released from steamed or sautéed vegetables can dilute the texture and flavor of low-fat, high-protein meals. Proper draining keeps the dish thick and satisfying, preventing separation or a soupy consistency.
Whisk egg whites until frothy before adding to the mix for extra fluffiness.
Incorporating air into egg whites creates volume and lightness without adding fat or calories. This technique improves texture and mouthfeel, making the snack feel more indulgent despite being low-calorie.
Add Greek yogurt off the heat to prevent curdling and maintain creaminess.
Heating yogurt too much causes it to separate and lose its smooth texture. Stirring it in after cooking keeps the mixture creamy and pleasant, enhancing overall eating experience.

Variations

Vegetarian Version with Edamame

Swap chicken for shelled edamame to keep protein high and add a fresh, nutty flavor. The texture will be slightly firmer but still satisfying. This variation raises fiber content and keeps calories similar.

Note: Edamame should be cooked and drained well. No change in cooking technique needed but flavor profile shifts toward plant-based.

Spicy Kick with Smoked Paprika and Chili Flakes

Add smoked paprika and chili flakes to the seasoning for a smoky, spicy twist. This adds flavor without calories and can help boost metabolism slightly. Expect a bolder taste and a warming sensation.

Note: Add spices with garlic and onion powder in the egg white mixture for even distribution.

Low-Carb Option with Cauliflower Rice

Replace quinoa with cauliflower rice to cut carbs further while maintaining volume. This lowers calories and carbs but keeps the dish filling and protein-rich. Texture will be lighter and less grainy.

Note: Use fresh or frozen cauliflower rice, sauté briefly to remove moisture before mixing.

Storage & Tips

Room Temperature

This snack should not be left out at room temperature for more than 2 hours due to the egg whites and dairy content. Keep covered to avoid contamination and moisture loss.

Refrigerator

Store in airtight containers for up to 4 days. The dish firms up when chilled but retains its volume well. Taste is often better cold or gently warmed with a splash of almond milk to refresh texture.

Freezer

Freeze portions for up to 1 month. Wrap tightly to prevent freezer burn. Thaw overnight in the fridge and reheat gently to avoid drying or separation of ingredients.

Reheating

Reheat in short bursts in the microwave or on low heat stovetop with a splash of unsweetened almond milk to maintain creaminess. Avoid high heat to prevent egg whites from becoming rubbery.

Make Ahead

Prepare the quinoa, chicken, and steamed veggies 1-2 days ahead and store separately. Combine with egg whites and yogurt just before serving or reheating to preserve fluffiness and avoid sogginess.

FAQ

Can I substitute protein powder for chicken in this recipe?
You can, but protein powder will change the texture and moisture balance. Use a high-quality whey isolate and add it gradually to avoid chalkiness. You may need to adjust liquid amounts and add a binder like xanthan gum to keep the dish creamy.
How can I avoid the dish becoming watery when using low-fat ingredients?
Drain all cooked veggies thoroughly and avoid adding excess liquids. Use thickening agents like egg whites or xanthan gum and add yogurt off heat to maintain texture. Cooking gently prevents water separation.
Is this recipe suitable for meal prep?
Absolutely! It stores well for up to 4 days in the fridge. Keep components like yogurt separate if possible and combine before eating to maintain freshness and texture.
Can I eat this snack while cutting calories?
Yes, it’s designed for that purpose. High protein and volume keep you full on fewer calories, making it ideal for a cutting phase or weight loss.
What’s the best way to reheat without drying out the egg whites?
Reheat gently on low heat or in short microwave bursts with a splash of unsweetened almond milk. This keeps the texture creamy and prevents rubberiness.
Can I use full-fat Greek yogurt instead of non-fat?
You can, but it will increase calories and fat content. Non-fat yogurt keeps the recipe lean while still providing creaminess.
How do I prevent protein powder from tasting chalky if I add it?
Use acidic dairy like Greek yogurt to balance chalkiness, add powder slowly, and mix thoroughly. Cold liquids help protein powder hydrate better, reducing chalkiness.
Can I make this recipe vegan?
You’d need to substitute chicken with plant protein like tofu or tempeh and replace egg whites with aquafaba or flax egg, but texture and protein content will differ.
