Easy No Cook Zucchini Protein Snack for Filling Low-Calorie Munching

Chef
Published on August 15, 2026
10 min Prep Time
6 Servings
Easy Difficulty
4.7 (29 ratings)✓ Thanks for rating!

This refreshing no cook zucchini snack is packed with protein and super low in calories. The secret? Adding creamy zero-fat Greek yogurt and crunchy cucumbers for volume without guilt. Perfect for those afternoon cravings or light meal prep days!

Introduction

I’ve always struggled with feeling hungry on low-calorie days, especially when I want to eat big portions without the extra calories. That’s why I love this no cook zucchini protein snack — it’s huge in volume but light on calories, so I can munch without guilt. Plus, it’s super quick to whip up, no stove needed! The trick is using zero-fat Greek yogurt mixed with unflavored whey isolate to add creamy protein without any fat. I also toss in fresh cucumber and dill for crunch and flavor, making it feel like a fresh dip-meets-salad bowl. I used to make versions that turned out watery or chalky, but adding a pinch of xanthan gum helps keep it thick and smooth. I usually eat this in the afternoon when I need a pick-me-up or pack it for meal prep — it fills me up for hours and tastes so fresh. Honestly, a friend once asked how I eat so much zucchini and still lose weight! This snack proves you can have volume, protein, and flavor all at once.

What You'll Need

Approximately 1 large bowl per serving, 6 snack servings total, about 2 cups volume per serving

  • 2 cups grated raw zucchini, drained
  • 1 cup zero-fat Greek yogurt
  • 1/2 cup diced cucumber
  • 1/4 cup finely chopped fresh dill
  • 1 scoop unflavored whey protein isolate powder
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Optional: pinch of xanthan gum to thicken

How To Do It

Step 1
Start by grating the zucchini and placing it in a fine mesh sieve or cheesecloth. Press or squeeze gently to remove excess water—this step is crucial to avoid a watery snack later. Zucchini holds a lot of moisture, so draining it well ensures your snack won’t be runny and that the flavors stay concentrated. If you skip this, you’ll end up with a soggy texture that dilutes the protein and yogurt mixture.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Zucchini should feel damp but not dripping wet; no pools of liquid in the bowl.
Step 2
In a mixing bowl, combine the zero-fat Greek yogurt with the whey protein isolate powder. Stir well until the powder fully dissolves—this prevents chalkiness and ensures a smooth creamy texture. Using unflavored isolate helps avoid aftertaste, but you can add lemon juice and garlic powder now to brighten the flavor. If you want extra thickness, sprinkle in a tiny pinch of xanthan gum and whisk quickly to avoid clumps. This step builds the protein-rich base that holds everything together.
⏱ Time: 3 minutes
✓ Doneness: Mixture should be thick, creamy, and smooth with no powder lumps.
Step 3
Add the drained zucchini and diced cucumber into the yogurt-protein mixture. Fold gently to combine without breaking down the veggies. This layering adds crunch and volume without calories, making the snack feel substantial. Mixing too vigorously can release more water from the veggies, so be gentle here.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Veggies are evenly coated but still retain their shape and crunch.
Step 4
Stir in the fresh dill, lemon juice, salt, and pepper. Dill adds a fresh herbaceous note that complements the zucchini perfectly. Season carefully, tasting as you go—since this snack is low calorie, the seasoning really makes the flavors pop without adding fat or sugar.
⏱ Time: 1 minute
✓ Doneness: A balanced, fresh flavor with a hint of brightness from the lemon and herbs.
Step 5
Chill the mixture in the fridge for at least 15 minutes before serving. This resting time helps the flavors meld and firms up the texture, especially important since there’s no fat to provide creaminess. If you serve it immediately, it may feel less cohesive and more watery.
⏱ Time: 15 minutes
✓ Doneness: Snack feels cool, thickened, and flavors are blended.
Step 6
Before serving, give the snack a gentle stir and adjust seasoning if needed. If the texture seems too thick after chilling, add a splash of cold water or unsweetened almond milk to loosen it slightly without adding calories.
⏱ Time: 1 minute
✓ Doneness: Creamy, scoopable texture that’s easy to eat but not runny.
Step 7
Portion the snack into bowls or containers for immediate eating or meal prep. This recipe makes about 6 servings, each roughly 2 cups in volume. The large portion size helps with satiety and makes it feel like a real snack, not just a tiny bite.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Evenly portioned servings, visually large and colorful.
Step 8
For variety, try topping with a sprinkle of paprika or chopped fresh chives before serving. These add a pop of color and extra flavor without calories. It’s a simple way to keep this snack interesting day after day.
⏱ Time: Optional, 1 minute
✓ Doneness: Attractive garnish that enhances visual appeal.
Step 9
Store any leftovers in an airtight container in the fridge. Because this snack contains fresh veggies and yogurt, consume within 3 days for best flavor and texture. Before eating leftovers, stir gently to restore creaminess and add a splash of almond milk if it’s thickened too much.
⏱ Time: N/A
✓ Doneness: Snack remains fresh, creamy, and flavorful after storage.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Skipping the zucchini draining step

Why it happens People often underestimate how watery zucchini is and assume grating is enough. They want to save time or don’t have the right tools.

Fix Use a fine mesh sieve or cheesecloth to press out excess water. Let it drain for several minutes and gently squeeze. This step is critical to avoid a watery, unappetizing snack.

Using full-fat yogurt instead of zero-fat

Why it happens Some think full-fat yogurt tastes better or is healthier. They don’t realize it adds unwanted calories and changes texture.

Fix Stick to zero-fat Greek yogurt for this recipe to keep calories low and maintain the intended thick, creamy texture without extra fat.

Adding too much protein powder and getting chalkiness

Why it happens Overloading protein powder without enough liquid or acid causes clumps and chalky flavor.

Fix Measure carefully and mix protein powder into yogurt thoroughly with lemon juice. Adding a bit of xanthan gum also helps smooth texture.

Pro Tips

Always drain zucchini thoroughly before mixing.
Zucchini contains a lot of water which can make your snack watery and dilute flavors. Draining prevents separation and keeps the texture creamy and thick, essential for a satisfying low-calorie snack.
Use unflavored whey isolate for the smoothest texture.
Isolate has fewer carbs and fats than concentrate, so it keeps calories low. It also dissolves better in cold mixtures, preventing chalkiness and giving a smooth mouthfeel without added flavors that clash with fresh veggies.
Add a pinch of xanthan gum to thicken without fat.
Xanthan gum mimics the creamy texture fat provides, stabilizing the mixture and preventing watery separation. Without it, low-fat snacks can feel thin and unsatisfying, so this tiny addition makes a huge difference.

Variations

Spicy No Cook Zucchini Snack

Add a pinch of cayenne pepper and chopped jalapeño for a spicy kick. This variation has the same macros but adds a warming flavor that’s great for those who love heat. The protein and volume remain high, but the spice makes it feel more like a savory dip.

Note: Adjust seasoning carefully and add spice gradually to avoid overpowering the delicate zucchini flavor.

Herb Medley Zucchini Bowl

Mix in fresh parsley, chives, and basil instead of dill for a bright herbal twist. This keeps it fresh and low calorie, while adding variety to the flavor profile. Protein and calories stay consistent, making it perfect for meal prep boredom busters.

Note: Use finely chopped herbs and add them after mixing the base to preserve their fresh flavor.

Cucumber Swap Zucchini Snack

Replace diced cucumber with shredded cucumber for a slightly different texture and even more hydration. This swap keeps the snack crunchy and refreshing with similar macros, great for hot days or post-workout refueling.

Note: Drain shredded cucumber well to avoid excess moisture.

Storage & Tips

Room Temperature

This snack should not be left at room temperature for more than 1 hour due to the fresh yogurt and veggies. Keep covered and refrigerated promptly to avoid spoilage and watery separation.

Refrigerator

Store in an airtight container for up to 3 days. The snack firms up when chilled and flavors meld nicely. Stir before serving to restore creaminess. It tastes best cold or at room temperature shortly after removing from fridge.

Freezer

Freezing is not recommended as the high water content causes icy texture and separation. Best to prepare fresh or store refrigerated.

Reheating

This snack is best eaten cold and should not be reheated. Warming causes separation and loss of texture.

Make Ahead

You can grate and drain zucchini 1 day ahead and store separately. Mix yogurt, protein, and seasonings the day of eating to keep texture fresh. Adding cucumbers last prevents sogginess. Perfect for meal prep Sundays.

FAQ

Can I substitute the whey protein isolate with plant-based protein?
Yes, but plant-based protein powders often have different textures and flavors that might affect creaminess and taste. Use unflavored pea or rice protein and mix thoroughly. You may need to adjust lemon juice or xanthan gum amounts to maintain texture.
What if I don’t have xanthan gum? Can I skip it?
You can skip xanthan gum but expect a thinner, less creamy texture that may separate after chilling. To compensate, drain zucchini very well and serve immediately after mixing for best results.
How do I prevent the protein powder from tasting chalky?
Mix protein powder thoroughly into the yogurt with lemon juice to help dissolve it. Using unflavored whey isolate helps, and chilling the mixture firms texture and reduces chalkiness perception.
Is this snack suitable for meal prep?
Absolutely! Store in airtight containers in the fridge and consume within 3 days. Add cucumbers fresh if you want to prevent sogginess. Stir before eating to restore texture.
Can I add sweeteners to this snack?
If you prefer a sweeter snack, add a small amount of stevia or monk fruit sweetener. Be cautious with quantity to avoid aftertaste. This recipe shines with savory notes, but a touch of sweetness can work.
When is the best time to eat this snack?
It’s great for an afternoon energy boost, post-workout refuel, or anytime you want a filling, low-calorie snack. The high protein and volume keep hunger away for hours.
How many calories and protein are in one serving?
Each serving contains approximately 140 calories and 22 grams of protein, making it a nutrient-dense snack that supports muscle maintenance and satiety.
Can I use full-fat Greek yogurt instead?
You can, but it will increase calories and fat content. The texture will be creamier but less suitable for low-calorie goals. For best results, stick to zero-fat versions.
