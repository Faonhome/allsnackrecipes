Easy No Bake Protein Fudge Bites Under 150 Calories Each

Cynthia Sullivan
Published on June 4, 2026
10 min Prep Time
6 Servings
Easy Difficulty

These fudge bites are a total game changer—super high in protein but light on calories, so you can snack guilt-free. They’re no bake, which means quick prep and a creamy texture thanks to zero-fat Greek yogurt. Perfect for a post-workout treat or when you need a sweet fix without wrecking your macros!

What You'll Need

Approximately 6 fudge bites, each about 2 tablespoons, totaling roughly 1/2 cup volume per serving

  • 1 cup zero-fat Greek yogurt, cold
  • 1/2 cup unflavored whey protein isolate powder
  • 2 tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1 tbsp sugar-free maple syrup
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/4 tsp xanthan gum
  • 1 tbsp water, cold
  • Pinch of salt

How To Do It

Step 1
Start by combining the cold zero-fat Greek yogurt with the whey protein isolate powder and unsweetened cocoa powder in a mixing bowl. Use a whisk or electric mixer on low speed to blend them together smoothly. This step is crucial because protein powder can clump easily in low-fat dairy. Whisking slowly while adding the powder helps it hydrate evenly, preventing that chalky texture. Watch for any lumps and break them up immediately to ensure a silky base for your fudge bites.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Mixture is smooth, thick, and free of lumps with a uniform chocolate color
Step 2
Add the sugar-free maple syrup, vanilla extract, and a pinch of salt to the bowl. These ingredients balance the bitterness of the cocoa and bring out a rich, fudge-like flavor without adding calories. Stir gently but thoroughly to incorporate. If you skip this step or rush it, your bites might taste flat or overly bitter. The salt especially enhances flavor complexity, so don’t omit it.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Mixture is glossy and evenly flavored with no pockets of syrup or vanilla
Step 3
In a small cup, mix the xanthan gum with cold water to create a slurry. This hydrocolloid acts as a binder and thickener, mimicking creaminess and preventing watery separation in this low-fat recipe. Slowly whisk the slurry into the yogurt mixture. Adding xanthan gum gradually helps you avoid clumps and ensures an even texture. Without it, the bites can be too loose or separate after chilling.
⏱ Time: 3 minutes
✓ Doneness: Mixture thickens noticeably and gains a slightly glossy, fudge-like sheen
Step 4
Once everything is combined, transfer the mixture into a silicone mold or line a small baking dish with parchment paper. Spread evenly to about 1-inch thickness. This helps the bites set uniformly and makes portioning easier. Avoid pressing too hard or making the layer too thin, as it will affect the final texture and firmness.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Mixture is evenly spread with smooth top surface and no air pockets
Step 5
Place the mold or dish in the refrigerator and chill for at least 2 hours. This chilling time is essential for the xanthan gum to fully hydrate and the protein to firm up, creating that perfect fudge consistency. If you skip chilling or rush it, the bites will be too soft and may fall apart when cut.
⏱ Time: 120 minutes
✓ Doneness: Firm to the touch and easily sliced into bite-sized pieces without crumbling
Step 6
After chilling, remove the fudge slab from the mold or dish and cut into 6 equal pieces. Use a sharp knife for clean edges. If the bites stick, dip the knife in warm water and wipe dry between cuts. This keeps the edges neat and prevents crumbling.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Cleanly cut, firm fudge bites with smooth edges
Step 7
Store the fudge bites in an airtight container in the refrigerator. They keep well for up to 4 days. The cold helps maintain their texture and flavor. If left out too long, they may soften or sweat due to the yogurt content.
⏱ Time: N/A
✓ Doneness: Bites remain firm and fresh tasting when refrigerated
Step 8
When ready to eat, let the fudge bites sit at room temperature for 5 minutes if you prefer a softer texture. This also brings out the chocolate flavor more. Avoid microwaving as heat can cause separation or a rubbery texture.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Slightly softened but still holds shape, with enhanced flavor
Step 9
For an extra touch, sprinkle a pinch of cocoa powder or a few freeze-dried raspberry bits on top before chilling. This adds a little texture and flavor contrast without adding calories. It also makes the bites look prettier for sharing or gifting.
⏱ Time: Optional, 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Evenly distributed garnish that sticks lightly to the surface

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Skipping xanthan gum and ending up with watery fudge bites.

Why it happens People often think they can omit thickening agents to keep things simple, but without xanthan gum, the protein-yogurt mixture won’t set properly and will separate after chilling.

Fix Always include xanthan gum and disperse it in cold water before adding. This ensures the mixture thickens evenly and the bites hold their shape after refrigeration.

Using a watery or low-quality zero-fat Greek yogurt.

Why it happens Not all zero-fat yogurts are created equal; some are too thin and cause the mixture to be runny, ruining texture.

Fix Choose a thick, strained zero-fat Greek yogurt brand. Test the consistency before making the recipe to ensure firmness.

Mixing protein powder too quickly causing lumps and chalkiness.

Why it happens Rushing the mixing process or adding powder all at once causes clumps that don’t hydrate properly.

Fix Slowly whisk protein powder into cold yogurt, breaking up lumps immediately to achieve a smooth base.

Cutting fudge bites before they’ve chilled enough.

Why it happens Impatience leads to soft, crumbly bites that fall apart when sliced.

Fix Refrigerate for at least 2 hours to let the mixture fully set before cutting.

Using full-fat yogurt or adding oils, increasing calories unintentionally.

Why it happens Some assume full-fat dairy adds richness without considering the calorie impact.

Fix Stick to zero-fat Greek yogurt and minimal fat additions to keep calories under control.

Pro Tips

Whisk protein powder slowly into cold yogurt to avoid clumps.
Protein powders tend to clump when mixed too quickly or with warm liquids. Whisking slowly while gradually adding powder allows it to hydrate evenly, producing a smooth, creamy texture. This prevents chalkiness and ensures the fudge bites have a pleasant mouthfeel.
Use xanthan gum sparingly and disperse it in cold water first.
Xanthan gum thickens mixtures by absorbing water, but if added dry directly, it can form lumps. Mixing it into cold water creates a slurry that disperses evenly when combined with yogurt. Using too much xanthan gum can make the texture gummy, so precise measurement is key.
Chill the fudge bites for at least 2 hours before cutting.
Chilling allows the protein and xanthan gum to fully set, giving the bites their firm, fudge-like texture. Cutting too early results in soft, crumbly pieces that don’t hold shape. Patience here ensures a professional-looking and satisfying snack.
Choose a thick zero-fat Greek yogurt brand to avoid watery bites.
Some zero-fat yogurts are runnier, which can cause watery fudge bites that separate after chilling. A thick yogurt provides structure and creaminess, improving texture and mouthfeel. Test brands beforehand to find one that holds up well in low-fat recipes.
Add a pinch of salt to balance cocoa bitterness.
Salt enhances chocolate flavor by reducing bitterness and rounding out the taste. Without it, the bites may taste flat or overly bitter, especially with unsweetened cocoa. This small addition makes a big difference in overall flavor.
Use sugar-free maple syrup to sweeten without calories.
Sugar-free syrups keep calories low while adding sweetness and moisture. They also help bind the mixture slightly, improving texture. Using regular syrup would increase calories and change the nutritional profile, defeating the purpose of a low-calorie snack.
Let fudge bites sit at room temperature for a few minutes before eating.
Allowing the bites to warm slightly softens them, enhancing their creamy texture and bringing out rich chocolate flavor. Eating them straight from the fridge can feel too firm or cold, which may mask some of the taste nuances.

Variations

Peanut Butter Protein Fudge Bites

Swap the cocoa powder for 2 tbsp of powdered peanut butter and add 1 tsp of natural peanut butter (use sparingly to keep calories low). This gives a nutty twist with a creamy texture. Expect slightly higher fat and calories but still under 180 per bite with 18g protein.

Note: Powdered peanut butter hydrates like protein powder, but adding natural peanut butter affects firmness. Chill longer if needed.

Berry Swirl Protein Fudge Bites

Fold in 2 tbsp of pureed frozen berries (like raspberries or blueberries) into the mixture before chilling. This adds natural sweetness, antioxidants, and a pretty swirl effect. Calories remain low, protein slightly diluted but still high.

Note: Extra moisture from berries may require slightly more xanthan gum for firm texture.

Mocha Protein Fudge Bites

Add 1 tsp instant coffee powder to the cocoa mix for a mocha flavor. It pairs beautifully with the chocolate and enhances the richness without extra calories. Protein and calorie counts stay similar.

Note: Dissolve coffee powder in a bit of hot water before mixing to avoid graininess.

Vanilla Almond Protein Fudge Bites

Replace cocoa powder with 1 tsp almond extract and add 1 tbsp finely chopped almonds on top before chilling. This gives a nutty, vanilla-flavored bite with a crunchy contrast. Slight calorie increase from almonds but still under 160 per bite.

Note: Chop almonds finely to avoid overpowering texture and sprinkle evenly.

Matcha Green Tea Protein Fudge Bites

Mix 1 tsp matcha powder into the yogurt base instead of cocoa for a fresh, earthy flavor and antioxidant boost. Calories and protein remain consistent. The color is vibrant and fun!

Note: Use high-quality matcha to avoid bitterness and sift powder to prevent lumps.

Storage & Tips

Room Temperature

These fudge bites should not be left at room temperature for more than 1 hour due to the yogurt base. Keep covered to avoid drying out or absorbing odors. Warm conditions can cause separation and loss of firmness.

Refrigerator

Store in an airtight container for up to 4 days. The bites maintain their texture well when chilled, staying firm and creamy. They taste best cold or slightly softened at room temperature for 5 minutes before eating.

Freezer

Freeze for up to 1 month by wrapping tightly in plastic wrap and placing in a freezer-safe container. Thaw overnight in the refrigerator. Texture may become slightly icy but still enjoyable. Avoid refreezing.

Reheating

Not recommended to heat fudge bites as warmth can cause separation and a rubbery texture. Instead, let them soften at room temperature for a few minutes to enhance creaminess.

Make Ahead

Prepare the mixture and pour into molds up to 2 days in advance, keeping covered in the refrigerator. Cut into bites just before serving to maintain neat edges and prevent sogginess.

FAQ

Can I use a different protein powder for these fudge bites?
Yes, but choose a whey protein isolate or a high-quality plant-based isolate with minimal fillers. Avoid blends with maltodextrin or added sugars to keep calories low. Some powders hydrate differently, so you might need to adjust xanthan gum slightly to maintain texture.
What if my fudge bites turn out too watery or don’t set?
This usually happens if the yogurt is too thin or you skipped xanthan gum. Use a thick zero-fat Greek yogurt and don’t omit the xanthan gum, which acts as a binder. Also, ensure you chill the bites for at least 2 hours to allow proper setting.
Can I make these fudge bites vegan or dairy-free?
It’s tricky because the recipe relies on zero-fat Greek yogurt and whey protein for texture and protein content. You could try plant-based yogurt and vegan protein powder, but expect changes in texture and flavor. Additional binders or thickeners might be needed.
How many fudge bites can I eat without going over calories?
Each bite is about 140 calories with 18g protein, so depending on your daily calorie goals, 1-2 bites make a great snack. Always track with your specific ingredients and brands to be precise.
Can I add nuts or nut butters to increase fat?
You can add small amounts, but be mindful that nuts and nut butters are calorie-dense and will raise the calorie count significantly. Adding more than 1 tsp of nut butter per batch may push bites over 180 calories each.
Why do my bites taste chalky sometimes?
Chalkiness often comes from protein powder not hydrating properly or using a low-quality powder. Mixing powder slowly into cold yogurt and choosing a smooth whey isolate helps. Also, the yogurt’s acidity helps mask chalkiness.
Can I use honey or maple syrup instead of sugar-free syrup?
You can, but these add more calories and sugar, which changes the nutrition profile. If you’re aiming for low-calorie snacks, sugar-free syrups are better to keep calories under control.
How long do these fudge bites keep in the fridge?
They stay fresh and firm for up to 4 days in an airtight container. After that, texture and flavor may start to degrade due to the yogurt base.
