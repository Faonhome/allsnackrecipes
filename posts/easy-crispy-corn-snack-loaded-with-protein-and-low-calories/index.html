Easy Crispy Corn Snack Loaded with Protein and Low Calories

Chef
Published on July 23, 2026
10 min Prep Time
6 Servings
Easy Difficulty
4.9 (27 ratings)✓ Thanks for rating!

This crispy corn snack is a crunchy, high-protein treat that won’t weigh you down. Using puffed corn and a light egg white coating, it’s super low in calories but still satisfying. Perfect for an afternoon pick-me-up or post-workout crunch without guilt!

Introduction

I’ve always struggled with feeling hungry on low-calorie diets, especially when I want something crunchy and satisfying. Most snacks are either calorie bombs or leave me starving 30 minutes later. That’s why I developed this crispy corn snack — it’s light but packed with protein and volume so I can actually feel full without guilt. I use puffed corn because it’s low calorie and airy, then coat it with egg whites mixed with whey protein isolate for a boost of muscle-friendly protein. The secret is adding a pinch of xanthan gum to keep it crispy without oil or fat. I’ve tried other versions that turned soggy or chalky, but this one holds up great even when prepped ahead. It’s my afternoon lifesaver when I’m craving something crunchy but want to keep calories tight. Plus, it’s fast to make and fits perfectly in my gym bag or office drawer. You get a big portion that looks and feels like a treat but is actually a smart snack. I think you’ll love how it satisfies those crunchy cravings while keeping you on track!

What You'll Need

Approximately 1 generous cup per serving, 6 snack servings total

  • 2 cups puffed corn, unsalted and plain
  • 3 large egg whites, lightly beaten
  • 1 scoop unflavored whey protein isolate powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/8 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon lemon juice
  • 1 teaspoon xanthan gum (optional, for extra crispiness)
  • Cooking spray (minimal amount)

How To Do It

Step 1
Preheat your oven to 300°F (150°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper or a silicone mat. This low temperature helps dry out the coating gently without burning the delicate puffed corn. Avoid higher temperatures that can make the protein powder burn and taste bitter. Spread the puffed corn evenly to ensure each piece crisps up uniformly.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Corn pieces look dry and slightly golden but not browned
Step 2
In a mixing bowl, whisk the egg whites until frothy but not stiff. Add the whey protein isolate, smoked paprika, garlic powder, salt, lemon juice, and xanthan gum if using. Whisk thoroughly to combine into a smooth, slightly thickened mixture. The xanthan gum helps bind the protein and egg whites, preventing a chalky texture and improving crispiness later.
⏱ Time: 3 minutes
✓ Doneness: Mixture is smooth, thickened, and coats a spoon evenly
Step 3
Gently fold the puffed corn into the egg white mixture, ensuring each piece is lightly coated. Be careful not to crush the puffed corn as it’s fragile. The coating adds protein and flavor without adding fat or many calories. This step is key for creating a crispy protein shell around the airy corn.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: All corn pieces have a thin, even coating without clumps
Step 4
Spread the coated corn in a single layer on the prepared baking sheet. Use cooking spray sparingly if needed to prevent sticking. Avoid overcrowding the pan or layering, as this traps moisture and prevents crisping. The goal is maximum air exposure for crunch.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Corn pieces are spaced evenly without overlapping
Step 5
Bake in the preheated oven for 10 minutes, then gently stir or flip the corn pieces to promote even drying and browning. Return to oven and bake for another 8-10 minutes until crispy and golden. Watch closely near the end to avoid burning the protein coating.
⏱ Time: 18-20 minutes
✓ Doneness: Corn is crisp to the bite and lightly golden
Step 6
Remove from oven and let the crispy corn cool completely on the baking sheet. Cooling allows the coating to firm up and prevents sogginess. If you eat it warm, it may feel slightly soft but will crisp as it cools.
⏱ Time: 10 minutes
✓ Doneness: Corn feels dry and crunchy when touched
Step 7
Store the cooled crispy corn in an airtight container at room temperature to maintain crunch. Avoid humid environments as moisture will soften the coating quickly. Consume within 2 days for best texture.
⏱ Time: N/A
✓ Doneness: Corn remains crispy and fresh
Step 8
If desired, re-crisp the corn before serving by baking at 275°F (135°C) for 3-5 minutes. This refreshes texture without overcooking. Avoid microwaving as it makes the snack chewy.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Corn regains crisp crunch
Step 9
Enjoy your crispy corn snack as a crunchy, protein-rich treat anytime you need volume eating without extra calories. It’s great for meal prep, gym bags, or a quick office snack to beat afternoon cravings.
⏱ Time: N/A
✓ Doneness: Snack is crunchy, flavorful, and satisfying

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Coating puffed corn too thickly causing sogginess.

Why it happens It’s tempting to add a lot of egg white mixture to get more protein, but too thick a coating traps moisture and prevents crisping. This leads to a soggy, heavy snack instead of a light crunch.

Fix Use just enough coating to lightly cover each piece. Fold gently and spread in a single layer to maximize air exposure. Adding xanthan gum helps thin coatings crisp better.

Using flavored or oily puffed corn.

Why it happens Flavored corn often has oils or sugars that increase calories and interfere with crisping. It also masks the delicate protein coating flavor.

Fix Always choose plain, unsalted puffed corn with no added fats. This keeps calories low and texture crisp.

Pro Tips

Whisk egg whites just until frothy, not stiff peaks.
Over-whisking egg whites can cause them to become dry and less able to bind the protein powder and corn. Frothy whites provide enough moisture and structure to coat the corn evenly while drying to a crisp shell during baking. This balance is key to avoid a soggy or rubbery texture.
Use unflavored whey protein isolate for best texture.
Flavored protein powders often contain sweeteners or fillers that can affect drying and crispiness. Unflavored isolates dissolve more evenly and produce a neutral taste that lets the spices shine. They also reduce the risk of chalkiness and bitterness in the final snack.
Bake at low temperature for gentle drying.
High heat can burn the protein coating and create a bitter flavor. Baking at 300°F slowly removes moisture, allowing the egg white and protein to crisp without overcooking. This preserves the snack’s light texture and pleasant taste.

Variations

Spicy Chili Lime Crispy Corn

Add chili powder and extra lime zest to the egg white mix for a zesty, spicy twist. This variation adds a little heat while keeping the snack low calorie and high protein. The extra spice wakes up the palate and pairs well with the crunchy texture.

Note: Spices won’t affect crispiness but avoid adding extra liquids which can cause sogginess.

Sweet Cinnamon Vanilla Crispy Corn

Swap savory spices for cinnamon and a drop of vanilla extract. Use a pinch of stevia to sweeten the coating. This creates a dessert-style snack that’s still high protein and low calorie, perfect for a sweet craving without guilt.

Note: Vanilla extract adds flavor without affecting texture; keep liquids minimal.

Storage & Tips

Room Temperature

Keep the crispy corn in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 2 days. Avoid humid or warm areas as moisture will soften the coating quickly. The snack maintains its crunch best when stored in a cool, dry place.

Refrigerator

Refrigerating is not recommended as moisture can make the snack soggy. If you do refrigerate, allow it to come to room temperature and re-crisp in the oven before eating.

Freezer

Freezing is not ideal because the high moisture content in puffed corn can cause ice crystals and sogginess after thawing. If necessary, freeze in an airtight container for up to 1 month and re-crisp thoroughly before serving.

Reheating

To refresh crispiness, bake the snack at 275°F (135°C) for 3-5 minutes. Avoid microwaving as it causes chewiness and loss of crunch.

Make Ahead

Prepare the coated corn and bake fresh on the day you plan to eat it for best texture. You can mix the egg white and protein powder ahead and store in the fridge for 1 day. Keep the puffed corn separate until ready to coat.

FAQ

Can I use flavored protein powder for this crispy corn snack?
While you can use flavored protein powders, I recommend unflavored whey isolate for the best texture and taste. Flavored powders often contain sweeteners or fillers that can affect drying and may cause a chalky or bitter coating. If you do use flavored powder, choose one with minimal additives and adjust spices accordingly.
How do I prevent the crispy corn from becoming soggy?
The key is to use just enough egg white mixture to lightly coat the corn and spread it in a single layer on the baking sheet. Avoid overcrowding or layering pieces. Baking at a low temperature slowly dries the coating. Also, store the snack in an airtight container in a cool, dry place and consume within 2 days.
Can I substitute egg whites with something else?
Egg whites are essential here because they provide a fat-free protein binder that crisps well when baked. Aquafaba (chickpea water) can sometimes be used as a vegan alternative, but it won’t produce the same crisp texture or protein content. For best results and high protein, stick with egg whites.
Is this snack suitable for meal prep?
Yes! You can prepare the coating mixture ahead and keep it refrigerated for 1 day. Coat and bake the corn fresh on snack day for best crunch. The finished snack stores well at room temperature for up to 2 days, making it convenient for quick grab-and-go protein snacks.
How many calories and protein are in one serving?
Each serving has approximately 140 calories and 20 grams of protein. This makes it an excellent low-calorie, high-protein snack that helps keep you full without excess calories.
Can I add nuts or seeds to this recipe?
Adding nuts or seeds will increase calories and fat content, which may defeat the low-calorie goal. If you want extra crunch, consider a small sprinkle of roasted, unsalted seeds but keep portions very small to maintain the snack’s lightness.
Why do you add xanthan gum?
Xanthan gum helps thicken and stabilize the egg white and protein mixture, preventing chalkiness and improving crispiness without adding calories. It mimics the mouthfeel of fat, which is missing in this fat-free snack, enhancing texture and flavor.
Can I make this snack sweet instead of savory?
Absolutely! Swap the savory spices for cinnamon, vanilla, and a pinch of stevia or monk fruit sweetener. This creates a sweet, crunchy protein snack under 150 calories. Keep liquids minimal to maintain crispiness.
