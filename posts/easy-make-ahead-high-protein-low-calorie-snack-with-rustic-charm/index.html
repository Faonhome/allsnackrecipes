Easy Make-Ahead High-Protein Low-Calorie Snack with Rustic Charm

Chef
Published on June 6, 2026
10 min Prep Time
6 Servings
Easy Difficulty

This snack is a vibrant, protein-packed delight served on a rustic wooden board that makes it feel homemade and satisfying. It’s low in calories but high in protein, perfect for keeping hunger away without the guilt. Great for meal prep or an afternoon pick-me-up!

What You'll Need

Approximately 6 snack servings, each about 1 cup volume, perfect for sharing on a rustic board

  • 1 cup zero-fat Greek yogurt, chilled
  • 3/4 cup low-fat cottage cheese
  • 1/2 cup diced cucumber, fresh
  • 1/2 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/4 cup sliced bell peppers, assorted colors
  • 2 tbsp chopped fresh parsley
  • 1 tsp lemon juice, fresh
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • Stevia or monk fruit sweetener, to taste
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

How To Do It

Step 1
Start by chilling your zero-fat Greek yogurt and low-fat cottage cheese in the fridge for at least an hour. This step is crucial because cold dairy holds its structure better and keeps the final snack creamy without separating. When you mix chilled dairy, it traps air and feels thicker, giving you that satisfying volume without adding calories. Avoid using room-temperature dairy here, as it can make the mixture watery and less appetizing.
⏱ Time: 60 minutes
✓ Doneness: The dairy should be cold and firm to the touch but scoopable
Step 2
In a mixing bowl, combine 1 cup of chilled Greek yogurt with 3/4 cup cottage cheese. Stir gently to keep the cottage cheese curds intact for texture. This combination boosts protein while maintaining a creamy mouthfeel. If you stir too vigorously, you risk breaking down the curds, which changes the texture to overly smooth and less interesting.
⏱ Time: 3 minutes
✓ Doneness: Mixture looks creamy with visible curds, not overly smooth or runny
Step 3
Add 1/2 cup diced cucumber, 1/2 cup halved cherry tomatoes, and 1/4 cup sliced bell peppers to the dairy mix. These vegetables add crunch and volume without many calories, helping you eat a bigger portion that fills you up. Make sure to pat the veggies dry if they’re wet to avoid watering down the mixture.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Veggies are evenly distributed and mixture looks chunky but not watery
Step 4
Sprinkle in 2 tablespoons of chopped fresh parsley for a fresh herbal note. Parsley also adds fiber and a pop of green color that makes the snack visually appealing on the rustic board. Chop parsley finely to avoid large leafy bits that might overpower the texture.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Parsley is evenly mixed in and the color looks vibrant
Step 5
Add 1 teaspoon fresh lemon juice and 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder to the bowl. These ingredients brighten the flavor and add savory depth without any fat or sugar. Lemon juice also helps prevent the dairy from tasting bland, which can happen in low-fat snacks. Stir gently to combine without breaking the cottage cheese curds.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Mixture smells fresh and tangy with a hint of garlic
Step 6
Sweeten lightly with stevia or monk fruit sweetener to taste. Since this snack is savory with a touch of tang, just a little sweetness balances the flavors and cuts any dairy tartness. Add small amounts at a time and taste as you go to avoid an artificial aftertaste.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Balanced flavor with no bitterness or overpowering sweetness
Step 7
Season with salt and freshly ground black pepper to your liking. Salt is important for enhancing the natural flavors of the dairy and veggies, but keep it moderate to stay low sodium. Pepper adds a subtle kick that complements the garlic and lemon nicely.
⏱ Time: 1 minute
✓ Doneness: Taste is well-rounded with savory notes and no blandness
Step 8
Transfer the mixture to a rustic wooden board or a large shallow bowl for serving. The presentation on wood adds charm and makes the snack feel more substantial and inviting. Arrange extra veggie sticks or fresh herbs around the bowl for a colorful, appetizing look.
⏱ Time: 3 minutes
✓ Doneness: Snack looks fresh, colorful, and generous in portion size
Step 9
Serve immediately or cover and refrigerate for up to 2 days. Chilling allows the flavors to meld and the texture to firm up slightly, making it even more satisfying. When ready to eat, give it a gentle stir and enjoy a big, filling snack that’s high in protein but low in calories.
⏱ Time: 0 minutes
✓ Doneness: Snack tastes fresh and creamy with a nice balance of textures

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Using room-temperature dairy causing watery texture.

Why it happens People often skip chilling to save time, not realizing cold dairy holds structure better.

Fix Chill Greek yogurt and cottage cheese for at least an hour before mixing to keep the snack creamy and thick.

Adding too much sweetener leading to bitter aftertaste.

Why it happens Trying to mask dairy tang with excess stevia or monk fruit can backfire.

Fix Add sweetener gradually and taste frequently to find the perfect balance without bitterness.

Overmixing cottage cheese curds breaking texture.

Why it happens Vigorous stirring can break down curds, making the snack overly smooth and less interesting.

Fix Stir gently just to combine, preserving curds for texture contrast.

Pro Tips

Always chill your dairy ingredients before mixing to keep the texture thick and creamy.
Cold dairy traps air better and prevents watery separation common in fat-free mixtures. This creates a satisfying mouthfeel and volume without adding calories, crucial for low-fat protein snacks.
Pat your vegetables dry before adding to the mix to avoid diluting the creamy base.
Excess water from veggies can thin the mixture, making it less creamy and more watery. Drying them ensures the snack maintains its rich texture and flavor balance.
Add lemon juice last and stir gently to brighten flavor and stabilize texture.
Acidic lemon juice enhances flavor and helps proteins maintain structure, preventing chalkiness or separation in low-fat dairy blends.

Variations

Berry-Infused Protein Snack

Swap the veggies for fresh berries like blueberries and raspberries to add natural sweetness and antioxidants. This variation still packs high protein but has a fruity twist. Expect a slightly smoother texture and a refreshing, tangy flavor profile.

Note: Berries release juice, so drain excess liquid before mixing to avoid thinning the dairy base.

Savory Herb and Cucumber Mix

Add fresh dill and chives with diced cucumber and a pinch of smoked paprika. This savory version is great for those who prefer herbaceous flavors and pairs well with whole-grain crackers. Protein stays high, calories remain low.

Note: Use fresh herbs finely chopped and add paprika sparingly to avoid overpowering the creamy base.

Storage & Tips

Room Temperature

This snack should not be left at room temperature for more than 1 hour due to the dairy content. Keep it covered to prevent drying out and contamination. Water separation may occur if left warm, which affects texture.

Refrigerator

Store in an airtight container for up to 2 days. Chilling firms the texture and melds flavors nicely. The snack holds its volume well when cold and tastes best served chilled or at cool room temperature.

Freezer

Freezing is not recommended as the high-water content dairy and veggies become icy and lose creamy texture upon thawing.

Reheating

This snack is best enjoyed cold. Reheating can cause separation and loss of creaminess, so avoid warming it.

Make Ahead

Prepare the dairy mixture and chop veggies 1-2 days ahead, storing separately. Combine and season just before serving to maintain freshness and texture. Perfect for meal prep Sundays.

FAQ

Can I substitute the Greek yogurt with another protein source?
You can substitute with zero-fat skyr or strained non-fat yogurt, which have similar protein content and texture. Avoid regular yogurt as it’s thinner and may water down the snack. Adjust seasoning and chilling time accordingly for best results.
How do I prevent the snack from tasting chalky due to protein powder?
If adding protein powder, choose a high-quality whey isolate and blend it with acidic dairy like Greek yogurt. Chilling and stirring gently also help prevent chalkiness. Using small amounts and balancing flavors with lemon juice reduces any off-putting texture.
Is this snack suitable for cutting or weight loss phases?
Absolutely! It’s high in protein to support muscle retention and low in calories to help maintain a deficit. The volume and fiber-rich veggies keep you full longer, making it ideal for cutting phases.
Can I make this snack vegan?
This recipe relies on dairy proteins for texture and flavor, so vegan substitutions are tricky. You might try silken tofu blended with plant-based yogurt, but texture and protein content will differ significantly.
How should I count the calories accurately?
Use a kitchen scale to weigh your ingredients and input exact brands into your tracker app. Pay attention to the fat content in dairy and any added sweeteners or seasoning to keep counts precise.
What’s the best time to eat this snack?
It works great post-workout to replenish protein or during an afternoon slump for sustained energy. It’s also perfect as a meal prep snack to keep you full between meals.
Can I add nuts or seeds for extra crunch?
You can, but be mindful of calories as nuts and seeds are calorie-dense. Add small amounts (like a teaspoon) to keep it low-calorie and still high-protein.
Why does the mixture sometimes separate or get watery?
This usually happens if the dairy isn’t chilled or if veggies release too much water. Pat veggies dry and keep dairy cold to maintain creamy texture.
