Easy Air Fryer Peaches: A High-Protein Low-Calorie Snack You’ll Love

Chef
Published on July 26, 2026
10 min Prep Time
8 min Time
6 Servings
Easy Difficulty
4.7 (60 ratings)✓ Thanks for rating!

These air fryer peaches are a juicy, warm snack that’s surprisingly high in protein thanks to a dollop of zero-fat Greek yogurt and a sprinkle of protein powder. Low calorie and perfect for a quick afternoon boost or post-workout treat!

Introduction

I’ve always struggled with feeling hungry on low-calorie days, especially when I’m trying to cut but don’t want to resort to tiny, unsatisfying snacks. That’s why I fell in love with this air fryer peaches recipe—it's warm, juicy, and packs a protein punch without packing on calories. The trick is pairing the naturally sweet peaches with a thickened zero-fat Greek yogurt blended with protein powder and a touch of xanthan gum to keep it creamy and luscious. I used to make the mistake of just spooning plain yogurt on top, but it would get watery and separate, which was a bummer. Now, with the xanthan gum and cold yogurt, it stays thick and velvety. Plus, air frying the peaches caramelizes their sugars just enough to feel indulgent but still fresh. I usually whip this up in 10 minutes when I need a mid-afternoon energy boost or after a gym session. The portion looks huge, so it really tricks your brain into feeling like you’re eating a lot, but it’s only about 140 calories and over 20 grams of protein. A friend even asked me how I eat so much and still lose weight! It’s perfect for anyone who wants a satisfying snack that’s both volume eating and macro-friendly.

What You'll Need

Approximately 1 peach half with yogurt topping per serving, 6 snack servings total

  • 3 large ripe peaches, halved and pitted
  • 1 cup zero-fat Greek yogurt, cold
  • 2 scoops vanilla whey isolate protein powder
  • 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 1 teaspoon powdered erythritol (optional, for sweetness)
  • 1 teaspoon lemon juice
  • 1/2 teaspoon xanthan gum (to thicken yogurt mixture)
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

How To Do It

Step 1
Preheat your air fryer to 375°F (190°C). Meanwhile, prepare the peaches by washing them well, then cutting them in half and removing the pits. Lightly brush the cut sides with lemon juice to prevent browning and add a slight tang that balances the peach's sweetness. This step is important because air frying caramelizes the natural sugars, so the lemon juice keeps the flavor bright and prevents them from turning mushy or overly sweet. Avoid skipping this or your peaches might brown unevenly or taste flat after cooking.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Peach halves are slightly golden and caramelized around the edges but still hold their shape
Step 2
Place the peach halves cut side up in the air fryer basket, making sure they’re not overcrowded to allow even air circulation. Sprinkle each half with cinnamon and erythritol if using, which adds warmth and sweetness without calories. Air fry for 7-8 minutes until peaches are tender but not mushy. The timing is key here; too short and the peaches won’t caramelize, too long and they’ll get watery. You want a soft, warm peach with a bit of a crusty edge for texture contrast.
⏱ Time: 8 minutes
✓ Doneness: Peaches are tender when pierced with a fork and have a slightly browned surface
Step 3
While the peaches cook, prepare the protein yogurt topping. In a chilled bowl, combine the zero-fat Greek yogurt with vanilla whey isolate protein powder, vanilla extract, and xanthan gum. Whisk vigorously or use a handheld mixer to fully incorporate the xanthan gum, which thickens the mixture without adding calories or fat. This step is crucial to avoid a watery topping that separates quickly. The protein powder hydrates better in cold yogurt, so keep ingredients chilled before mixing.
⏱ Time: 3 minutes
✓ Doneness: Yogurt mixture is smooth, thick, and creamy with no lumps or watery separation
Step 4
Taste the yogurt mixture and adjust sweetness if needed by adding a pinch more erythritol or stevia. Be cautious with sweeteners as too much can cause an aftertaste. The goal is to complement the natural peach sweetness, not overpower it. Mix again briefly to blend any additions.
⏱ Time: 1 minute
✓ Doneness: Balanced sweetness that enhances but does not mask the peach flavor
Step 5
Once peaches are done, carefully transfer them to serving bowls or plates. They will be hot and juicy, so handle gently to keep the halves intact. The caramelized surface adds a lovely texture contrast to the creamy topping.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Peaches remain in half shape with a glossy, caramelized exterior
Step 6
Spoon a generous dollop of the protein yogurt mixture over each peach half. The thickened yogurt clings nicely, creating a creamy layer that adds protein and richness without fat. This combination makes the snack filling and satisfying despite the low calorie count.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Yogurt topping covers peaches evenly and holds shape without running off
Step 7
Optional: sprinkle a dash of cinnamon or a few chopped fresh mint leaves on top for extra aroma and freshness. This adds a nice visual pop and enhances the flavor complexity without adding calories.
⏱ Time: 1 minute
✓ Doneness: Garnish is evenly distributed and visually appealing
Step 8
Serve immediately while peaches are warm and yogurt is cool. The temperature contrast makes the snack more enjoyable and satisfying. If you wait too long, the yogurt may start to warm and lose creaminess, so enjoy right away for best texture.
⏱ Time: Serve immediately
✓ Doneness: Warm peaches with cool, creamy yogurt topping
Step 9
For meal prep, cool the peaches to room temperature before storing in airtight containers with the yogurt topping separate. Combine and serve chilled or briefly warmed in the microwave (10-15 seconds) just before eating. Avoid reheating yogurt topping directly to prevent separation.
⏱ Time: Variable
✓ Doneness: Snack maintains texture and flavor after reheating peach halves only

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Skipping the xanthan gum and ending up with watery, separated yogurt topping.

Why it happens People often assume Greek yogurt alone will stay thick, but when mixed with protein powder and liquid, it can separate quickly without a stabilizer.

Fix Add a small amount (about 1/2 teaspoon per cup) of xanthan gum and whisk thoroughly to ensure a creamy, stable topping that holds together when served.

Using full-fat or flavored yogurt that increases calories and alters flavor.

Why it happens Many assume any Greek yogurt will work, but flavored or full-fat varieties add hidden sugars and fats, increasing calorie count and masking the delicate peach flavor.

Fix Choose plain, zero-fat Greek yogurt to keep calories low and allow the peaches’ natural sweetness to shine.

Overcooking peaches in the air fryer until mushy or dry.

Why it happens Rushing the process or setting too high a temperature can cause peaches to lose their shape and become watery or tough.

Fix Air fry at 375°F for 7-8 minutes, checking tenderness with a fork. You want soft but still intact halves with caramelized edges.

Pro Tips

Always chill your Greek yogurt and protein powder mixture before combining to prevent chalkiness.
Cold yogurt helps the protein powder hydrate smoothly and reduces the chalky texture that often happens with room temperature or warm liquids. This keeps the topping creamy and pleasant to eat, especially important in low-fat, low-calorie snacks where fat isn’t masking texture.
Brush peaches with lemon juice before air frying to keep them vibrant and prevent browning.
The acid in lemon juice slows enzymatic browning and balances the caramelization process, resulting in peaches that look fresh and taste bright rather than dull or overly sweet. This simple step improves both appearance and flavor.
Use xanthan gum sparingly to thicken yogurt without adding calories or heaviness.
Xanthan gum creates a creamy mouthfeel by binding water and stabilizing mixtures. Too much can make the topping gummy or slimy, so precise measurement is key. It’s a great fat substitute in low-calorie snacks to maintain texture.

Variations

Berry Protein Topping Swap

Swap the vanilla whey protein powder for a berry-flavored isolate and add fresh blueberries or raspberries on top. This adds antioxidants and a tangy twist to the snack. The macros stay similar, but the flavor profile brightens, making it perfect for summer snacking.

Note: Berry protein powders may have added sweeteners; adjust erythritol accordingly. Fresh berries add volume and fiber but watch for extra carbs.

Egg White Meringue Topping

Replace the yogurt topping with whipped egg whites sweetened lightly with stevia and folded with protein powder for a fluffy, airy texture. This variation is even lower in fat and calories but has a different mouthfeel—light and cloud-like rather than creamy.

Note: Whip egg whites to stiff peaks and fold protein powder gently to avoid deflating. Serve immediately for best texture.

Cinnamon Spice Pumpkin Twist

Add 2 tablespoons of canned pumpkin puree and pumpkin pie spice to the yogurt-protein mixture for a seasonal flavor boost. This adds fiber and volume with minimal calories, creating a cozy fall snack.

Note: Pumpkin puree thins the topping slightly; increase xanthan gum by 1/4 teaspoon to maintain thickness.

Storage & Tips

Room Temperature

Because this snack contains zero-fat Greek yogurt and air-fried fruit, it’s best consumed immediately or kept chilled. At room temperature, the yogurt topping may separate and the peaches can become warm and less appealing. Avoid leaving out for more than 1 hour.

Refrigerator

Store air-fried peaches and protein yogurt separately in airtight containers for up to 3 days. The peaches remain juicy but lose their warm caramelized texture. The yogurt topping firms up when cold but can be stirred before serving. Combine just before eating for best experience.

Freezer

Freezing is not recommended as the yogurt topping will separate and peaches become watery upon thawing. If needed, freeze peaches alone for up to 1 month and thaw overnight in fridge.

Reheating

Reheat peaches gently in the microwave for 10-15 seconds to warm without drying. Do not reheat yogurt topping as it will separate and lose creaminess. Add fresh yogurt topping after warming peaches.

Make Ahead

Air fry peaches up to 2 days ahead and store chilled. Prepare protein yogurt topping fresh or up to 1 day in advance. Assemble just before eating to keep textures optimal. This method works great for meal prep and busy snack times.

FAQ

Can I use any protein powder for the yogurt topping?
You can, but I recommend whey isolate for the best texture and lowest calories. Plant-based powders often have grittier textures and may not blend as smoothly with yogurt. Also, watch for added sugars or fillers that increase calories. Mixing protein powder into cold yogurt helps prevent chalkiness.
How do I prevent the yogurt topping from becoming watery?
Adding a small amount of xanthan gum stabilizes the mixture and prevents separation. Also, use zero-fat Greek yogurt which is thicker, and keep the mixture cold before serving. Whisk or blend well to fully incorporate ingredients for a creamy, stable topping.
Can I substitute air frying with baking or grilling peaches?
Yes, baking or grilling peaches works well but air frying caramelizes them faster and with less moisture loss. If baking, use 375°F for about 10-12 minutes and watch closely to avoid mushiness. Grilling adds a smoky flavor but can dry peaches out if overcooked.
Is this snack suitable for meal prep?
Absolutely! Store peaches and yogurt topping separately in airtight containers in the fridge. Combine just before eating to maintain texture and flavor. This snack keeps well for up to 3 days refrigerated and is perfect for quick grab-and-go protein boosts.
Can I eat this snack while cutting or on a calorie deficit?
Yes, it’s designed for that! With around 140 calories and 22 grams of protein per serving, it’s filling and supports muscle maintenance while keeping calories low. The volume from peaches and thick yogurt helps curb hunger without guilt.
What if I don’t have xanthan gum? Can I skip it?
You can skip it, but the yogurt topping will be thinner and may separate faster. Alternatives like guar gum or chia seeds can work but may alter texture or flavor. If you skip stabilizers, serve the snack immediately for best results.
How do I avoid the protein powder tasting chalky?
Mix the protein powder into cold, acidic yogurt and whisk thoroughly. The acidity and cold temperature reduce chalkiness. Also, choose a high-quality vanilla or flavored whey isolate with a fine texture. Avoid mixing protein powder into warm or room temperature liquids.
Can I add nuts or seeds for crunch?
You can add a small sprinkle of chopped nuts or seeds for texture, but be mindful they add calories and fat. Keep portions small (1 tsp) to maintain the low-calorie profile. Alternatively, cinnamon or spices add flavor without calories.
