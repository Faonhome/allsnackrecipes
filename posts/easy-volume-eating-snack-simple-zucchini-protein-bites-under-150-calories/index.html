Easy Volume Eating Snack: Simple Zucchini Protein Bites Under 150 Calories

Published on July 18, 2026
10 min Prep Time

These zucchini protein bites are a perfect low-calorie snack that fills you up without guilt. Using shredded zucchini for volume and egg whites for protein, they’re light but satisfy hunger, ideal for afternoon cravings or post-workout fuel.

I’ve always struggled with feeling hungry on low-calorie days, especially when cutting. Tiny snacks just don’t cut it, leaving me reaching for something that ruins my macros. You’ll love how much volume you get without the guilt.

What You'll Need

Approximately 6 protein bites, about 1/2 cup volume each

  • 1 medium zucchini, shredded and squeezed dry
  • 4 large egg whites
  • 1/4 cup zero-fat Greek yogurt
  • 2 tbsp unflavored whey protein isolate powder
  • 1 tsp xanthan gum (optional, for better texture)
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • Freshly ground black pepper to taste

How To Do It

Step 1
Start by shredding the zucchini using a box grater, then place the shredded zucchini in a clean kitchen towel or cheesecloth and squeeze out as much moisture as possible. This step is crucial because zucchini contains a lot of water that can make your bites soggy and watery. Removing excess water ensures your bites hold together well and have a pleasant texture. If you skip this, your snack will be watery and won’t firm up properly. Aim for the zucchini to be damp but not dripping.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Zucchini feels dry to the touch and no liquid pools when pressed
Step 2
In a mixing bowl, whisk together the egg whites, zero-fat Greek yogurt, and whey protein isolate powder until smooth. The protein powder hydrates in this mixture, thickening it and adding creaminess without fat. Whisking well prevents clumps and chalkiness. If you rush or don’t mix thoroughly, you might get a gritty texture. The yogurt adds a subtle tang and helps mask any protein powder aftertaste.
⏱ Time: 3 minutes
✓ Doneness: Mixture is smooth, creamy, and slightly thickened without lumps
Step 3
Add the squeezed zucchini to the protein mixture along with garlic powder, salt, and black pepper. Stir gently to combine all ingredients evenly. Mixing thoroughly is important to distribute flavors and ensure each bite has consistent texture. Overmixing can break down the zucchini too much, making the mixture mushy, so fold carefully.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Mixture is uniform with visible zucchini strands evenly coated
Step 4
If using xanthan gum, sprinkle it evenly over the mixture and fold it in gently. Xanthan gum acts as a binder and thickener, helping the bites hold their shape without adding calories or fat. It mimics the creaminess fat usually provides, preventing watery separation. Don’t skip this if you want firmer bites, especially if your zucchini was very juicy.
⏱ Time: 1 minute
✓ Doneness: Mixture thickens slightly and holds together better when scooped
Step 5
Preheat a non-stick pan over medium heat. Lightly spray with cooking spray to prevent sticking without adding fat. This step ensures your bites cook evenly and develop a slight crust without burning. Avoid high heat as it can cook the outsides too fast and leave the inside uncooked.
⏱ Time: 3 minutes
✓ Doneness: Pan is hot but not smoking, cooking spray is evenly distributed
Step 6
Scoop about 1/4 cup of the mixture per bite and gently press into a small patty shape. Place onto the pan carefully to avoid breaking. Cooking in small, even portions helps the bites cook through quickly and evenly. Don’t overcrowd the pan to allow proper browning.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Bites hold shape and sizzle gently when placed on pan
Step 7
Cook the bites for about 3-4 minutes on each side until golden brown and firm to the touch. Flip carefully using a spatula. The goal is a crispy outer layer that contrasts with the soft, fluffy interior. Cooking too fast or too long will dry them out, so watch closely.
⏱ Time: 8 minutes
✓ Doneness: Golden brown crust forms and bites feel set when pressed
Step 8
Remove the bites from the pan and place on a paper towel-lined plate to absorb any excess moisture. Let them cool slightly; this resting helps the texture set and prevents them from falling apart when eaten. Serving warm enhances flavor and keeps the texture perfect.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Bites are slightly cooled but still warm and firm
Step 9
Serve your zucchini protein bites immediately or store in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days. They’re great on their own or with a low-calorie dip like salsa or mustard. Enjoy a guilt-free, filling snack that packs protein and volume without the calories!
⏱ Time: 0 minutes
✓ Doneness: Bites maintain shape and texture after storage

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Skipping squeezing the zucchini and ending up with watery bites.

Why it happens People often underestimate how much water zucchini holds and assume shredding is enough. This results in soggy texture and poor cooking performance.

Fix After shredding, wrap zucchini in a clean towel or cheesecloth and squeeze firmly to remove as much liquid as possible before mixing. This step is essential for firm bites.

Using full-fat yogurt instead of zero-fat, increasing calories unexpectedly.

Why it happens Some may think any Greek yogurt works, not realizing fat content impacts calorie count significantly.

Fix Always check labels and use 0% fat Greek yogurt to keep the snack low calorie and macros on track.

Adding too much xanthan gum causing gummy or slimy texture.

Why it happens Xanthan gum is potent; overestimating the amount leads to unpleasant mouthfeel.

Fix Measure carefully, using about 1 tsp for this recipe, and mix it in gradually to avoid over-thickening.

Not mixing protein powder well, resulting in chalky lumps.

Why it happens Protein powders can clump if added directly to dry ingredients or mixed too quickly.

Fix Whisk protein powder into wet ingredients like yogurt and egg whites thoroughly before adding solids.

Cooking on too high heat causing burnt outsides and raw centers.

Why it happens Rushing cooking leads to uneven texture and poor bite quality.

Fix Cook on medium heat, allowing bites to brown slowly and cook through evenly.

Pro Tips

Always squeeze the shredded zucchini thoroughly before mixing.
Zucchini contains a lot of water, which can make your snack watery and prevent it from holding shape. Removing excess moisture ensures your bites are firm and have the right texture. If you skip this, the mixture will be too loose and won’t cook properly.
Whisk protein powder into yogurt and egg whites until fully smooth before adding zucchini.
Proper hydration of protein powder prevents clumps and chalky texture. Mixing well ensures a creamy base that blends evenly with vegetables, improving mouthfeel and overall flavor.
Use xanthan gum sparingly to thicken and stabilize the mixture.
Xanthan gum mimics fat’s texture without calories, binding moisture and preventing watery separation. Too much leads to a gummy texture, so precise measurement is key for perfect consistency.
Cook the bites on medium heat with light spray, not high heat.
Medium heat allows even cooking and browning without burning the outside or leaving the inside undercooked. Using a non-stick pan and minimal spray prevents sticking without adding fat.
Let the bites rest a few minutes after cooking before eating.
Resting helps the texture set and moisture redistribute, preventing the bites from falling apart and improving flavor.
Use unflavored whey isolate to keep flavor clean and macros lean.
Flavored powders often contain sweeteners or fillers that add calories and alter taste. Unflavored whey isolate gives you control over seasoning and keeps the snack low calorie.
Store bites in airtight containers in the fridge to maintain texture.
Proper storage prevents moisture loss and helps the bites keep their shape and flavor for up to 3 days, making them great for meal prep.

Variations

Spicy Zucchini Protein Bites

Add 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper and chopped fresh jalapeño to the base mixture for a spicy kick. This variation adds flavor complexity without extra calories. Expect a slight heat that pairs well with a cooling yogurt dip. Protein and calorie content remain similar.

Note: Spicy ingredients won’t affect texture but may increase moisture slightly; ensure zucchini is well drained.

Herbed Zucchini Protein Bites

Mix in 2 tbsp chopped fresh herbs like parsley, dill, or chives for a fresh, aromatic snack. Herbs add flavor and antioxidants with negligible calories. The bites stay light and fluffy, perfect for a savory snack option.

Note: Fresh herbs add moisture; keep zucchini well squeezed to maintain firmness.

Cheesy Zucchini Protein Bites (Low-Fat Cheese)

Fold in 1/4 cup fat-free shredded mozzarella for a cheesy twist. Adds a bit of richness while keeping calories low. Expect slightly creamier texture and more savory flavor, with a small bump in protein.

Note: Fat-free cheese melts well but can release moisture; cook bites thoroughly to avoid sogginess.

Vegan Zucchini Protein Bites

Replace egg whites with aquafaba (chickpea liquid) whipped to stiff peaks and use plant-based protein powder. This vegan version keeps volume and protein high but may be softer in texture. Calorie count remains low with careful ingredient choices.

Note: Aquafaba requires gentle folding to maintain airiness; cooking time may vary slightly.

Sweet Zucchini Protein Bites

Add 1 tsp cinnamon and 1 tbsp erythritol or monk fruit sweetener for a sweet snack version. Use vanilla whey isolate and omit garlic powder. These taste like a light zucchini muffin with protein boost, great for dessert or breakfast.

Note: Sweeteners dissolve best in wet ingredients; avoid overmixing to keep fluffiness.

Storage & Tips

Room Temperature

These zucchini protein bites should not be left at room temperature for more than 2 hours due to the egg whites and yogurt content. Keep them covered to avoid drying out, but refrigeration is best for safety and texture.

Refrigerator

Store bites in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days. They maintain texture well, though chilling firms them up and intensifies flavors. They taste great cold or gently warmed.

Freezer

Freeze bites in a single layer on a baking sheet, then transfer to an airtight container or freezer bag for up to 1 month. Thaw overnight in the fridge. Texture may be slightly less fluffy but still enjoyable.

Reheating

Reheat gently in a microwave for 30-45 seconds or in a warm pan to restore crispness. Avoid overheating, which can cause separation or rubbery texture. Adding a splash of water or almond milk when reheating helps maintain moisture.

Make Ahead

Prepare the mixture 1 day ahead and store covered in the fridge. Cook bites fresh to maintain volume and texture. Alternatively, cook all bites and refrigerate; reheat before eating. This makes for quick grab-and-go snacks during the week.

FAQ

Can I substitute the whey protein isolate with plant-based protein powder?
Yes, you can use plant-based protein powders like pea or rice protein, but texture may be slightly different—often less creamy and a bit grainier. You might need to adjust liquids slightly to maintain consistency. Also, plant proteins sometimes have stronger flavors, so consider adding herbs or spices to balance.
What if my mixture is too watery even after squeezing zucchini?
Try squeezing the zucchini more firmly or use a fine mesh strainer to press out extra moisture. Adding a small amount of xanthan gum helps bind liquids. If still watery, reduce yogurt slightly or add more protein powder to thicken.
Can I use whole eggs instead of egg whites?
You can, but whole eggs add fat and calories, which changes the snack’s low-calorie profile. The texture will be richer and less fluffy. If you’re okay with slightly higher calories, it’s a fine substitution.
How do I avoid the chalky taste from protein powder?
Mix protein powder thoroughly with yogurt and egg whites before adding zucchini to hydrate it well. Using zero-fat Greek yogurt’s acidity helps mask chalkiness. Also, choose a high-quality whey isolate with minimal additives and a mild flavor.
Is xanthan gum necessary?
It’s not mandatory but highly recommended to improve texture and prevent watery separation, especially if your zucchini is very moist. Without it, bites may be softer and less cohesive.
Can I bake these instead of pan-frying?
Yes, bake at 375°F (190°C) for about 15-20 minutes, flipping halfway. Baking reduces added oil use but may produce a softer crust compared to pan-frying.
How many bites should I eat per snack?
One to two bites provide a satisfying snack with about 140-280 calories and 20-40g protein depending on portion size, ideal for most low-calorie meal plans.
Can I add other vegetables?
Yes, finely grated carrots or spinach work well, but adjust moisture content accordingly. Always squeeze extra water out to maintain texture.
