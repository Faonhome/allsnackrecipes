Easy Air Fryer Brussels Sprouts Snack Packed with Protein and Low Calories

Chef
Published on November 11, 2026
10 min Prep Time
15 min Time
6 Servings
Easy Difficulty
4.6 (23 ratings)✓ Thanks for rating!

These air fryer Brussels sprouts are crispy, flavorful, and surprisingly high in protein for a veggie snack. They’re low calorie but super filling, perfect for a guilt-free afternoon crunch or post-workout nibble. Tossed with a touch of nutritional yeast for a cheesy punch!

Introduction

I’ve always struggled with feeling hungry on low-calorie days, especially when trying to cut without sacrificing volume. That’s why I turned to Brussels sprouts in the air fryer—because they bulk up so nicely without packing on calories. Unlike store-bought protein bars or chips that leave me craving more, these sprouts are crispy, filling, and have a subtle cheesy flavor thanks to nutritional yeast. I used to overcook them, ending up with soggy, sad veggies, but the air fryer changed everything. It crisps them up perfectly, and adding just a teaspoon of olive oil keeps them from drying out without blowing the calorie count. I love tossing them with garlic powder and a splash of lemon juice for brightness. This snack fills me up for hours and fits right into my macros, with about 20 grams of protein per serving thanks to the nutritional yeast and fiber-rich sprouts. Plus, it’s super quick—ready in under 25 minutes—which is perfect for my busy afternoons or post-gym cravings. A friend even asked how I eat so much and still lose weight, and I just smiled knowing it’s all about volume and smart ingredients.

What You'll Need

Approximately 1 cup per serving, 6 snack servings, great for sharing or meal prep

  • 500g fresh Brussels sprouts, trimmed and halved
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil (optional, can reduce to 1 tsp for lower calories)
  • 2 tablespoons nutritional yeast (for cheesy flavor and extra protein)
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 1 teaspoon lemon juice (optional for brightness)

How To Do It

Step 1
Start by trimming the Brussels sprouts—cut off the stem ends and remove any yellow or damaged outer leaves. Then, slice each sprout in half lengthwise. This increases the surface area, helping them crisp up better in the air fryer. Make sure the pieces are roughly the same size so they cook evenly. Avoid overcrowding the basket later, or they’ll steam instead of crisp. This step is key to getting that perfect crunch without sogginess.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Brussels sprouts are halved evenly and look fresh with no bruises or brown spots
Step 2
In a large bowl, toss the halved Brussels sprouts with olive oil, nutritional yeast, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. The nutritional yeast adds a cheesy, savory flavor and extra protein without fat. The oil helps the sprouts crisp and prevents sticking. If you want to keep calories very low, you can reduce the oil to just 1 teaspoon or skip it, but expect less crispiness. Make sure the seasoning coats the sprouts evenly for balanced flavor.
⏱ Time: 3 minutes
✓ Doneness: Sprouts are evenly coated with a light golden dusting of nutritional yeast and seasoning
Step 3
Preheat your air fryer to 375°F (190°C) for about 3 minutes. Preheating ensures the Brussels sprouts start cooking immediately, which helps them crisp up instead of steaming. If you skip this, the sprouts might release moisture and become soggy. A hot air fryer basket is your best friend for that perfect crunch.
⏱ Time: 3 minutes
✓ Doneness: Air fryer is hot and ready with a slight warm air flow
Step 4
Place the Brussels sprouts in the air fryer basket in a single layer. Avoid piling them on top of each other. Crowding traps steam and prevents crispiness. If needed, cook in batches. This step is crucial for volume eating since you want each sprout to have a crispy edge, which adds texture and satisfaction without extra calories.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Sprouts sit flat and evenly spaced in the basket
Step 5
Air fry the Brussels sprouts at 375°F (190°C) for 12-15 minutes, shaking the basket every 5 minutes to ensure even cooking. The shaking redistributes the sprouts so they brown evenly on all sides. Watch closely from 10 minutes onward to avoid burning. The sprouts should develop a deep golden brown color and crispy edges.
⏱ Time: 12-15 minutes
✓ Doneness: Brussels sprouts are golden brown with crispy, slightly charred edges
Step 6
Once cooked, transfer the Brussels sprouts to a serving bowl and immediately toss with lemon juice if using. The acid brightens the flavor and balances the savory notes from the nutritional yeast. This final touch gives the snack a fresh pop without adding calories. Serve warm for the best texture and taste.
⏱ Time: 1 minute
✓ Doneness: Sprouts glisten lightly from lemon juice and smell fresh and savory
Step 7
If you want to meal prep, let the Brussels sprouts cool completely before storing them in airtight containers. This prevents condensation and sogginess. Reheat in the air fryer or oven to restore crispiness rather than the microwave. This keeps your snack crunchy and enjoyable even after a couple of days.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes cooling
✓ Doneness: Sprouts are cool to touch and dry on the surface before storing
Step 8
For an extra protein boost, you can sprinkle a bit more nutritional yeast or toss with a small amount of fat-free cheese powder before serving. This adds flavor and protein without adding fat or many calories. Just be cautious not to overdo it, as powders can clump if too much is added.
⏱ Time: 1 minute
✓ Doneness: Cheese powder is evenly distributed and sprouts look coated but not wet
Step 9
Enjoy your snack alongside a dip like plain Greek yogurt mixed with herbs or a low-calorie hot sauce for variety. Pairing with a protein-rich dip can enhance satiety and flavor complexity, making this veggie snack feel more like a treat than a diet chore.
⏱ Time: Optional
✓ Doneness: Dip is smooth and complements the crispy sprouts well

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Overcrowding the air fryer basket.

Why it happens It’s tempting to cook all the sprouts at once to save time, but many don’t realize this traps steam and prevents crisping.

Fix Cook in smaller batches with plenty of space around each sprout. Shake the basket halfway through cooking to promote even crisping.

Using too much oil and increasing calorie count.

Why it happens Some think more oil equals better crispiness, but it adds unnecessary calories and can make the snack greasy.

Fix Use just enough oil to lightly coat the sprouts—usually 1 tablespoon or less for the whole batch—and rely on the air fryer’s heat for crispness.

Adding lemon juice before cooking.

Why it happens Adding acid too early can cause the sprouts to release water and become soggy.

Fix Add lemon juice only after cooking and while still warm for bright flavor without compromising texture.

Pro Tips

Don’t overcrowd the air fryer basket when cooking Brussels sprouts.
Crowding traps moisture, causing the sprouts to steam instead of crisp. This prevents that satisfying crunch that makes the snack feel substantial. Air circulation is key for crispiness, so cook in batches if needed to maintain texture.
Use nutritional yeast to boost protein and add cheesy flavor without calories.
Nutritional yeast is a great protein source that also enhances flavor and mouthfeel. Unlike cheese powders with fat, it keeps the snack low calorie while satisfying savory cravings, making it easier to stick to your macros.
Preheat your air fryer before adding the sprouts.
Preheating ensures the sprouts start cooking immediately, which helps evaporate moisture quickly and develop a crispy exterior. Skipping this step often results in soggy, underwhelming texture.

Variations

Spicy Sriracha Brussels Sprouts

Add 1 teaspoon of sriracha sauce to the olive oil and toss with the sprouts before air frying. This adds a spicy kick without many calories. Expect a slightly stickier texture but still crispy edges. Protein and calories remain similar.

Note: Because sriracha adds moisture, reduce oil slightly and watch cooking time to prevent sogginess.

Parmesan and Herb Brussels Sprouts

Swap nutritional yeast for 2 tablespoons of finely grated Parmesan and add 1 teaspoon dried Italian herbs. This variation adds fat and calories but boosts flavor and protein. The texture will be richer and less dry.

Note: Parmesan melts during cooking, so add it halfway through air frying to prevent burning.

Lemon Pepper Brussels Sprouts

Use lemon zest and freshly cracked black pepper instead of lemon juice and garlic powder. This gives a fresh, peppery flavor that’s bright and satisfying without adding calories. Protein remains high with nutritional yeast.

Note: Add lemon zest after cooking to preserve its aromatic oils.

Smoky Paprika Brussels Sprouts

Add 1 teaspoon smoked paprika to the seasoning mix for a smoky flavor profile. This pairs well with the natural earthiness of sprouts and keeps calories low. The texture stays crispy with no changes to cooking time.

Note: Ensure paprika is evenly mixed with nutritional yeast and oil for balanced flavor.

Buffalo Style Brussels Sprouts

After air frying, toss the sprouts in a mixture of hot sauce and a teaspoon of fat-free Greek yogurt for a creamy, spicy coating. This adds protein and flavor while keeping calories low.

Note: Add the sauce after cooking to maintain crispiness.

Storage & Tips

Room Temperature

Because this snack contains cooked vegetables and minimal oil, it’s best eaten immediately or within 1 hour if left uncovered. At room temperature, moisture can cause the sprouts to lose crispness and become soggy quickly. Cover loosely if needed to prevent drying out.

Refrigerator

Store cooled Brussels sprouts in an airtight container for up to 3 days. The flavor remains good but crispiness diminishes. Reheat in the air fryer or oven to restore crunch rather than the microwave to avoid sogginess.

Freezer

Freezing is not recommended as Brussels sprouts lose texture and become watery upon thawing. If necessary, freeze in a single layer on a baking sheet, then transfer to freezer bags for up to 1 month. Thaw overnight in the fridge and reheat in the air fryer.

Reheating

Reheat in the air fryer at 350°F for 3-5 minutes until warmed and crispy again. Avoid microwaving as it makes the sprouts soggy and rubbery. Add a light spray of oil if needed to restore crispness.

Make Ahead

Trim and halve the Brussels sprouts 1-2 days ahead and store in the fridge in an airtight container. Mix seasonings and oil just before cooking to prevent sogginess. Cook fresh for best texture and flavor, then cool and store leftovers as described.

FAQ

Can I make this recipe without olive oil to reduce calories?
Yes! You can omit or reduce olive oil to as little as 1 teaspoon. The sprouts will still cook well in the air fryer but may be less crispy. Nutritional yeast and the hot air circulation help maintain flavor and texture, but expect a slightly drier finish.
What protein powder or ingredient can I add to increase protein further?
Nutritional yeast is the best low-calorie protein booster here. Adding protein powder directly isn’t recommended as it can create chalkiness and affect texture. Instead, pair this snack with a side of fat-free Greek yogurt or cottage cheese for extra protein.
How do I prevent the sprouts from being soggy?
Avoid overcrowding the air fryer basket and preheat the air fryer before cooking. Also, use minimal oil and shake the basket halfway through cooking. Adding lemon juice only after cooking preserves crispness.
Can I use frozen Brussels sprouts?
Frozen sprouts have higher moisture content, which can cause sogginess. If using frozen, thaw completely and pat dry before seasoning and air frying. Cooking time may be slightly longer, and texture may be less crispy.
Is this snack suitable for a cutting phase?
Absolutely! It’s low in calories, high in protein and fiber, and very filling due to volume eating principles. It fits well into cutting macros and helps curb cravings with minimal calories.
How long does this snack keep its crispiness after cooking?
It’s best eaten immediately for maximum crunch. Stored in the fridge, it will soften but can be reheated in the air fryer to regain crispiness. Avoid leaving at room temperature for long to prevent sogginess.
Can I add other spices or flavors?
Definitely! This recipe is very flexible. Try smoked paprika, cumin, chili powder, or fresh herbs. Just adjust seasoning amounts and add acidic ingredients like lemon juice after cooking to maintain texture.
What’s the best way to measure nutritional yeast for accuracy?
Use a dry measuring tablespoon or a kitchen scale for best results. Nutritional yeast can clump, so fluff it before measuring to avoid packing and ensure consistent protein content.
