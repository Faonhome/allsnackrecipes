Easy 10-Minute Simple Peaches Protein Snack That’s Low Cal and Filling

Published on August 12, 2026
10 min Prep Time
6 Servings
Easy Difficulty

This simple peaches snack is a fresh, juicy way to get a protein boost without loading calories. I love mixing in zero-fat Greek yogurt for creaminess and extra protein. It’s perfect for that afternoon slump or a light post-workout treat!

Introduction

I’ve always struggled with feeling hungry while cutting calories, especially in the afternoons when my energy crashes. Small snacks just didn’t cut it — I needed something that felt like a real snack without blowing my macros. That’s how I landed on this simple peaches recipe. It’s juicy and filling, thanks to the fresh peaches, but what really makes it stand out is the zero-fat Greek yogurt and whey protein powder combo. Together, they create a creamy, satisfying texture that tricks your stomach into thinking you’re eating way more than just 150 calories. I used to make batches that were too watery or chalky from the protein powder, but adding just a touch of lemon juice and cinnamon balances the flavor and texture perfectly. Plus, tossing in a few ice cubes and blending it lightly gives it a fluffiness that feels indulgent but keeps it low-calorie. I love eating this after workouts or when I need a pick-me-up at work. It’s easy to prep on Sundays and stores well in the fridge, so I always have a snack ready that fills me up and keeps me on track. Honestly, my friends ask how I eat so much and still lose weight — this is the secret! If you hate feeling hungry on a diet, give this one a try.

What You'll Need

Approximately 1 large bowl per serving, 6 snack servings, about 1.5 cups volume per serving

  • 2 cups fresh peaches, diced
  • 1 1/2 cups zero-fat Greek yogurt (0% fat)
  • 1 scoop vanilla whey protein isolate powder
  • 1 tsp fresh lemon juice
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 packet stevia or monk fruit sweetener (optional)
  • Ice cubes (optional, for fluffiness)

How To Do It

Step 1
Start by washing and dicing the fresh peaches into bite-sized pieces. Using ripe peaches is key here because they add natural sweetness without extra calories. The juicy texture also helps bulk up the snack, which is great for volume eating. Avoid overripe peaches as they can get mushy and watery, which will affect the final texture. If your peaches are a bit tart, the cinnamon and sweetener will balance them out nicely.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Peaches should be firm but juicy, evenly diced with no mushy bits
Step 2
In a mixing bowl, combine the zero-fat Greek yogurt with the vanilla whey protein isolate powder. Mix vigorously to prevent any chalkiness, which can happen if the powder isn’t fully hydrated. Adding lemon juice here helps break down the protein powder and enhances creaminess. If you want a fluffier texture, toss in a few ice cubes and blend lightly with a hand blender. This step is crucial for achieving a smooth, creamy base that holds well without fat.
⏱ Time: 3 minutes
✓ Doneness: Mixture should be smooth, creamy, and slightly thickened with no visible protein clumps
Step 3
Fold the diced peaches gently into the yogurt-protein mixture. This helps keep the fruit’s texture intact and prevents the snack from becoming too watery. Mixing too aggressively can release excess juice from the peaches, which will thin out the snack and reduce satiety. The goal is a balance of creamy and chunky for satisfying mouthfeel.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Peach pieces are evenly distributed without excess liquid pooling at the bottom
Step 4
Add the ground cinnamon and stevia or monk fruit sweetener if desired. Cinnamon adds warmth and depth without calories, while the sweetener balances the tartness of the peaches and lemon juice. Mix gently to combine. Taste and adjust sweetness as needed, but keep it light to avoid overpowering the natural fruit flavor.
⏱ Time: 1 minute
✓ Doneness: Evenly mixed with a pleasant aroma of cinnamon and balanced sweetness
Step 5
Chill the snack in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes before serving. This resting time allows the protein powder to fully hydrate and the flavors to meld. It also firms up the texture, making it more satisfying and less watery. Skipping this step can result in a runnier texture and less pleasant mouthfeel.
⏱ Time: 30 minutes
✓ Doneness: Snack is firm but creamy, chilled throughout, and flavors taste well combined
Step 6
Serve the snack in large bowls or portable containers if meal prepping. The volume looks generous because the protein and yogurt base is thickened and the peaches add bulk without many calories. This makes it perfect for volume eating when you want to feel full without overeating.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Portions appear large and satisfying, with visible peach chunks and creamy base
Step 7
For meal prep, portion into airtight containers and keep refrigerated. The snack holds well for up to 3 days but is best enjoyed fresh for optimal texture. Avoid freezing as the peaches can become watery and the yogurt texture may change.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Containers sealed and stored in fridge, snack maintains texture and flavor
Step 8
If you want to add extra volume on serving day, sprinkle with a few fresh peach slices or a dash of cinnamon. This adds freshness and a little crunch without extra calories. It also makes the snack look more appealing, which helps with satisfaction.
⏱ Time: 1 minute
✓ Doneness: Fresh peach slices look vibrant and add texture contrast
Step 9
Enjoy your simple peaches protein snack cold. The creamy yogurt and protein powder combined with juicy peaches make it a refreshing, filling treat that’s perfect for any time you need a low-calorie protein boost. Pair with a glass of water or iced herbal tea for a complete snack experience.
⏱ Time: 0 minutes
✓ Doneness: Snack is cool, creamy, and refreshing with balanced sweetness and texture

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Using full-fat yogurt instead of zero-fat, increasing calories unexpectedly.

Why it happens Many assume all Greek yogurts are low calorie, but full-fat versions add unwanted calories and fat, defeating the low-calorie goal.

Fix Always check labels and choose 0% fat Greek yogurt to keep calories low while maximizing protein.

Skipping the chilling step and eating immediately.

Why it happens Impatience or lack of planning leads to eating before protein powder hydrates, causing a runny or chalky texture.

Fix Plan ahead to chill at least 30 minutes so the snack firms up and flavors develop.

Adding too much sweetener causing an artificial aftertaste.

Why it happens Trying to mask tartness with excess stevia or monk fruit leads to bitterness.

Fix Add sweetener sparingly and taste as you go to find the perfect balance.

Using overripe peaches making the snack watery.

Why it happens Overripe fruit releases too much juice, thinning the mixture and reducing satiety.

Fix Choose firm ripe peaches and drain any excess juice if using frozen fruit.

Mixing protein powder directly into peaches causing clumps.

Why it happens Protein powder doesn’t hydrate well in watery fruit juices, leading to chalky lumps.

Fix Always mix protein powder with yogurt first for smooth texture.

Pro Tips

Mix protein powder with yogurt before adding fruit to avoid clumps.
Protein powders can clump when added directly to juicy fruit. Mixing first with thick yogurt hydrates the powder evenly, resulting in a creamy texture without chalky bits. This step is crucial in low-fat recipes where fat can’t mask dryness.
Use ripe but firm peaches to prevent watery snack texture.
Overripe peaches release excess juice, thinning the snack and reducing satiety. Firmer peaches hold their shape and add volume without diluting the creamy base, ensuring a satisfying mouthfeel and fullness.
Chill the snack for at least 30 minutes before eating.
Chilling allows protein powder to fully hydrate and flavors to meld. It firms up the texture, making the snack more pleasant and filling. Eating immediately can result in a runnier, less satisfying snack.
Add a small splash of lemon juice to improve protein powder hydration.
Acidity from lemon juice breaks down protein powder particles, reducing chalkiness and enhancing creaminess. This simple trick improves mouthfeel without adding calories or fat.
Blend in a few ice cubes for extra volume and fluffiness.
Ice adds bulk and a refreshing texture without calories. It also helps create a light, mousse-like consistency that feels indulgent but stays low-calorie.
Measure sweeteners carefully to avoid bitter aftertaste.
Natural sweeteners like stevia can turn bitter if overused. Start with a small amount and adjust to taste to keep the snack flavorful but clean tasting.
Store leftovers in airtight containers to maintain freshness.
Exposure to air can dry out yogurt-based snacks and cause flavor loss. Airtight storage preserves texture and taste for up to 3 days.

Variations

Berry Peach Protein Bowl

Add a mix of fresh berries like blueberries and raspberries to the peaches for extra antioxidants and flavor. This swap adds fiber and antioxidants, with minimal calorie increase but more volume. The texture stays creamy with a fruity burst in every bite.

Note: Because berries add more juice, drain excess liquid before mixing to avoid thinning the snack.

Peach Protein Snack with Egg Whites

Whip pasteurized egg whites and fold into the yogurt-protein mixture for extra fluff and protein without calories. This variation is lighter and airier but still creamy and filling. It boosts protein by 5-7 grams per serving.

Note: Whip egg whites to soft peaks before folding gently to maintain volume and avoid deflating.

Spiced Peach and Ginger Snack

Add freshly grated ginger and a pinch of nutmeg for a warming spice twist. This adds flavor complexity without calories and can help digestion. Expect a slightly zingy, aromatic snack that pairs well with creamy yogurt.

Note: Use fresh ginger sparingly to avoid overpowering the natural peach sweetness.

Peach and Cucumber Protein Salad

Mix diced cucumber with peaches for a refreshing crunch and hydration boost. This combo adds fiber and volume, making the snack even more filling with a crisp texture contrast.

Note: Drain cucumber well to prevent excess moisture and maintain creamy texture.

Vegan Peach Protein Snack with Pea Protein

Swap whey powder for a pea protein isolate and use coconut yogurt (light version) for a vegan-friendly option. The texture is a bit denser and less creamy but still satisfying. Protein content remains high with slightly different flavor notes.

Note: Pea protein hydrates slower; blend well and let chill longer to improve texture.

Storage & Tips

Room Temperature

This snack is best kept cold and should not be left at room temperature for more than 1 hour due to the dairy content. Keep covered to avoid drying out or contamination. Water separation may occur if left warm, so refrigerate promptly.

Refrigerator

Store in airtight containers for up to 3 days. The texture firms up when chilled and flavors meld nicely. The snack holds its volume well but may thicken slightly. It tastes best cold but can be eaten at room temperature shortly after removal from fridge.

Freezer

Freezing is not recommended as the high water content from peaches and yogurt causes icy texture and separation upon thawing. If necessary, freeze only for up to 1 month and thaw slowly in fridge.

Reheating

This snack is intended to be eaten cold. Reheating can cause separation of yogurt and protein, resulting in an unpleasant texture. If you prefer it warmer, let it sit at room temperature for 10 minutes but avoid microwaving.

Make Ahead

Prepare the yogurt-protein base and dice peaches up to 2 days in advance and store separately. Combine just before eating to maintain peach texture and prevent watery mixtures. This keeps the snack fresh and voluminous for meal prep.

FAQ

Can I substitute whey protein isolate with plant-based protein?
Yes, you can use plant-based protein powders like pea or rice protein, but expect a slightly different texture and flavor. Plant proteins often hydrate slower and can be grainier, so blend well and chill longer to improve creaminess.
How do I avoid the protein powder tasting chalky in this snack?
Mix protein powder thoroughly with Greek yogurt before adding peaches. Adding a bit of lemon juice helps break down powder particles, improving creaminess. Also, chilling the snack allows protein to hydrate fully, reducing chalkiness.
Is this snack suitable for meal prep?
Absolutely! Prepare the base and peaches separately and combine just before eating for best texture. Stored properly in airtight containers, it keeps well in the fridge for up to 3 days without losing flavor or volume.
Can I use canned peaches instead of fresh?
Canned peaches often contain added sugars and syrup, increasing calories. If you use canned, choose those packed in water or juice, drain well, and adjust sweetener accordingly. Fresh peaches are preferred for natural sweetness and texture.
How many calories and protein does one serving have?
Each serving has approximately 140 calories and 20 grams of protein, making it a perfect low-calorie, high-protein snack to support satiety and muscle maintenance.
Can I add nuts or seeds for extra texture?
While nuts add healthy fats and crunch, they also increase calories significantly. If you want to keep it low-calorie, sprinkle a small amount (like 1 tsp) of chopped nuts. Otherwise, enjoy the snack as is for volume eating.
What if my snack turns out watery?
Watery texture usually means too much juice from peaches or not enough protein powder/yogurt to bind. Use firmer peaches, drain excess juice, and ensure proper mixing and chilling to prevent this.
Is this snack good after workouts?
Yes! It provides a quick, easily digestible source of protein and natural carbs from fruit, ideal for muscle recovery and replenishing energy without excess calories.
