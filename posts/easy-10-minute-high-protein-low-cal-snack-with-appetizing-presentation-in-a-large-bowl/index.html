Easy 10-Minute High-Protein Low-Cal Snack with Appetizing Presentation in a Large Bowl

Cynthia Sullivan
Published on June 4, 2026
10 min Prep Time
6 Servings
Easy Difficulty

This snack is a creamy, protein-packed delight served in a big bowl that looks as good as it tastes. Using zero-fat Greek yogurt and egg whites, it’s low calorie but fills you up nicely. Perfect for an afternoon pick-me-up or post-workout refuel!

Introduction

I’ve always struggled with feeling hungry when I’m cutting calories, especially on days when I only have around 1200 to work with. Tiny portions just don’t cut it for me—I need something that feels like a real snack, not just a nibble. That’s why I came up with this high-protein, low-calorie snack served in a large bowl. It’s huge, creamy, and satisfying without packing on the calories. Plus, it’s made with simple ingredients—no weird fillers or artificial stuff. The trick? Whipping liquid egg whites into the yogurt with xanthan gum to create a fluffy, mousse-like texture that feels indulgent but isn’t. I also add a splash of sugar-free vanilla syrup and fresh berries for natural sweetness and color. It’s become my go-to afternoon pick-me-up or post-workout snack. The first time I tried it, I accidentally added too much xanthan gum and ended up with a gel—lesson learned! Now I get the perfect creamy fluff every time. Honestly, my friends always ask how I can eat so much and still lose weight. It’s all about volume and protein. You’ll love how filling it is without feeling heavy.

What You'll Need

Approximately 1 large bowl per serving, 6 servings total, about 2 cups volume each

  • 2 cups zero-fat Greek yogurt, chilled
  • 1 cup liquid egg whites, pasteurized
  • 1 scoop unflavored whey protein isolate powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon xanthan gum
  • 1 cup mixed fresh berries (blueberries, raspberries)
  • 1 tablespoon sugar-free vanilla syrup
  • 1 teaspoon lemon zest
  • A pinch of salt

How To Do It

Step 1
Start by chilling your zero-fat Greek yogurt and liquid egg whites in the fridge for at least 30 minutes before mixing. Cold ingredients help the mixture whip up better and hold volume longer. Pour the yogurt into a large mixing bowl and slowly whisk in the protein powder to avoid clumps. The protein powder hydrates better in cold yogurt, which prevents that chalky texture you sometimes get. Avoid rushing this step—take your time to get a smooth base. If you mix too fast or add powder all at once, you’ll get lumps and a gritty mouthfeel.
⏱ Time: 10 minutes
✓ Doneness: Smooth, thick, and creamy mixture with no visible lumps or powder clumps
Step 2
Next, use a hand mixer to whip the chilled liquid egg whites until they form soft peaks—this usually takes about 3-4 minutes. Whipping egg whites adds volume and fluffiness without fat, which is key for a satisfying texture in low-calorie snacks. Be careful not to overbeat; stiff peaks will make the mixture dry and less creamy. Gently fold the whipped egg whites into the yogurt mixture using a spatula, preserving as much air as possible. This folding technique keeps the snack light and airy, mimicking a mousse-like texture.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Mixture looks light, airy, and slightly glossy without deflating
Step 3
Sprinkle in xanthan gum gradually while folding to thicken the mixture without adding calories. Xanthan gum acts as a natural thickener and stabilizer, preventing watery separation common in zero-fat dairy snacks. Add just 1/2 teaspoon; too much will cause a gummy texture. Mix gently and evenly to distribute it well. This step is crucial to get that creamy mouthfeel without fat, so don’t skip it or eyeball the amount.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Mixture thickens slightly but remains smooth and scoopable
Step 4
Add the sugar-free vanilla syrup and lemon zest to brighten the flavor. These ingredients add complexity and sweetness without calories, making the snack taste indulgent. Stir gently to combine, making sure the syrup is evenly dispersed. This step helps mask any protein powder aftertaste and keeps the snack refreshing.
⏱ Time: 1 minute
✓ Doneness: Evenly mixed with a pleasant vanilla aroma and a hint of citrus
Step 5
Fold in fresh mixed berries carefully to add natural sweetness, color, and a bit of crunch. The berries add volume and fiber, which help you feel full longer. Use about 1 cup total, and fold gently so they don’t crush and release juice, which could thin the mixture.
⏱ Time: 1 minute
✓ Doneness: Berries are evenly distributed without breaking down or bleeding juice
Step 6
Transfer the mixture into large bowls or meal prep containers. The generous portion size tricks your brain into feeling satisfied because you’re eating a lot of food, even though it’s low calorie. The volume eating effect is a game changer for hunger control during dieting or cutting phases.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Bowls filled with a fluffy, colorful snack that holds shape
Step 7
Chill the snack in the fridge for at least 30 minutes before serving. Chilling helps firm up the texture since there’s no fat to provide structure. It also melds the flavors together nicely. Don’t skip this step or the snack will feel loose and less satisfying.
⏱ Time: 30 minutes
✓ Doneness: Snack firms up slightly but stays creamy and fluffy
Step 8
Before serving, give the snack a gentle stir to fluff it up again if it has settled. This step refreshes the texture and makes it look appetizing in a large bowl or plate. It also helps distribute any juices from the berries evenly.
⏱ Time: 1 minute
✓ Doneness: Snack looks light, airy, and inviting
Step 9
Enjoy immediately or store leftovers in airtight containers. If storing, keep chilled and consume within 2 days for best texture and flavor. The high protein and volume keep you full and satisfied without the calorie overload.
⏱ Time: 0 minutes
✓ Doneness: Snack maintains texture and flavor after storage

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Adding too much xanthan gum causing gummy texture.

Why it happens People often think more thickener equals better texture, but xanthan gum is potent and needs careful measurement.

Fix Use a precise measuring tool and add only 1/2 teaspoon gradually. Mix thoroughly and test texture before adding more.

Not chilling ingredients leading to watery, loose snack.

Why it happens Warm dairy and egg whites don’t whip well and xanthan gum doesn’t thicken effectively at higher temps.

Fix Always chill yogurt and egg whites for at least 30 minutes before mixing to ensure volume and proper thickening.

Mixing protein powder too quickly causing lumps and chalkiness.

Why it happens Rushing causes powder to clump and hydrate unevenly, leaving gritty bits.

Fix Add powder slowly while whisking continuously for a smooth, lump-free base.

Overwhipping egg whites until stiff peaks form.

Why it happens Stiff peaks make the mixture dry and less creamy, reducing palatability.

Fix Whip to soft peaks only and fold gently to maintain fluffiness and moisture.

Using full-fat yogurt unknowingly increasing calories.

Why it happens Some yogurts labeled Greek may still have 2% or more fat, adding unexpected calories.

Fix Check labels carefully for zero-fat or nonfat Greek yogurt to keep the snack low calorie.

Pro Tips

Always chill your ingredients before mixing.
Cold yogurt and egg whites whip better and hold volume longer, which is essential for achieving the fluffy, mousse-like texture without fat. Warm ingredients can cause the mixture to be runny and reduce the effectiveness of xanthan gum as a thickener.
Add protein powder slowly and whisk well to prevent lumps.
Protein powder can clump easily in dairy, especially when cold. Gradual incorporation ensures smooth hydration and prevents chalky texture, making the snack more enjoyable and easier to eat.
Use a gentle folding technique when combining whipped egg whites.
Folding preserves the air bubbles in the egg whites, which add volume and lightness. Stirring aggressively will deflate the mixture, resulting in a denser snack that feels heavier and less satisfying.
Measure xanthan gum precisely with a microplane or scale.
Too much xanthan gum causes a gummy, unpleasant texture, while too little won’t prevent watery separation. Accurate measurement ensures the perfect creamy consistency without adding calories.
Add fresh berries last to maintain their shape and texture.
Folding berries in gently prevents them from crushing and releasing juice, which would thin the mixture and reduce volume. The berries add natural sweetness and fiber, enhancing satiety.
Chill the snack for at least 30 minutes before serving.
Chilling firms up the texture in the absence of fat, making the snack creamier and more satisfying. It also helps flavors meld and improves mouthfeel.
Use a high-quality unflavored whey isolate for best results.
Unflavored isolates avoid artificial tastes and blend seamlessly with yogurt and egg whites. They provide a clean protein boost without adding calories or unwanted flavors.

Variations

Berry Citrus Protein Bowl

Swap the mixed berries for a combination of chopped strawberries and a splash of fresh orange juice. This adds a bright citrus twist and a bit more natural sweetness without extra calories. The texture stays light and fluffy, and the macros remain similar with about 140 calories and 20g protein.

Note: Because orange juice adds liquid, reduce yogurt by 2 tablespoons to maintain thickness.

Chocolate Mocha Protein Snack

Add 1 tablespoon unsweetened cocoa powder and a teaspoon of instant coffee granules to the yogurt mixture for a mocha flavor. Use a chocolate-flavored whey isolate if you like. This variation is rich but still low calorie and high protein, perfect for chocolate lovers watching their macros.

Note: Mix cocoa and coffee with protein powder before adding to yogurt to ensure even flavor distribution.

Tropical Protein Bowl

Replace berries with diced pineapple and mango, and add a teaspoon of lime zest. This tropical version is refreshing and sweet with the same creamy texture. The natural sugars in fruit add flavor without needing sweetener, keeping calories low.

Note: Use firm, fresh fruit to avoid excess juice that might thin the mixture.

Vanilla Cinnamon Protein Delight

Add 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon and a splash of vanilla extract to the base mix. This warm flavor combo pairs well with a few sliced apple pieces folded in. It’s cozy, filling, and keeps macros tight with around 150 calories and 21g protein.

Note: Cinnamon can thicken the mixture slightly; if needed, add a teaspoon more yogurt to maintain creaminess.

Pumpkin Spice Protein Bowl

Mix in 1/4 cup pure pumpkin puree and pumpkin spice blend for a seasonal twist. This adds fiber and moisture, making the snack even more filling. It’s a great way to enjoy fall flavors while keeping the snack low calorie and protein-rich.

Note: Pumpkin adds moisture; reduce yogurt slightly to keep the right consistency.

Storage & Tips

Room Temperature

This snack should not be left at room temperature for more than 1 hour due to the egg whites and dairy content. Keep covered if you need to set it out briefly, but best served chilled to maintain texture and safety.

Refrigerator

Store in airtight containers for up to 2 days. The flavor may intensify and texture firms up when chilled, which many find preferable. Give it a gentle stir before serving to refresh the fluffiness.

Freezer

Freezing is not recommended as the high water content and egg whites can cause icy crystals and loss of texture. If you must freeze, use airtight containers and thaw slowly in the fridge, stirring gently before eating.

Reheating

This snack is best enjoyed cold or at room temperature. Reheating can cause separation and loss of fluffiness, so avoid warming it up. If it feels too thick after chilling, stir in a splash of unsweetened almond milk to loosen.

Make Ahead

Prepare the yogurt-protein base and whip egg whites separately up to 1 day ahead. Store both components chilled and combine just before serving to maintain maximum volume and freshness. Add fresh berries last to prevent sogginess.

FAQ

Can I substitute the whey protein powder with plant-based protein?
Yes, you can use a plant-based isolate like pea or rice protein, but expect slight changes in texture and flavor. Plant proteins may not hydrate as smoothly and can be a bit grittier. To improve texture, blend protein powder with yogurt first and consider adding a bit more xanthan gum.
What’s the best way to track calories for this snack?
Measure each ingredient precisely using a kitchen scale and input exact brands into your tracking app. Protein powders vary widely, so check the label carefully. This ensures accurate macro tracking and helps stay within your calorie goals.
