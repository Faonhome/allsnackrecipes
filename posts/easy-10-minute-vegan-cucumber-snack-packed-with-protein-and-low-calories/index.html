Easy 10-Minute Vegan Cucumber Snack Packed with Protein and Low Calories

Chef
Published on July 7, 2026
10 min Prep Time
6 Servings
Easy Difficulty

This crunchy vegan cucumber snack is a refreshing way to get protein without the calories. Using silken tofu blended with fresh herbs gives it creamy texture and boosts protein. Perfect for a light afternoon bite or post-workout refuel!

Introduction

I’ve always struggled with feeling hungry on low-calorie diets, especially when cutting. Tiny snacks just don’t cut it for me, so I needed something that felt like a real snack without blowing my macros. That’s how I started experimenting with vegan cucumber recipes that pack protein but stay light. This version is my favorite because it’s crunchy, creamy, and fills me up without any weird ingredients or high calories. I use silken tofu as the protein base — it’s smooth and blends perfectly with fresh herbs and lemon juice. To get the right thick texture without fat, I add a pinch of xanthan gum, which helps the dip hold together and cling to the cucumber slices. I also learned that chilling the cucumbers and dip separately keeps everything crisp and refreshing. This snack is perfect for afternoon cravings or post-workout when I want something satisfying but still under 150 calories. Plus, it’s huge — you get a big bowl of food that feels way more than just a nibble. My friends always ask how I eat so much and still lose weight, and this is my little secret. I hope you enjoy it as much as I do!

What You'll Need

Approximately 2 cups dip with 2 cups cucumber slices per serving, 6 snack servings total

  • 2 cups cucumber, sliced thin and chilled
  • 1 cup silken tofu, drained
  • 1 tablespoon fresh dill, chopped
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon xanthan gum (optional, for thickening)
  • Stevia or monk fruit sweetener, to taste (optional)

How To Do It

Step 1
Start by slicing your cucumbers thinly and chilling them in the fridge. Thin slices maximize the crunchy volume without adding calories, and chilling keeps them crisp. Avoid thick slices which can feel heavy and less satisfying. The cold temperature also enhances the refreshing quality of the snack, making it perfect for warm days or after workouts.
⏱ Time: 10 minutes
✓ Doneness: Cucumbers should be firm, cool, and crisp to the bite without any sogginess.
Step 2
In a blender, combine silken tofu, lemon juice, garlic powder, salt, pepper, and fresh dill. Blend until smooth and creamy. Silken tofu blends easily and creates a luscious base with high protein content. Make sure the tofu is well drained to avoid watery dip. If you want a thicker texture, add xanthan gum gradually while blending to prevent clumping.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Dip should be creamy, smooth, and thick enough to coat the back of a spoon without dripping.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Skipping draining tofu leading to watery dip.

Why it happens Many assume silken tofu can be used straight from the package, but the excess liquid dilutes the dip.

Fix Place tofu in a fine mesh strainer or wrap in paper towels and gently press to remove moisture before blending.

Adding too much xanthan gum causing slimy texture.

Why it happens Xanthan gum is powerful; overuse results from not measuring carefully.

Fix Start with 1/4 teaspoon, blend well, then add more only if needed to thicken.

Using thick cucumber slices that feel heavy.

Why it happens Thick slices reduce volume and crunch, making snack less satisfying.

Fix Slice cucumbers thinly for maximum volume and crispness.

Pro Tips

Drain silken tofu well before blending to avoid a watery dip.
Excess water in tofu dilutes the dip, making it runny and less satisfying. Proper draining ensures a creamy, thick texture that clings well to cucumber slices and enhances satiety.
Add xanthan gum slowly and blend thoroughly to prevent clumps.
Xanthan gum thickens dips effectively but can form lumps if added too quickly. Gradual addition ensures even distribution and a smooth, creamy texture without sliminess.
Chill cucumbers and dip separately to maintain crispness.
If cucumbers sit in dip too long, they release water and become soggy. Keeping them chilled and separate preserves crunch and freshness, making the snack more enjoyable.

Variations

Spicy Vegan Cucumber Snack

Add a pinch of cayenne pepper or smoked paprika to the tofu dip for a spicy kick. This variation keeps calories low but adds bold flavor and a slight metabolism boost. Expect a creamy dip with a warming aftertaste that pairs well with cooling cucumber slices.

Note: Spices won’t affect texture but add flavor complexity. Adjust spice level to taste.

Herby Vegan Cucumber Snack with Basil and Mint

Swap dill for fresh basil and mint for a bright, garden-fresh flavor. This combo is refreshing and perfect for summer snacking. Macros stay the same, but the flavor profile shifts to a sweeter, aromatic note.

Note: Use equal amounts of fresh herbs. Blending herbs finely helps distribute flavor evenly.

Lemon-Ginger Vegan Cucumber Snack

Add freshly grated ginger and extra lemon zest for a zesty, slightly spicy dip. This variation adds antioxidants and digestive benefits while maintaining low calories and high protein. The dip will have a zingy, refreshing taste.

Note: Grate ginger finely to avoid fibrous texture. Adjust lemon juice to balance tartness.

Storage & Tips

Room Temperature

This snack should not be left at room temperature for more than 1 hour due to the tofu dip. Keep covered and chilled until ready to serve. Leaving it out causes water separation and loss of freshness.

Refrigerator

Store the dip and sliced cucumbers separately in airtight containers for up to 3 days. The dip firms up slightly when chilled but remains creamy. Cucumbers stay crisp longer when kept separate and chilled.

Freezer

Freezing is not recommended as tofu texture degrades and cucumbers become watery and mushy upon thawing.

Reheating

This snack is best served cold or at room temperature. Reheating is not advised as it alters tofu texture and reduces freshness.

Make Ahead

Prepare the tofu dip up to 2 days ahead and store in an airtight container. Slice cucumbers fresh on the day of serving to maintain maximum crunch. Assemble just before eating for best texture.

FAQ

Can I substitute silken tofu with another protein source?
Silken tofu works best for creamy texture and high protein with low calories. You can try blended white beans or soft chickpeas but expect a different flavor and texture. For vegan protein, tofu remains the most neutral and smooth option.
How do I avoid the dip tasting chalky from protein?
The lemon juice acidity helps mask chalkiness. Also, blending thoroughly and adding fresh herbs improves flavor. Using a good quality silken tofu with mild taste reduces chalkiness. Xanthan gum adds creaminess that balances texture.
Is xanthan gum necessary?
Not strictly, but it helps thicken the dip without adding fat or calories. Without it, the dip may be thinner and less clingy, which can reduce satisfaction. You can omit it if you prefer a looser dip.
Can I make this snack gluten-free?
Yes, all ingredients are naturally gluten-free. Just ensure your xanthan gum brand is certified gluten-free if you choose to use it.
How long does this snack keep its volume and crunch?
Cucumbers stay crunchy best when sliced fresh. The dip keeps well chilled for up to 3 days but may thin slightly. Assembling just before eating preserves maximum volume and texture.
Can I add other vegetables?
Yes, bell pepper strips or celery sticks work well for volume and crunch. Just adjust portion sizes to keep calories low.
Is this snack suitable post-workout?
Absolutely! It provides a good protein hit with refreshing hydration from cucumbers, helping muscle recovery without excess calories.
How do I count calories accurately for this recipe?
Use the nutrition label on your tofu and measure cucumbers by weight or volume. Stevia and xanthan gum add negligible calories. Tracking apps like MyFitnessPal can help you log ingredients precisely.
